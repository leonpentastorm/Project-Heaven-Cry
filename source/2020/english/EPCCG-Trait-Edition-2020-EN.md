# EPCCG: Trait Edition (2020, English edition)

> Converted from the "Trait Edition" tab of the living Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/DupaUzweyVDdbcx4mJGoqs on 2026-11-05. **Historical.** Superseded by EPCCG: Trait Edition, Revision 2 (74 traits, `data/traits.json`), which rewrote every trait in-universe and removed the pop-culture references kept below.

A trait is something only your character has, like a character who can't live without their glasses, or a robot that can't love. In Project Heaven Cry a trait is a modifier on your Skills or combat Attributes. Think of a buff and a debuff mixed together and glued onto your character with industrial epoxy.

**Trait rules**

- Every trait has at least one upside and at least one downside.
- Trait modifiers **can't be used to unlock perks**, and nothing in the game can remove them.
- Some traits also unlock new dialogue, or grant items and classes unique to that trait.
- Each player picks **at most 2** traits.

## Combat trades

#### Mind Without Fog

*You were raised by priests, monks, preachers or whatever. What do you call holy people in your world again? Whatever faith you follow now, your mind is the same: so clear and still that nobody can change it.*

- **+ Zen:** gain 4 Core points and 10 Skill points immediately.
- **− Unmoving:** equipment bonuses to combat Attributes and Skills no longer work on you.

#### The Brightest Light

*Destroy. Exterminate. Wipe them out.*

- **+ AHAHAHAHAHAH:** +20% to all damage, +50% Critical Damage.
- **− Burns Out the Fastest:** weapons that need maintenance wear out twice as fast in your hands.

#### I Know Kung Fu

*Talk to the hand.*

- **+ THESE HANDS:** fighting with nothing but gloves, gain +1 AP and +20% Physical Damage.
- **− I Said THESE, Not THIS:** with any melee weapon other than gloves, −2 AP and −10% Physical Damage. Ranged weapons get no bonus, but no penalty either.

#### Kamikaze

*Tennōheika banzai!!!*

- **+ The Emperor's Calling:** gain 3 AP.
- **− Final Destination:** you can't wear armour above Armour Class I.

#### Mister YEET Man

*Throws hard. Very hard.*

- **+ Ya YEETT:** +1 unit of throwing range.
- **− My Hand Can't Take Much More:** every thrown weapon costs 1 AP more.

#### Glass Runner

*They said you had brittle bone disease. You ran up and gave them a brittle knee to the face.*

- **+ Runner:** your bones break easily, but that doesn't mean you can't use them. +5 Initiative.
- **− Glass:** +50% limb damage taken.

#### Blademaster of the Basement

*While they partied, you studied the blade. While they dated, you mastered the blockchain. While they chased pocket change, you cultivated inner strength. Now the world is ending, the demons are at the gate, and they come to you for help? Since when?*

- **+ Blade Master:** your weapon mastery levels up 25% faster.
- **− What a Fucking Weeb LOL:** −1 Charisma, Implication and Authority.

#### 50 Shades of Darkness

*The old world had a word for people like you. Every time you think about it, your whole body tingles strangely.*

- **+ Sadism:** +50% damage with every weapon.
- **− Masochism:** +50% damage taken from everything.

## Music traits

All three work the same way. You start with a Musek Blestor, pick a song on YouTube and write it in your Bio's Favourite Song field. When you kill, revive an ally, land a big heal or shield, or whenever the mood is right, your song replaces the area or combat music.

#### The Soundscape of the Soul — Musek Blestor (S)

*Music. Gentle as a ripple, violent as a July flood, lyrical enough to drown your whole soul in.*

- **+ Vibing:** while your song plays, +2 to every Soul skill.
- **− Where Me Vibe Go:** if you haven't heard your song in 4 loading screens (4 area changes), −1 to every Mind and Body skill.

#### Nevada Head Banger — Musek Blestor (B)

*The bass drops harder than that one big bomb on that one big city.*

- **+ Absolute Banger:** while your song plays, +2 to every Body skill. The bonus fades after 2 loading screens without it.
- **− Losing My Mind:** after 4 loading screens without your song, −1 to every Mind and Soul skill.

#### Indie Indeed — Musek Blestor (M)

*Music while sleeping, cooking, driving, even while listening to music. And I'll have you know this band is CRIMINALLY underrated!*

- **+ Brain Dancing:** while your song plays, +2 to every Mind skill. The bonus fades after 2 loading screens without it.
- **− Dancingn't:** after 4 loading screens without your song, −1 to every Body and Soul skill.

## Translator's notes

**Skill names.** The Trait Edition still uses some early, Disco-era skill names. They are mapped to the main guide's names here:

| In the original | This edition |
|---|---|
| Encyclopedia | Rumination |
| Hand/Eye Coordination, H/E Coordination | Dexterity |
| Sensitivity | Sensory |
| Endurance (as a skill) | Durability |

**Typos fixed.** The downsides of Symbol of Order, Agent of Chaos and Thousand Faces were marked "+" in the original; they are downsides and are marked − here.

**One trait left out.** The original list has 28 traits; this edition has 27. One trait's name is a spoonerism of a racial slur, and its mechanic is a penalty for talking to people of colour. It isn't translated. If the group wants a bigoted-character trait, it can work against in-world groups instead (robots, Crawlers, other factions), which fits the robot-equality story in MPAT's backstory.
