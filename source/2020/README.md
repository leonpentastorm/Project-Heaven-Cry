# 2020 source material

Original Project Heaven Cry material, kept for reference. Rules here are **superseded** by `docs/rules/PHC-Core-Rules-v1.0.md`.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Official_EPCCG_Trait_Edition_2.docx` | The 2020 trait book (Vietnamese). Rebalanced and rewritten in-universe as EPCCG: Trait Edition, Revision 2 (74 traits) → `data/traits.json`. |
| `english/EPCCG-Player-Character-Creation-Guide-EN.txt` | English edition (3 Nov 2026 translation) of `Official_EPCCG.docx`, the Starter Edition character creation guide: the pitch, the factions, all 27 skills with their in-world field quotes, MPAT's sample sheet and the translator's notes. **The main lore source for the world's people and factions.** Its 27 quoted speakers are painted in `art/character-portraits/first-patch-revival/`; index in `docs/lore/EPCCG-Cast-and-Factions.md`. Rules in it are superseded by the spec. |
| `english/EPCCG-Trait-Edition-2020-EN.txt` | English edition of the 2020 trait book (27 of its 28 traits; one bigoted trait deliberately not translated). Superseded by Trait Edition Revision 2 (74 traits, `data/traits.json`), which removed its pop-culture references. Kept as history. |
| `screenshots/recovered-*.jpg` | Phone screenshots of recovered 2020 perk and magic notes. Their content is transcribed in `docs/design/Recovered-Design-Notes-2020.md`. |

Not in this repo (they live in the claude.ai Project "Project Heaven Cry"; add them here if you want them versioned):

- `Official_EPCCG.docx`: the Vietnamese original of the Starter Edition guide. Its English edition is now in `english/` (above).
- `PHC_Official_Character_Sheet.xlsx`: the blank character sheet (Combat Sheet + Character Bio). Its fields are reproduced in the story-mode character panel tabs.
- `M1.4_8R4-D83-RRY_A5.xlsx`: MPAT's sheet. Summarised in English in `docs/lore/MPAT-and-M813.md`.
