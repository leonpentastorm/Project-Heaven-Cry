## 01 · Mind Without Fog · Social, Karma

*You were raised in a Lumeri cloister, or by priests of some other faith. Whatever you believe now, your mind is still water. Nobody has ever managed to stir it.*

- **+ Still Water:** gain 2 Core points at creation. Heart Debuffs never affect you.
- **− Unmoving:** gear bonuses to Attributes and Skills don't work on you, and your Karma can never change.
- **Narrator:** every faction wants to convert the one recruit who can't be converted.
