## Design link: MPAT and Wick

Wick is the EPCCG's cartoon of a 258th Medical Unit field automaton (`docs/lore/Wick.md`). MPAT *is* one. They should visibly share a design language: ceramic shell, screen face, a triage lamp in the chest with the 258th's medical cross, and a short field cloak. MPAT is the adult-proportioned, worn, real version. Wick is the cheerful print. A player who meets MPAT after reading the EPCCG should recognise the model and feel the gap.

## 1. MPAT: character portrait

- **Collection:** new versioned folder `art/character-portraits/m813/`, catalog ID `mpat`.
- **Subject:** M1.4 8R4-D83-RRY, "MPAT". Robot combat medic of the 258th Medical Unit, built by the Factory. 7 years old, 180 cm, 73 kg, right-handed. Lean and fast (high Agility, a dodge-and-rescue medic), not a tank.
- **Construction (proposal):** 258th field automaton. Ivory ceramic shell, chipped and field-patched with mismatched plates from other units. Charcoal screen face with sparse square pixel features. Triage lamp in the chest behind a medical-cross grille. Short, scorched field cloak. Surgical manipulators on the right hand.
- **Action:** kneeling, with the still-running robot head of its last comrade strapped to its chest harness. MPAT's left hand rests on the head; its right hand holds a ration tin it is cooking over a small field stove. (It's a good cook, and it never leaves a teammate behind.)
- **Emotion:** steady attention. It is listening to the head talk. No smile; MPAT has no sense of humour.
- **Setting:** a dim shelter on M813, salvaged parts stacked behind.
- **Light:** the triage lamp (cool white) and the stove (warm orange). Locally coloured; no attribute tint.
- **Format:** 4:5, 800 × 1000 PNG.

## 2. The robot head

- **Catalog ID:** `mpat-comrade`. Close-up, 4:5.
- **Subject:** a 093rd infantry robot reduced to its head, wired to a scavenged battery pack, still running. Its name was never recorded; leave its screen blank or showing a single line of light. *Proposal:* a heavier military head than MPAT's, with a different manufacturer's stencil.
- **Action:** propped on a workbench beside half a body assembled from other robots' parts.
- **Emotion:** the conversation about which of them gets rebuilt. Undecided.

## 3. Outpost-813: world illustration

- **Use:** opening chapter image for the one-shot (story-mode lore picture), 16:9.
- **Show:** the rear landing pad on M813 after the last ship left. Scorch ring where the engineers' ship lifted off. Twenty-three badly damaged infantry robots sitting or lying where they stopped, with no spare parts. Four medic robots, MPAT among them, working on them. The black hole **B813** fills the upper sky: a dark disc with a thin bright accretion ring, bending the starlight around it.
- **Supporting detail:** a sealed hatch scorched by grenades; abandoned platinum crates with one split open; a Thousand-Planet City Army regimental mark (093) on a broken sign.
- **Light:** the accretion ring's cold glow from above; warm sparks from a welding torch below.

## 4. The repair bay at Outpost-1: final image

- **Use:** the one-shot's last screen. It shows the ending, so keep it out of anything players see before the end.
- **Show:** inside a repair bay, MPAT finishing its comrade's body, closing the last panel. The bay doors are sealing behind them; through the closing gap, the black hole near Outpost-1 (the log doesn't say whether it is B813). MPAT faces its comrade, not the doors.
- **Emotion:** finished. Not afraid.
- **Light:** the bay's work lamps going to red; the comrade's screen lighting up for the first time.
