# GPT prompts, 19 Nov 2026, file 10: the gun counterparts (17 repaints, 15 new forms)

For the artist, and for GPT. 32 gear paintings: 17 repaints of guns that exist (same id, same file name, a new picture) and
15 new guns in five maker lines. Every gun here is the in-world counterpart of a well-known firearm pattern.

## How to read this file

1. **These guns must read as their pattern at a glance.** A gun enthusiast who sees the icon at 64 px in the shop grid
   should know the pattern instantly. Keep the pattern's layout and proportions, its magazine's shape and curve, its gas
   system and sight layout, its stock and furniture style and its receiver's character. Do not invent a new silhouette.
2. **The name, the maker and the markings are ours.** No real logo, rollmark, maker mark, text, letter, number or serial
   appears on any gun. Each prompt describes its pattern by its features only; draw what the features say.
3. **One small maker's touch** is named at the end of each prompt (a band, a tag, a rivet, a medallion, a screw). It is
   the only departure from the pattern and it never changes the silhouette.
4. **One image a prompt.** Paste the style block below first, then the entry's prompt, and attach every file the entry
   names (all are in the repo). Save under the exact file name and folder given; do not rename.
5. **Order:** section 1 (the repaints) first, because their grade pictures are made from the new picture. In section 2,
   paint each line's base first (marked "the line's base"); once it is saved, attach it too when you paint the other two
   forms of its line, so the three read as one maker's work.
6. **Grades.** Every id in this file is a graded item with five grades. The picture asked for here is the Standard, the
   master. The four higher grades of each id (`<stem>--fine.png`, `<stem>--masterwork.png`, `<stem>--relic.png`,
   `<stem>--celestial.png`, same folder) are made from it by the grade method of
   [`08-grades-and-uniques.md`](08-grades-and-uniques.md), section A: guns use the Guns block (A3.3); the repainted
   `smetanita_thumper`, `breacher_tube` and `blackpoint_700` use the Heavy weapons block (A3.8). For a repainted id the
   grades are made from the new picture, never the old one.

Every picture shows in the shop grid (64 px), the backpack row (32 px), the loot card and the gear card.
