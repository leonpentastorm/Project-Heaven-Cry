# Archetype portraits: 46 player cards

**From Leon, 6 November 2026. Start now.** Paint one portrait for each of the 46 archetypes in `data/archetypes.json`. They become:

- the art next to each archetype on the creation screen;
- portraits players can pick for their own character. In the game these are **roleplay cards**: the picker shows the painting alone, with no name and no set label, so the player sees *themselves*, not someone else;
- a pool the Narrator casts NPCs from. When a player takes a card an NPC was using, the NPC quietly swaps to another card that fits the same role (Pathfinder: Kingmaker does this). So every card needs **role tags** (see Delivery).

## Style: the same painter as the character portraits

- **Heavenprint**, exactly like the current character sets: style guide `art/style-guide/v1.3/PHC-Style-Guide.md` sections 01–07 and 12. Anchors are `approved-original-27.jpg`, `approved-new-faces-27.jpg` and the First-Patch Revival set.
- **800 × 1000 PNG (4:5)**, an opaque painted scene, one person.
- Lay each card out next to the existing 99 before delivering: it must look like the same hand.

## What makes these different: they are *player* cards

1. **The person is the hero of their own card.** Three-quarter view, head and shoulders in the **top third**, eyes to the viewer or just past them. The face must read at a 48 × 60 px thumbnail. The world characters can be caught mid-errand; these people *know they're being looked at*.
2. **Fun first.** Each card has one clear, specific beat (a gesture, a prop or an expression) that tells you the archetype's joke or promise from its blurb. Not a pin-up or a stat line, but a person you want to *be* for 50 hours. Bold silhouettes, a grin or a glare, a story in the hands.
3. **Nobody in particular.** No face, costume, haircut-and-outfit combination, weapon or prop that copies an existing character from any game, film or comic (the Unspoken Name). Paint the type. Don't feed outside character art into generation.
4. **A mixed table.** Across the set, aim for about half women and half men, with a few who read as neither. Cover ages from late teens to old, every skin tone, and every body type. The table below suggests one for each card so the spread works; change it if a better card comes out, but keep the spread.
5. **The world, not real Earth.** Gear comes from the EPCCG world: service pistols, Evokers (Sigil focus devices, angular and engraved with command glyphs), Emanators (heart-linked Soul conduits worn on the chest or wrist), mana cores (glassy crystals), field armour, AHOC patches are optional. Sigils glow as drawn line-glyphs; Soul magic glows from the chest along the veins. **Never** swap them: an Evoker is Mind (Sigil) and an Emanator is Soul.
6. **No text, no letters, no logos** in the painting.
7. **The background is a world**, not a studio backdrop: a hint of the place this person works. Keep it painterly and secondary to the face.

The light key per family: Frontline warm amber; Guns and hunters cold dawn; Shadows low violet with one warm rim light; Sigil casters cyan glyph-light; Minds lamp-and-screen; Soul and faith violet-gold; Voices and leaders stage-warm; Hybrids mixed; All-in **one colour pushed to the extreme** (they are 7/1/1 builds: everything in one place, so paint them as caricatures of their one thing, the funniest cards in the set).

## Delivery

- **Folder:** `art/character-portraits/archetypes/<family>/<id>.png`, with families `frontline`, `gunners`, `shadows`, `sigils`, `minds`, `soul`, `voices`, `hybrids`, `all_in`. Add a `previews/all-46.jpg` contact sheet and a `README.md` like the other sets.
- **Catalog** (`art/catalog.json`, `characters`): one entry each, id `arch-<id>`. Fields: `label` (the archetype's name, for the team only; the game never shows it on the card), `path`, `sha256`, `width`, `height`, `background: "opaque painted scene"`, `usage: "character portrait"`, `collection: "archetypes"`, `pool: "player-card"`, `archetype: "<id>"`, and `tags` (the table's tags, plus `woman` / `man` / `androgynous` and an age band `young` / `adult` / `old`). The game uses the tags to swap an NPC's card when a player takes it.
- **Checksums and validation:** update `art/checksums.sha256`, run `python3 scripts/validate_art.py`; it must pass.
- **Review sheet:** all 46 at thumbnail size (48 × 60) in a grid. Every face must still read, and no two cards should look like the same person.
- If time allows, a **second card for any archetype** (a different gender or age) is welcome later in the same folder as `<id>-2.png`, with the same catalog fields. More cards means more choice for players and more room for the NPC swap.
