# Central City Survival kit

All 36 Central City pieces are present as review candidates. The kit uses wet cobbles, weathered slate and brick, street-market props and worn cloth. The [64 px kit sheet](review/central-city-kit-64px.png) shows all pieces.

The wall family has matching 128 px connections and exposed rims. The door insert clears the passage when open. See the [64 px assembly](review/wall-joins-64px.png) and [full-size assembly](review/wall-joins-256px.png).

The three painted grounds were exported with a 20 px edge feather to match opposing edges. The [repeat measurements](ground-metrics.json) report values before and after this step. The [wet cobbles](review/ground-1-3x3-1024px.png), [flagstone](review/ground-2-3x3-1024px.png) and [damaged street](review/ground-3-3x3-1024px.png) have full-size 3×3 previews.

The [render prompts](prompts.json), native PNGs and superseded renders are archived here. The [updated queue](missing-assets.json) has 198 jobs. Daggerfall is the next full kit.
