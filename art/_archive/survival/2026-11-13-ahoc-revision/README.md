# AHOC Survival kit revision

The 21-piece core trial is ready for review. This revision repaints 13 of those pieces with the built-in image generator. The selected art stays marked as review candidates, with production approval and implementation readiness both false.

- The ceramic plate floor (K002) uses a new quieter repeat. The mean absolute RGB difference across the left/right join fell from 8.97 to 3.75. The top/bottom join fell from 49.04 to 3.08. Measurements are on a 0–255 scale. The gravel floor (K001) still shows stronger boundary differences than its interior and awaits visual approval.
- The corner, end, doorway and broken wall (K008–K011) use overhead cap paintings. The exports register their painted caps to the guide and make the connecting edge cuts solid. All five pieces have a centred 128 px band at the expected edges at alpha ≥ 128. The painted joints remain visible in places, especially in the straight run. The wall doorway has an exact 128 px clear gap before the door sprite is overlaid.
- The crate, low wall, barricade, door pair, container, terminal and lamp were repainted for the overhead camera and inspected at 64 px. The open door leaves the passage clear between its jambs. The lamp glass and terminal markings are painted on the objects. The crate footprint is approximately one metre at the trial scale.
