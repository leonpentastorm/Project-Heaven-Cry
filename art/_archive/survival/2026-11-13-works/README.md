# Machine Works Survival kit

All 36 pieces are present as review candidates. The [64 px kit sheet](review/works-kit-64px.png) shows the assembled set.

The wall connections use a matching 128 px cross-section. See the [64 px wall assembly](review/wall-joins-64px.png) and [full-size wall assembly](review/wall-joins-256px.png), including the open and closed door inserts.

The three ground exports use a 20 px boundary feather. [Measurements](ground-metrics.json) record the edge differences before and after it. The review folder contains 3×3 repeats at full and game scale. [Prompts](prompts.json), native PNGs, and the [remaining queue](missing-assets.json) are archived here.
