# Survival first-stage review notes

This is a trial, not an accepted map kit. 24 fight backdrops and 21 AHOC core pieces have been painted. The full 294-image commission is unfinished.

- Backdrops: native 1672 × 941, exported to 2560 × 1440 by ordinary resizing. They do not meet the requested native minimum of 1920 × 1080; no extra detail is claimed. The open centres and edge-dominant compositions are intentional for the tactical overlay.
- Gravel ground was repainted after the first attempt incorrectly included metal plates. Review its 3×3 repeats for visible seams and recurring diagonal texture.
- Plate ground has a strong horizontal join and is not accepted as seamless. Its planned repeat-unit revision hit the image-generation usage limit.
- Wall straight, end and doorway were repainted with overhead geometry guides. Corner correction hit the usage limit, and broken wall correction did not start after the limit. The selected corner and broken piece are the initial failed camera/geometry candidates. None of the wall family is certified for exact game-grid joins; inspect wall-joins.png and wall-geometry.json.
- Several initial kit pieces, especially the low wall, show excess front-face depth or a baked soft light/shadow. These need another visual pass before game integration. Transparency alone does not establish a shadow-free sprite.
- The brief explicitly calls for the AHOC core trial to be shown before the other five map kits. Painted battle maps, portraits, node/path scenes, gear follow-ups and cards have not been generated yet.
- Image generation is quota-blocked until 14 November 2026 around 09:31 Bangkok time. This is a tool usage limit, not an approval rejection.

Use the individual files as review candidates; do not integrate the failed wall/ground pieces as certified seamless assets. Native originals, superseded attempts, prompts and geometry guides are retained. The kit geometry guides are simple layout references; all painted artwork was made with image generation.
