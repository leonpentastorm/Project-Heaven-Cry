# Survival battle maps

All 18 battle maps are delivered as 2600 × 2600 opaque PNGs under `art/maps/survival/`, three per setting. These are style-model training material and review candidates. The archive keeps the native built-in ImageGen sources and records each export in the [map manifest](map-manifest.json).

The user chose local upscaling for this delivery. The map sources are 1254 × 1254. The exports use Lanczos resizing, which preserves the composition at the requested dimensions. The [native-resolution exceptions](native-resolution-exceptions.json) list also records the 37 earlier upscaled backdrop, path-map, node-scene, and card exports.
