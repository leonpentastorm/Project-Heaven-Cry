# Survival scenes and portraits

Two path-map directions, eight arrival scenes, ten NPC and creature portraits, and three optional mode cards are included as review candidates.

- [NPC portrait sheet](review/npc-portraits.png). The five human roles were repainted with distinct facial proportions, casting, silhouettes, viewpoints and local lighting. Their superseded render attempts are archived.
- [Scene and card sheet](review/survival-scenes.png). The path maps were rendered at 1672 × 941 and exported at 2560 × 1440. The node scenes and cards were rendered at 1448 × 1086 and exported at 2048 × 1536. Those native dimensions fall below the brief's minimums. The 1122 × 1402 portrait sources meet the portrait minimum.
- [Battle-map studies](map-studies.json). The three AHOC studies are 1254 × 1254 native. All 18 painted battle maps remain in the queue for a renderer that supports the 2080 × 2080 native minimum.

The [manifest](manifest.json), [dimensions](image-dimensions.json), [prompts](prompts.json) and [remaining queue at this stage](missing-assets.json) record the delivery. The inventory follow-ups have their own archive.
