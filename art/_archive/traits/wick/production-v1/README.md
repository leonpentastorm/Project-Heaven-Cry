## Review 3 — 5 November 2026 (51 icons, traits 3–60)

Reviewed by Claude for Leon on 128 px and actual 44 px sheets of every delivered icon, in data order. Leon can overrule any verdict.

**Overall:** the set is consistent. Line weight, screen-face language, the capelet and the thick-armed cross hold across all 51; no lettering, no second full figure, pure white ink. The best of the run carry the meaning in the prop alone: Company Man (puppet strings), Born Performer (spotlight cone), Heavyworlder (doorframe), Rival (measuring post), Cover Story (fan of ID cards), Market Blood (scale), Grudge Bearer (tally scroll), One Shot (the only prone silhouette), Insomniac, Holo-Drama Addict, Bottle Butts for Eyes.

**Pass (46):** every icon not listed below.

| # | Icon | Verdict | What to change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Southern Fist | Fix | At 44 px the rifle held at arm's length reads as Wick wielding it, the opposite of the trait (fists only; anything else is a clumsy stick)../../../../icons/traits Let the rifle dangle upside down from two mitten fingertips, well away from the body, and make the punching fist the biggest shape. |
| 46 | Sworn Sibling | Fix | The bound hand reads as a spear point at 128 and 44 px. Make the reaching hand bigger with an open palm, and the cord thicker with one knot halfway, so it's two hands tied together. |
| 32 | Daggerfall Veteran | Fix | Reads at 256 px, mush at 44: the blast cloud and debris swallow Wick. Keep one clean blast shape behind it, drop the debris, keep the mushroom on the screen. |
| 6 | Glass Runner | Small fix | The shin cracks carry the downside and vanish at 44 px. Make them two or three bold cracks, not hairlines. |
| 33 | Upper-Touched | Small fix | The dotted twin head dissolves into dots at 44 px. Use fewer, bigger dashes. |

**Silhouettes:** most of the run is standing or striding, three-quarter view, prop on the viewer's right. Still inside the brief's limit, but the remaining 14 traits and the perks should lean on seated, lying, crouched, upside-down and from-behind poses, and put the prop on the left now and then.

Keep `production_approved: false` on all 51 until Leon signs off.
