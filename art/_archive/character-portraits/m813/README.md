# M813 portraits — version 1

Two new Heavenprint review candidates, 800 × 1000 RGB PNGs, from [the M813 brief](../../../briefs/m813-mpat.md)../../../portraits/m813

| Catalog ID | File | Visible action |
|---|---|---|
| `mpat` | [MPAT](../../../portraits/m813/mpat.png) | Kneeling medic cooks over a field stove while listening to the head carried in its chest harness. |
| `mpat-comrade` | [The unnamed comrade](../../../portraits/m813/mpat-comrade.png) | Still-running infantry head wired to a scavenged battery, beside an unfinished replacement body. |

Original Heavenprint, New Faces, the approved pilot and First-Patch Revival sheets were supplied as references. The MPAT render also supplied continuity for the comrade and the world scenes. Source hashes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json)../../../portraits/m813 The full generated compositions were exported to the requested size; no existing approved portrait was replaced.

The ceramic shell, screen, chest lamp and field cloak connect MPAT to the 258th Medical Unit and Wick. The specific plating, cloak, orange comrade stripe and other construction choices remain **visual proposals**, as in the brief; this art does not add lore facts. The comrade remains unnamed.

Both portraits are opening-safe. World images and the ending-only delivery are indexed in [the M813 illustration collection](../../illustrations/m813/v1/README.md)../../../portraits/m813
