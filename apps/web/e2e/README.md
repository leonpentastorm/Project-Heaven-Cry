# End-to-end tests

Two real browsers play a session through the room relay, with the scripted Narrator (no API key needed).

- `two-players.mjs`: host opens a room, both make characters through the creation screens, the session starts, both speak, the Narrator calls a check (rolled on the dice table), a fight runs to the end on the grid, the story resumes, Level up opens.
- `turn-card-side.mjs`: the turn card's CARD SIDE in its customise dialog (Auto, Left, Right): Right brings the next card in on the right, Left on the left, kept after a reload, Auto clears it.
- `tabs.mjs`: the combat action tabs in the Proving Ground at 1440×900 and 1280×720 (every tab, the glyph keyboard and the Outburst design cards open; the grid and Confirm stay on screen with no page scroll), keys 1–6 and arrows, the turn card and a hostile click switching tabs, the last tab remembered, and a 900 px window with the action dock at the bottom. Shots: `tabs-*.png`. Combat tests click actions with `pickAction()` from `lib.mjs`, which opens the tab that holds the action first.
- `mapzoom.mjs`: the combat map's size in a Proving Ground fight: Auto at 1280×720, 1600×900, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 (bigger screens, bigger map; it always fits the window height and the action zone keeps 400 px), − / 100% / + / Auto, the − and + keys, Ctrl + wheel over the map, 250% scrolling inside its zone, kept after a reload, Auto following the splitter. Shots with `SHOTS=dir`: `mapzoom-*.png`.
- `layout.mjs`: the combat screen's adjustable layout in a Proving Ground fight: the splitter (drag and arrow keys; the action zone keeps its 360 px), the combat log dragged into the action zone, the legend above the map, the action menu dragged left to swap the sides, all kept after a reload; Hide panel (H) brings the log back under the map; ↺ Layout resets; narrow windows hide the splitter and grips. Shots with `SHOTS=dir`: `layout-*.png`.
- `slate-title.mjs`: the title at 1280×720, 1440×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 900×800 and 390×844: the words never clip, the back and mid key art load, parallax moves, Wick runs / idles / says hello, hover works on other rows during his hello, and the first-key greeting. Shots: `slate-title-*.png`.
- `slate.mjs`: the combat panel in a Proving Ground fight: the Say row stays on screen (1440×900, 1280×720, 1093×526, 900×800), Hide panel (the map widens and stays clear of the mini-bar, keys 1–4 and H, remembered after a reload), and customising the turn card's four commands (slot 1 → Reload shows on the next card, Reset). Shots: `slate-combat-*.png`.
- `slate-stamp.mjs`: the Lock in stamp caught at ~120 ms and ~500 ms, APPROVED, filed once, reduced motion fades. Shots: `slate-stamp-*.png`.
- `slate-music.mjs`: two players in the Proving Ground: no fight music while gearing up, Gunline for a wave, Heaven Falls for a boss, Last Rounds with half the party Downed (both browsers), then Leave puts both on the room screen.
- `sandbox.mjs`: the Proving Ground from the title: gearing up, the magic kit, give / unequip / equip, a mini-map click starts the fight, waves, a Sigil, Retry, Leave to the room, no Narrator calls.
- `magic.mjs`: magic from creation to a fight (Sigils under rules 1.4): the Kit step's starting pair (SPARK + SEEK by default; FIRE + ARC picked, a Subject and a Reach word), the MAGIC sheet tab, a 3-glyph Line (FIRE · ARC · SPREAD, the Narrator teaching SPREAD) walked on the area map and Evoked from its end, then in a fight a Line walked away from the raider and Evoked back at it; begin / hold / release an Outburst, vent, the workshop. Shots: `magic-*.png`.
- `sigil14.mjs`: Sigil rules 1.4 in the Proving Ground (Solo, Enemy AI off, the Recruit given an Evoker, cores and words through the tools): a FIRE · SEEK · SPREAD Line walked with the glyph keyboard; Evoke's Line panel, the facing arrows where you can stand, the tinted area, a hovered target with its burst tiles and the affected units with numbers, the Evoke FX; a CHAIN spell's numbered jumps; a WAIT trap set on a drill unit that it springs at its turn start; a WATCH turret firing; RAISE walls and NULL wiping them; a STEP blink; KIN's fizzle warning; a Sigil adept (`sandbox/spawn` with `caster`) drawing and Evoking at you. Shots: `sigil14-*.png`.
- `sigil15-strokes.mjs`: Sigil 1.5 strokes (rules 1.9) in the Proving Ground as a Mage with Brain Power Mastered (Solo, Enemy AI off): two Lines drawn side by side with the stroke picker under the selected action ("extends FIRE", "new Line", the junctions), the dashed joining line of the preview, the faint joins on the grid (none between the two rows), the hovered stroke lit and named ("FIRE · SEEK"), each Line Evoked on its own, and a junction with both joins and its two Lines offered on Evoke. Shots: `sigil15-*.png` (or `SHOTS=dir`).
- `sigil15-growth.mjs`: Sigil 1.5 growth (rules 1.9) in the Proving Ground, Solo: the tools' Magic tab (the five word groups and the Outburst lessons, collapsible; grant study, set a word's use count, the Junctions switch, place a sigil mark by tile), the sheet's MAGIC tab (the study bar, Learn a word in the five groups, a word learned with a pick, use counts with hollow and filled stars, inflections greyed until Mastered; Sigil and Outburst sections collapse and stay so after a reload), and the mark on the area map: the carved plate, its panel (age, damage, TN, Analysis or Rumination, unread until copied) and the outcome of Investigate. Shots: `sigil15g-*.png` (or `SHOTS=dir`).
- `sigil15.mjs`: Sigil 1.5 (rules 1.9) end to end in the Proving Ground, Enemy AI off: a sigil mark placed by the tools against a wall, Investigated from the area map's panel (the word copied, +3 study, the panel's outcome); two Lines drawn side by side (a glyph under a finished Line starts its own; the picker's "extends SPARK") and each Evoked as its own spell; a crossing: no junction offered below Brain Power Mastered, the tools' "Junctions for everyone here" switch, SPREAD joining FIRE's and SPARK's Lines, both carried on through it (FIRE · SPREAD · SEEK / SPARK · SPREAD · ARC), Evoke offering both Lines and firing one; the three Sigil 1.5 sounds served (sg_join, sg_inflect, sg_study); then a two-browser team fight where Team B draws SHOCK and Team A Analyzes that fighter through the action menu and copies the word (study rises). Shots: `sigil15e-*.png` (or `SHOTS=dir`).
- `perks.mjs`: perks, traits and class moves in play. In the Proving Ground: the tools' Perks and Traits sections (sandbox/perk, sandbox/trait), the PERKS tab and Breaching Strike's knockback on a drill unit, Fighter from the tools (the CLASS tab and the You card carry the emblem) and Lunge, Used to the Pain's AP line, reload only on a gun (none with the sabre), the level-up class panel. Then a room with the scripted Narrator: Oil in My Soul's +1 named in the dice table's roll math and the story line. Shots: `perks-*.png`.
- `classes.mjs`: Class Roster Revision 3 in the Proving Ground: every attribute tied at level 3 gives the level-up picker all nine classes (emblem, attribute in its ink, fantasy line, resource, both rank I moves, the permanent warning; 3 × 3 at 1440×1000, one column at 420 wide); select, preview, confirm; a second pick refused; the attribute at 5 unlocks rank II; the CLASS tab's emblem and moves in a fight; the tools' class menu has only the nine base classes. Shots: `classes-*.png`.
- `classes-moves.mjs`: the 20 Revision 3 class moves in play (Proving Ground with a friend: the host acts, the guest is the squadmate): Shove (the Knock Prone chip), Unstoppable, Command (squadmate, then tile), Vanish, Field Fix (jobs that don't apply greyed with their reason), Rig (✱ RIG on the grid), Sabotage on a drone, Read (the intent marker on the enemy and its target), False Image (the translucent decoy, the guest sees it too), Silver Tongue, Sleight, Rally, Duck and Roll and Shrug It Off off/on (the one in force ticked), Vent 0 AP and Swap core 1 AP for the Mage, Eye of the Storm ARMED, the Guard and Strain rows; each through its real button, engine state and fx events checked. Shots: `classes-moves-*.png`.
- `missions.mjs`: OOC and the mission board with the scripted Narrator (no API calls): the MISSIONS · 5 header button and the board (no Narrator notes on it), an OOC line from the OOC chip and from a typed `(OOC:` marker (muted, labelled, the Say unused), the Narrator's answer in its NARRATOR · OOC box, an objective done (the toast, the header count, the board's ✓), the next mission, and the act end: the MISSION COMPLETE card, Continue back to the story, still closed after a reload. The scripted Narrator marks `done:<objective id>` from a player's line. Shots with `SHOTS=dir`: `missions-*.png`.
- `combat-fixes.mjs`: the Proving Ground tools' Max every skill, All to 0 and each skill's own Max; and the enemies' turns: a move clicked while they still play waits ("the other turns are still playing out") and goes through once they have played.
- `reconnect.mjs`: both players refresh mid-session and come back to the story; the EPCCG "?" entry and the lightbox open.

Run against local dev (`npm run dev -w @phc/server` after `npm run build`) or the live site:

```bash
node apps/web/e2e/two-players.mjs
BASE=https://project-heaven-cry.leonpentastorm.workers.dev node apps/web/e2e/reconnect.mjs
```

Screenshots land in `apps/web/e2e/shots/`. Playwright is loaded with `require('playwright')`.
