# Prayer 2.0: the god is seen, and the Chaplain sings for the squad (proposal, full size)

15 Nov 2026, first draft. Status: **design, nothing built**. Every number below is PROVISIONAL and a data field
(`data/classes.json` guardian rows, `data/conditions.json`, `data/patrons.json` unless named); if accepted, the spec
(rules 1.6, 1.22, 1.31) changes first, as a new revision, with its numbers as spec items. Every item is
`adjudication: engine`. Sister drafts: `2026-11-bonds-2.0.md` (origin/wip/design-bonds-2), `2026-11-figments-2.0.md`;
this one reuses their P3 and P8 and adds one piece (P9).

Leon, 15 Nov 2026, verbatim: "All players need to feel like they do something only they do best, all player need to
feel satisfy fighting." and, on the newer doors: "no effect, no animation, no notthing, feels like standing still".

## 1. Honest diagnosis (three lines)

1. Prayer is already the widest door after Sigils: eight patrons, 24 Hymns, seven Whispers, Devotion, Witnessed,
   interventions and a Miracle cut-in. It does not need more prayers.
2. What it lacks is **feedback for the Chaplain's own acts**: Ward reuses the Sigil's `ward` look, Smite, Judged, Rebuke,
   the patron's presence and the Hymn's wait have no moment at all, and the patron's likes are only readable in a tab.
3. The Chaplain has **no job of its own**: its typical turn (Hymn, shot with Smite, Ward) is a gunner who prays.
   Nothing it does makes the squad around it stronger turn after turn, which is the fantasy of a chaplain.

**What only the Chaplain does best, in one sentence a player would repeat:** *"My Chaplain keeps a Canticle going, and
everyone standing near him fights blessed until somebody hurts him."*

## 4. What only the Chaplain does: the Canticle and its turn

The Canticle is the Chaplain's **gain** (`classes.json guardian.gain`, beside The Patron at Your Side), so it is the
class's own and keeps "two moves a rank". It is **not** a prayer: it does not use the turn's Whisper or the Hymn wait,
so the turn becomes Canticle (1) + Whisper or Ward (1) + weapon (4), or Canticle + Hymn (3) + a shot (2).

## 5. A Chaplain's turn after this

Round 1: Canticle (verse I) + Hymn (Saint's Shield on the tank) + a shot with Smite: the squad carries *Shelter*, the
tank a shield, the foe is Judged. Round 2: Canticle (verse II), Ward on yourself is not allowed, so step behind cover
(1 AP) and shoot twice; the gunner's hit on the Judged foe rings Amen. Round 3: Canticle (verse III, 3 tiles, +1 to
hit), Rebuke the half-dead foe (Holy Dread: Shaken), shoot. Decisions a turn: keep singing or not (1 AP of the gun),
where to stand (2 tiles of whom), Whisper or Ward, whom the Hymn helps and who will Amen it, when to Rebuke. Today: 3 to 4;
after: 6 to 7, and four of them only a Chaplain has.

## 7. Onboarding (the three rules, exact texts)

1. **The first action is always on the bar.** For a character with Faith 1: the Whisper group stays listed when it
   cannot be used, greyed, with its reason: *"One Whisper a turn. It comes back on your next turn."* For a Chaplain the
   Canticle row is always there; greyed reasons: *"You sang this turn."* and, after a wound, *"The song broke. Sing again
   next turn: stand behind cover or ask for a Ward."* Reasons are descriptor `why` strings from the action's data
   (`why` fields in the gain row), never typed in the client.
2. **The door's line on its tab** (`skills.json faith.door_line`, the audit's shared field; no digits in it): *"Pray to
   your patron. A Whisper is quick and comes once a turn; a Hymn is strong and then must wait. Your patron watches what
   you do: the marks on your actions say what it likes."* The Chaplain's line (`guardian.gain canticle.door_line`):
   *"Sing the Canticle each turn: everyone near you carries your patron's verse until a wound breaks the song."*
3. **One hint, once** (`apps/web/src/game/tips.tsx` `TIPS`, the audit's hook: combat screen, my turn, my character has
   the door): title **PRAYER**, text the faith `door_line`; for a Chaplain a second entry, title **CANTICLE**, text the
   Canticle's `door_line`. Shown once per browser, like every tip.

a Chaplain past rank II. Proof: `test/old-save.test.ts` with a rank III Chaplain fixture.
