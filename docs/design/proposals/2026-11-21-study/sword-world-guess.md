## 2. Sword World 2.5 in depth

### (1) Core roll
- 2d6 + Standard Value (usually class level + ability modifier) against a target number. Double 6 auto-success, double 1 auto-failure (RIGHT, GM Binder). Double 1 pays 50 XP (RIGHT, EN World).
- Modifier = score (base + correction + growth + item bonuses) divided by 6, rounded down (RIGHT, GM Binder). The three-base-plus-rolled-corrections A to F structure was not re-confirmed in this pass; "no penalties" not verified.
- **Growth roll (corrected):** after a session or when the GM says, roll 2d6, choose one die, and add +1 to the matching ability's correction box (RIGHT, GM Binder). EN World's list maps die faces 1 to 6 to Dex, Agi, Str, Vit, Int, Spirit.
- HP = Adventurer Level x 3 + Vitality score. MP = total Wizard-type class levels x 3 + Spirit score. Adventurer Level = highest class level (all RIGHT, GM Binder).
- Power Table, crit values, and the "+4 to +20" range are NOT FOUND/not verified (the cap-end figures are the researcher's estimate).

### (2) Classes
- XP cost tables RIGHT (EN World), cumulative: Table A 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,500 / 5,000 / 7,000 / 9,500; Table B 500 / 1,500 / 2,500 / 4,000 / 5,500 / 7,500 for levels 1 to 6. Which classes use which table is NOT FOUND in that source.
- Starting XP: almost all origins 2,000; a few 2,500; Normal Person and Adventurer 3,000 (EN World). "No class above 2 at creation" not verified.
- Level 15: the thread says Rulebook III goes up to level 15 (EN World). The per-book split (I: 1 to 5, II: to 10) is NOT FOUND.
- Per-class derived-number design and the Sage and Scout examples are (reading) apart from the Sage point below.

### (4) Enemies and campaign length
- Sage Monster Knowledge reveals weaknesses and the party benefits (RIGHT, Rascal). Reputation/Weakness stat names not verified.
- Climactic boss: 1 or 2 levels above the party's average Adventurer Level, with one Sword Shard; non-climactic bosses are one level higher with no shard (RIGHT, EN World). "Ordinary fights at party level, as many monsters as party members" not verified.
- **Sword Shards:** +5 HP and +1 MP each; resistance bonus +1 for 1 to 5 shards, +2 for 6 to 10 (so about +1 per 5: RIGHT); augmented monsters give no extra XP; each retrieved shard can be cashed at session end for 1d6 Prestige, or sold for 200 G (RIGHT, EN World). **"To each party member" is NOT FOUND** in the source; it says only that each shard can be turned in. Treat as unclear.
- **Session XP:** 1,000 success, 500 failure, 10 x monster level per kill, 50 per double 1 (RIGHT, EN World, with one worked example). The thread also gives "x100" in one place and leaves the conflict unresolved.

## What it teaches PHC (reading)
1. One number per role keeps multiclassing strong: each door reads its own skill.
2. Rising per-level XP costs, paid per class, make a cheap second line and an expensive main line.
3. Pay failure: half XP for a lost scenario and a bonus for a natural 1.
4. Optional difficulty paid in a side currency (shards: tougher boss, same XP, Prestige or gold) fits Survival Elites and Bosses.
5. A growth roll gives a steady trickle but fits poorly with a hard cap at 10.
6. Session-shaped encounters (a few fights, then a boss 1 to 2 levels up) resemble PHC's budget generator.
