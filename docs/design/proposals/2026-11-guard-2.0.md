# Guard 2.0: a wall you play every turn (proposal, full size)

15 Nov 2026, first draft. Status: **design, nothing built**. Every number below is PROVISIONAL and a data field
(`data/techniques.json rules.guard` unless named); if accepted, the spec (Guard, rules 1.13 and the 0.28.3 tank fit)
changes first, as a new revision, with its numbers as spec items 2300 to 2349 (the lead assigns the range). Every item
is `adjudication: engine`. Sister drafts: `2026-11-figments-2.0.md` (origin/wip/design-figments-2) and
`2026-11-bonds-2.0.md` (origin/wip/design-bonds-2); this one reuses their P1, P2 and P8 and adds three pieces.

Leon, co-op Survival on live 0.28.2, verbatim: "the tank have nothing fancy for his own, playing him feels like liquid
Shit. even with a shield it's just 10 more fake hp a turn." / "Maybe all hit with the tank is a duo hit when he have
the shield because he swing his shield with his weapon too." / "he got hit by one crit and 2 normal gun shot with 2
shield and just barely escape death, at like 5 durability." / earlier: "the tank can't hit shit due to all skill point
being in durability and his shield points block NOTTHING."

**Diagnosis, in three lines.** Guard is invisible arithmetic: a pip on the sheet, a log line, no BLOCKED, no plate,
nothing breaks. It is spent passively, so the tank's turn is "press Guard, wait"; the only things that spend it on
purpose are Bash, Draw Fire and Raise Shield. And it protects only the tank, only from the first hit, never from a
crit, and never anyone standing next to it.

## 1. Where the door stands (what this builds on)

| Door | What a player chooses and earns | What it does on a turn |
|---|---|---|
| Sigil, Outburst, melee, guns | words, feelings, techniques, modes; deep forks; strong moments | many orders, each with its own look |
| **Guard today** | nothing: Durability points are the pool and the gain; mastery 3 / 5 are Take the Hit and Second Wind | Guard (1 AP) or Brace (1 AP), Bash with a shield; everything else is automatic |

**The numbers lane (build on it, do not redo it).** `origin/fix/0.28.3-tank` is not on origin yet: the fit is in flight,
uncommitted, in the worktree `phc-0283-tank` (local branch `fix/0.28.3-tank`). It makes Guard Durability's own
technique with any weapon (`techniques.json guard.innate {skill: durability, min: 1}`, drawn on a gun's pad by
`techniquePad`'s door groups), cuts the soak 10 to 6 a point (`rules.guard.soak`; measured about 70% taken alone,
44% beside a gunner, `test/balance/tank-measure.ts`), and gives a Guard holder +1 to hit per Durability point above 3,
at most +4 (`skills.json durability.unlocks.1`, a `roll_mod` on `attack` with `self_has_res: [guard]`). Everything
below sits on top of that and is fitted with the same measure.

**Duplicates found (fixed by item 12 and the build order, not by new rules):** two Brace actions with two formulas
(the weapon's `braceHook`, combat.ts, flat 2 / 3; the Bulwark's "Hold the Line", classes.json, 1 + Durability's
modifier, and `classes/j.ts` copies it for the Adamantile Knight); two Interposes (the class move, `classes/i.ts`, and
the Answer, `physical/guard.ts`); Take the Hit at Durability 3 beside the innate Interpose at Durability 3; Reactive
Plate's "10 a point" beside the general soak every Guard holder now has.

## 6. The shield strikes too (Leon's pair)

| # | Item | Rule a player reads | Data | Built from | Builds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | **The shield's own swing** | A shield in the off hand strikes with Durability, not with your fists. | each shield row (`items.json buckler`, `riot_shield`) gains `phys.strike {hits_with: durability, scales_with: [durability], damage, mult}` (buckler 12, riot shield 16); `inHand` (physical/core.ts) reads it before its `fists` fallback; Shield Bash uses it | **P11** (an off-hand item's own strike row) | P11 |
| 17 | **The Shield Follows** | With a shield, each weapon attack on a foe next to you is followed by a shield swing; a hit leaves it Off Balance (Body save). | the shield property row: `strike {when: acted, if: {action: [attack], target_within: 1, target_alive: true}, hand: off, free: true, extra: follow_hit}` (`follow_hit` −1, the paired one-hander's off-hand term), then `condition off_balance {via: durability}` on its hit; once per attack action (a Burst is one action, one swing) | trigger `acted`; **P9** (`strike`, with `hand: off`), P11 | P9, P11 |

**Judged against the machinery that exists.** `pairedAttack` (combat.ts) is a separate action and requires
`u.offWeapon`; a shield is `offItem`, so it never fires, and making a shield a weapon would put it on the weapon pad,
the gun collection and the ammo code. `PAIRED_TECHNIQUES` needs two weapons of one system. `inHand` already swings a
shield, as `fists` (the neutral fallback). So the pair is the shield's own data (item 16) plus one general op that
makes one attack with a named hand (P9); no second paired-attack path. A gun shot at an adjacent foe gets the swing
too (Leon: "he swing his shield with his weapon"), question 1.

## 10. Saves

Guard lives only in a fight (lost at its end, `character.ts`), so no saved number changes. A character gains
`c.practice.durability` (P2) and a `guard_paths` pick list (P8), both empty; a character past Durability 3, 5 or 7 is
owed those picks on its next level-up card. Shield rows gain a field; an old shield instance reads it from the catalogue.
The Bulwark's Interpose policy carries over as its `answer_on` entry. Proof: `test/old-save.test.ts` with a fixture of a
Durability 7 Bulwark holding a riot shield.

## 11. Needs a new primitive (general pieces only; each the lead's to approve)

Reused from the Figments and Bonds drafts: **P2** (one practice ledger by door), **P8** (one fork action for every
door). New, three:

1. **P9, the `strike` op:** the holder makes one attack at the event's target (or `targets`) with a named hand (`main`,
   `off`), `free` (no AP), `extra` to hit, `mult`; off its own turn it is a reaction (its `limit`). It goes through
   `resolveAttack` and `inHand`, so every hook, the fx and the log are the ordinary ones. Uses: the Shield Follows, Hold
   the Gap; later, any "strike back" item. It retires Anchor's per-item opportunity code in `classes/rev3.ts` (a
   baseline that shrinks).
2. **P10, a condition gives its unit cover:** a condition row field `cover: half | full` (the condition table's
   fields), read by `coverUnits` beside `owned.cover`; such a unit never enters `sightBlockers`. Uses: Wall, Stand
   Behind Me; later a riot-shield foe line.
3. **P11, an off-hand item's own strike row:** a non-weapon off-hand item may carry `phys.strike` (`hits_with`,
   `scales_with`, `damage`, `mult`); `inHand` reads it before the `fists` fallback. Uses: every shield; later anything
   held in the off hand that can hit.

Not primitives: two fields on the existing `guard` fx (`soaked`, `why`); the conditions `walled` (data).
