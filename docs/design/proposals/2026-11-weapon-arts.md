# Weapon Arts: a weapon's own special action, as a card (queued)

Leon, 10 Nov 2026. **Queued, not designed.** This file records the idea as he gave it and what has to be decided before it is built. "Weapon Art" and "card" are working names (Suggested).

## The idea, in Leon's words

> "like i want a katana that have a skill that fires out a sword wave like anime or some shit, these takes kills to charge and go on a different row in the attack tab. this is my card idea i tell you about, the katana have this swordwave skill, very exclusive, then you get to mk3 on that katana then you can pull that sword wave card out and put in on a great sword. Special martial class let you use 2 of these card at once in one weapon. dualwield get you 1 per weapon"

## What that says

1. **A weapon can carry one special action of its own**, far stronger and stranger than a technique (a katana's sword wave). It is exclusive to that weapon: nobody learns it from the shared pool.
2. **It charges on kills**, not on AP, Momentum or Pressure.
3. **It has its own row on the Attack tab**, apart from the plain attack and the Techniques pad.
4. **It is a card.** At drill mark III with that weapon, the card can be taken out of it and set into another weapon (the sword wave on a greatsword).
5. **One card to a weapon.** A special martial class holds two in one weapon. Dual wielding gives one for each weapon.

## Where it sits beside what exists

- **Techniques** (Physical 2.0): 60, shared, readied from a list; a weapon lends one and teaches it at mark I. Weapon Arts are a layer above these, not a replacement.
- **The 59 old weapon skills** (Fan the Hammer, One Breath, Through and Through…): written in Weapons v2, 32 never run by the engine, and off the Attack tab since Physical 2.0. They are the first source of Arts for ordinary weapons.
- **Drill marks** already exist (I teaches the signature, later marks give picks). Mark III unlocking "take the card out" fits without a new track.
- **Kills as a charge** already count for drill; the charge can read the same event.

## To decide before building

1. **Which weapons have an Art.** Every weapon, or only named and rare ones (so finding the katana matters).
2. **The charge.** How many kills, by enemy tier (drill already weighs minion 5, soldier 10, elite 25, boss 50); whether it carries between fights; whether a moved card keeps its charge.
3. **Moving a card.** Whether the source weapon loses it; what a card may be set into (any weapon, the same system, the same hands); what it costs and where it is done (a rest, a workbench, anywhere).
4. **Which class holds two**, and whether that is a base class's rank or one of the advanced classes still shelved.
5. **Scale.** A sword wave is a ranged area attack from a melee weapon: what damage against the balance targets (350 a hit, 500 on a mega crit), and whether an Art ignores the weapon's own system (a melee weapon making a ranged attack).
6. **Art needs.** Each Art wants its own effect on screen; by the standing rule, textured effects are sourced (CC BY or CC0), SVG ones are drawn.
7. **Enemies.** Whether elites and bosses carry Arts, and whether killing one drops the card.

## When

After Skeleton 2.0. It belongs with the equipment pass (Leon's "Equipment 2.0", which also holds tri-scale armour), since cards are a property of items.
