**The targets conflict, and the table (`xp_total` 100 / 250) is why.** Level 2 by stop 5 on the gear route means two
fights must pay 100 (the gear route has only 2 to 3 fights in rows 1 to 5, the most-fights route 3 to 4). At 50 a
fight, the next three fights give level 3: entering stop 8 (median) on the most-fights route, before the gate, against
Leon's "only get to level 3 as you are about to take on the first boss". No linear f(d) separates the two: the
front-loaded C (55, -3) still gives level 3 at stop 9 median with 108 of 230 most-fights characters before the gate,
and slows stage 2. The gear route's level 2 jumps from 50% (base 42) to 91% (base 50) exactly where two fights reach
100. **A is recommended because it honours Leon's words and the three level-3 targets; level 2 arrives one to two
stops late on the gear route.** If the lead prefers level 2 by stop 5, set B (50 / 0): every listed target is met,
and level 3 comes at the gate, not near the Boss. The real fix for both is a level-2 threshold, which is the shared
`xp_total` (not this lane's dial).
