# Items 2.0: the tables (companion to `2026-11-20-items-2.0-design.md`)

Every family, form and grade the design signs off, checked against `data/` and the code on `release/0.33` (commit
`957d14f53`). EXISTS: the id is a row in `data/weapons.json` or `data/items.json` today. NEW: the id is the family
file's own (ruled 19 Nov, R1); none of them occurs anywhere in `data/`, `packages/` or `apps/` (checked with
`git grep -w`). Where a family file and the data disagree, the data is what is written here and the file is named as
stale.

Abbreviations: Dex = Dexterity (hits) / Agility (grows); Str = Raw Power (hits) / Strength (grows); Dex/Str = hits with
Dexterity, grows with Strength (the hatchet's pattern). "Second" = the R5 second skill (an alpha skill or none): it feeds
the family signature, never the hit.

Numbers for NEW rows (damage, range, magazine, AP beyond what the category decides, price, durability, mod slots) are
**not decided**: they come from the per-form ratio table (design section 1, item 11) and are fitted in stage C.

## 1. Grades (one rule for every graded item)

Today's `data/rules.json` `rarity` rows (rules 1.26, PROVISIONAL), read at `combat.ts:580` (accuracy), `:585` (damage
multiplier), `character.ts:175` `maxDurOf` (durability), `credits.ts` `priceOf` (price), `gearpreview.ts` `rarityMarks`
(marks). The grade lives on the instance (`GearState.rarity`, `character.ts:169`); a row is always the Standard.

| Grade | Accuracy | Damage | Durability | Price | Marks | `arm` (new, armour) | Where it comes from in Survival |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | +0 | x1 | x1 | x1 | 0 | x1 | drops, the counter |
| Fine | +1 | x1 | x1.25 | x3 | 1 | x1 (stage C) | shop, rewards, Forge step 1,500 |
| Masterwork | +2 | x1 | x1.5 | x8 | 2 | x1 (stage C) | shop by stage, boss reward, Forge step 3,500 |
| Relic | +2 | x1 | x1.5 | x25 | 3 | x1 (stage C) | Relic step 6,500 at a Forge from stage 3 (one of 7 `relic_props`; refused for armour in this pass), boss reward |
| Celestial | +2 | x1 | x1.5 | x50 | 4 | x1 (stage C) | boss reward only; never sold |

Forge step fees: `data/survival.json` `grade_up.price` (fine 1,500, masterwork 3,500, relic 6,500), x0.8 at a Forge for
Fine and Masterwork, the Relic step flat. The `arm` column is the only new field; it is 1 for every grade until stage C
fits it (design section 1, item 4). It scales a piece's armour value only, applied in `gearEffects` reading the
instance; a worn piece with no armour value is ungraded (`graded: false`). The spec's +1 mod slot for Fine and
Masterwork is not built today; it is built in stage C behind a data column that can be 0 (design item 3). Celestial's
quirk: not designed.

Not graded (file 08, section 8): the uniques; grenades, flashbang, satchel charge; consumables, rounds, magazines;
materials, tools, food, lights, loot; mods; prosthetic arm, drone frame; prescription glasses, smiling mask; fists and
beast bite; bot items; the mana and advanced-class weapons (`evoker_blade`, `emanator_gauntlet`, `emanator_katana`,
`spellslinger_pistol`, `grand_staff`, `core_rifle`, `lumeri_censer_gun`); every worn piece with no armour value
(design item 4: `field_helmet`, `night_goggles`, `gas_mask`, `utility_belt`, `runner_trousers`, `work_gloves`,
`field_boots`; new: `cowl`, `hood`, `blackout_hood`, `spotters_monocle`, `sight_visor`, `breather`, `sealed_visor`,
`rig_belt`, `jump_boots`). Graded items in all: 164 as first counted (72 rows + 5 body armour classes exist; 87 new),
less the 16 worn pieces above (7 existing, 9 new) = 148; not recounted beyond that.
