# Items 2.0: the lead's numbers for the new rows (step B0, 20 Nov 2026)

Written by the lead (the game designer). **Every number here is PROVISIONAL**, goes into `data/` exactly as written, and is fitted in stage C by simulated runs against the 0.33 walk-in baseline. A builder types these rows and **chooses no number**: a field missing here is copied from the row named under "copy from", and anything still missing is handed back, not guessed. Companion to `2026-11-20-items-2.0-design.md` and `-tables.md`.

## The rule behind the numbers (one copy)

- **A family's three forms trade, they do not climb.** Close: lighter, shorter, cheaper, about 10% less damage. Long: more range and about 10 to 15% more damage a hit, paid for with a smaller magazine, weight, heat, or a slower cycle. Nobody's best weapon is simply "the long one".
- **Melee damage follows AP.** 2 AP forms (light, hybrid, gloves) sit at 15 to 24; 3 AP forms (Strength) at 32 to 46. A reach or two-handed 2 AP form sits at the top of its band and pays in hands.
- **A hybrid hits with Dexterity and grows with Strength** and sits 1 to 3 points over its family's Dexterity form.
- **Price follows power and rarity of the maker**, in the bands today's rows already use.
- **Everything not listed** (durability, mod slots, heat rules, recoil, reload, modes' AP) is copied from the "copy from" row. Durability and mod slots are by category as today.
- **Signatures and second-skill effects** (design item 10) are not numbers of this file: a row ships plain unless an existing engine piece carries its signature.

Placements the design left to the lead: the unique-ready variants line and the saved `GearState.weapon_art` field belong to lane **A1**. The reviser's four placements (A4 types `graded: false` and removes `skill` and repeated `teaches`; A2 owns `classes/j.ts:372`; A1 owns `physical/data.ts`; B0 writes the spec section 14 listings) are confirmed.
