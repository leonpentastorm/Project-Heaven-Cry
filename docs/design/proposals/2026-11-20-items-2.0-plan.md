# Items 2.0: the plan and Leon's rulings (20 Nov 2026)

Starts the moment 0.33.0 is live. Owed work stays in `docs/ROADMAP.md`; this file is the scope and the rulings. Design inputs: `2026-11-18-scout-items-2.0.md`, `2026-11-19-items-2.0-families/`, `2026-11-weapon-arts.md`. The lead had read only the outlines of the scout and the family files when this was written: stage 0 reads them in full, and lists here that depend on them may change.

## Leon's rulings (20 Nov 2026)

1. **Placeholder pictures: yes.** "Only the stats need to work to test." The artwork is being made now (about 300 of about 800 done, started days ago); an item ships with a placeholder until its picture is taken in.
2. **Weapon Arts are not usable in this pass.** "Only balance needs to remember Weapon Arts exist; Weapon Art being usable will come in another patch later dedicated to that system." Balance leaves the room for them (a unique card puts a weapon at the melee ceiling at once; see the Weapon Arts note, Decided 5).
3. **The uniques are cut from this pass**, "but the underlying tag system needs to be robust enough for them to be added later in the artwork / Weapon Art patch."
4. **"Arts" means Weapon Arts**, not artwork.
5. **Weapon-side balance fits wait for this pass** (the Dexterity melee line, gun ceilings with mods, the Figments door and Rigger class lines, the Mender's solo kit). Baseline: the ambush lane's "walk in" column in `docs/design/handoffs/0.33-MEASURES.md` and the 0.33 ambush hand-off.
6. **Scope is the lever** for the weekly usage: build the systems once as data, fill them with content already ruled, leave out what needs art or the advanced classes.

## Stages

**0. Design sign-off (the lead; no builder before Leon rules).**
- Audits: unused artwork with a thumbnail sheet (Leon picks reuse; nothing deleted); the armour slot list; items with an icon and no engine reader (ration pack, chalk and wax, prayer beads, cigarettes; flare and torch unverified); whether the Forge realign can override `scales_with` per weapon.
- One design document: the family tables, a "Built from" table against `docs/ENGINE-HOOKS.md`, one consolidated list of new primitives.

**A. The item system (one Opus builder).**
- Families as data: a weapon belongs to a family; the family gives its form, techniques and second skill (R5: a variant's second skill is an alpha skill or none).
- **The tag system (ruling 3):** every item carries tags from one closed, documented list (family, form, maker line, grade, rarity, pattern, slot, and a free `card` slot for a Weapon Art); general code reads tags, never ids; a unique is a row with its own tags and, later, its own card: adding one is a data line.
- Grades as a rule: grade scheme S1, the five grades computed from the Standard row (no row per grade; no grade pictures needed to play: a frame and colour on the Standard picture).
- Armour slots; one item card component everywhere an item is drawn.
- Old saves: today's 72 weapon ids load unchanged (migration tested through the real load path).

**B. Content as data (Sonnet builders, from verified tables).**
- The 87 new Standard items as rows; the 17 repaints change pictures only, never numbers; placeholders until intake.

**C. Balance, once, at the end.**
- The weapon-side fits of ruling 5, by simulated runs; ceilings leave Weapon Arts their room (ruling 2).

## Not in this pass

- Weapon Arts usable (the card row on the Attack tab, kill charge, copies at mark III, family cards): its own later patch.
- The 23 uniques and their cards: the artwork / Weapon Art patch.
- Grade pictures (656): intake as they arrive.
- Enemies using Arts; the class that holds two cards: with the advanced classes.

## Art intake (when pictures arrive)

`art/catalog.json` entries, `python tools/rehash_art_checksums.py`, `python tools/validate_art.py`, `npm run assets`; a replaced picture's old file goes to `art/_archive/`.
