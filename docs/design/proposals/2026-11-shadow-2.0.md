# Shadow 2.0: a door you can find, see and play (proposal, full size)

15 Nov 2026, first draft. Status: **design, nothing built**. Every number below is PROVISIONAL and a data field
(`data/shadow.json` unless named); if accepted, the spec (rules 1.32, `docs/design/shadow-spec.md`) changes first, as a
new revision, with its numbers in the next free block of spec items (the lead assigns it). Every item is
`adjudication: engine`. Sister drafts: `2026-11-figments-2.0.md` (origin/wip/design-figments-2) and
`2026-11-bonds-2.0.md` (origin/wip/design-bonds-2); this one reuses their P1, P2, P4 and P8 and adds two pieces.

Leon, verbatim: "For the shadow guys i got a player picking that up to try and don't even know how to go to stealth in
survival mode, he then give up." And: "Check both the tank and shadow for eye candy, if they have no effect, notthing
special and playing them feel like dirt, then add/rework/redraw, do fucking something, save them."

**Diagnosis in three lines.** (1) The rules are mostly there (19 of 21 verbs of rules 1.32 are live), but a level-1
player cannot find the door: Hide sits on the Utility tab like everyone's, and the Shadow tab does not exist until
Ingenuity 3. (2) Almost nothing is drawn: a Hidden token at 60%, a HIDDEN word, the sight chip; a Break, a Fade, a
Decoy, a Plant have no moment at all. (3) The loop is "Hide, wait a turn, Break, be revealed, start again": two real
decisions, no way back to the dark alone below Ingenuity 5, and no choice in how the door grows.

## 1. Where the door stands (checked on origin/wip/feature-0.29 @31e49beb)

| Door | What a player chooses and earns | What it does on a turn |
|---|---|---|
| Sigil (rules 1.4, 1.10) | 40 words, study picks, grand forms | draws, walks a Line, Evokes |
| Outburst (rules 1.8, 1.19) | schools and ways, modules by practice, 25 feelings found | Begins, Pumps, Releases |
| **Shadow today** (rules 1.32) | nothing: the rungs at Ingenuity 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 open their verbs automatically; the choice is only the perk steps (Sleight of Hand, Shadow Step) | Hide (everyone's, 2 AP, Utility tab), a cover move, end the turn, then Break |

**The door's facts.** Perception's door, alpha skill **Ingenuity** (`skills.json ingenuity.unlocks.1`, "Shadow").
The meter is the resource `shadow` (caps `rules.json`: 1 at Ingenuity 1, 2 at 5, 3 at 7, the Operative +1; 0 at the
fight's start, the Operative 1; zeroed at the fight's end and when Downed). Gains (shared cap 2 a turn, key `gain`):
+1 at turn end Hidden with no attack, +1 for a cover-to-cover move unseen, +1 for a kill nobody saw; −1 at turn start
while seen. **Break** is a toggle on the attack card: ×(1 + 0.7 a Shadow + 0.5 ambush + 0.15 Marked), all Shadow
spent, revealed after (×2.2 at cap 1, ×2.9 at cap 2, ×3.6 at cap 3). The **Operative** (`classes.json infiltrator`,
earned at level 3): Tradecraft, Mark (3 at once), Vanish (1 AP, once a turn, out of view), Toxin, Sabotage, Execution,
Called Shot.

**Finding the door today, step by step (a level-1 Survival character with Ingenuity 1 to 2).**
1. The fight opens. Nothing says anything about Hide or Shadow (`tips.tsx` has six generic tips; none is about it).
2. The Shadow tab is absent (`commands.ts` shows it only when it has rows; the first door verb opens at Ingenuity 3).
3. **Hide** is on the **Utility** tab, 2 AP (`actions.json hide`), for every character, door or not
   (`actions.ts:109`). It is refused while Marked, while a Boss stands in the open, or while any enemy that is not
   itself Hidden has eyes on your tile (`tactics.ts hideBlock`). It is **not** cover-gated. The refusal reads "An
   enemy can see you: get out of sight first." No tile on the map shows where "out of sight" is.
4. Break is a toggle on the attack card; it needs Hidden **and** 1 Shadow, so it is greyed on the turn you Hide; the
   first Break comes on the next turn at the earliest.

**Measured** (80 seeded stage-1 Survival first fights, solo, 16×16 maps, 1 or 3 foes, foe vision 12; start of turn 1,
enemies still; a scratch test against this branch, nothing written to the repo):

| | Agility 1 (1 tile/AP) | Agility 4 (2.5 tiles/AP) |
|---|---|---|
| You start in an enemy's eyes (Hide refused at once) | 43 / 80 (54%) | same |
| A reachable tile out of every foe's eyes with **2 AP** left to Hide | 59 / 80 (74%); when seen at start 22 / 43 (51%) | 78 / 80 (98%) |
| The same with Hide at **1 AP** | 66 / 80 (83%); when seen at start 29 / 43 (67%) | 78 / 80 |
| Dark maps | 0 / 80 | |

**Verdict:** going Hidden at level 1 is not impossible, it is **unfound**: in three fights of four a hiding tile is in
reach, but nothing shows it, the button is on the wrong tab, and half the time the first press is refused with no
pointer to where to go. The cheapest data change that makes it practical is the door's own Hide at 1 AP (item 5:
74% to 83%, and 51% to 67% for the player who starts seen), not a change to everyone's vision.

**Effects today.** Exist: the Hidden token at 60% (`tokenFade.ts`), HIDDEN float word and a sound on Hide
(`fx/play.ts`), the sight layer's "!" chip, hide fade and reveal shimmer (`fx/sight.ts`), RIGGED, SABOTAGED and INTENT
words (`fx/classes.ts`), the melee Ambush Opener flash (`melee-techniques.ts`), the Seen / In view / Unseen word on the
resource row (`resources.ts`). None: a Break or gun-ambush flash, Shadow pips on the token, Fade, Decoy, Plant, Lift,
Mark, Vault, Hijack, any overlay of safe tiles, any line from the eyes that found you.

## 6. The solo loop

Alone, a Shadow: moves to a shaded tile (item 2) and Hides for 1 AP (5), First Shade gives the Shadow (6), Tags the
heaviest foe (14) and Breaks it. Revealed, it either kills unseen and sinks back (16), Slips on the first blow that
lands (15, from Ingenuity 3), throws smoke and Hides inside it (17), or Shade Steps away once Hidden again (13). Every
step costs AP or Shadow; the map, not a percentage, is the puzzle.

## 7. Balance

**The line** (shadow-spec section 7): Operative all in 125 (three-turn window, one Break), the door all in 109, the
door at a normal share 87. **Today** (step 11, `test/balance/shadow-measure.test.ts`): door 118 and Operative 125 at
level 1, an upper bound with Ingenuity 7; a real Ingenuity-4 door holds cap 1.

**What moves it:** Go to Ground and First Shade bring the first Break forward a turn (one extra ×2.2 attack in the
window at cap 1: about +20 points for the door at a normal share, estimated, not measured); Ghost Kill and Slip add
re-entries, which are worth AP, not damage, until a second Break. Knife's +0.3 a Shadow is the only new damage term.
**Measure** with rows `shadow_door_2`, `shadow_door_normal_2`, `shadow_operative_2` in `shadow-measure.test.ts` (the
same sequences played through the reducer, a stage-1 map with cover, levels 1/5/10/20) and add `shadow_solo` to
`survival-run.ts`. **Dials:** `ingenuity.unlocks.1` Break `per` 0.7, First Shade's `n`, Knife's 0.3, Shade Step's
`tiles` 3. The guard stays: no Break takes a full-HP Elite or Boss at or above your level under 1 HP. Re-run the
Hide-spot measure (section 1) with the 1 AP Hide as a test: at least 80% of stage-1 first fights must have a spot.

Numbers (all PROVISIONAL, data fields): Hide −1 AP for the door (min 1); First Shade 1, once a fight; Shade Step 1 AP,
1 Shadow, 3 tiles; Tag 1 AP, +2 to hit; Slip 1 Shadow, one reaction a round; Ghost Kill once a turn; Ways: Ghost 5
tiles, Knife +0.3, Hand 2 tiles and −1 AP; practice 6 / 18 / 18.

## 8. Art and effects brief

All pure SVG or CSS in `apps/web/src/game/fx/` (a new `shadow.ts` beside `figments.ts`), paced by the combat speed
setting, off under Reduce motion (the token states then show as a still outline and the word), the acting token moving
first. Ink: Perception `#40BAA0` for the door's own marks, always with a word or glyph.

- **Hide:** the acting token sinks 2 px and its fill fades to a 1.5 px outline over 300 ms; an ellipse (`#000` at 45%,
  1.2 × the token's width) grows under it; the word HIDDEN floats up. Allies' view: the same, outline dashed `3 3`.
- **Wisps (Shadow held):** one 6 px curved stroke per Shadow, fanned under the token; a gain draws a new one in with
  `stroke-dashoffset` (200 ms); a loss frays it (the dash breaks up and fades) with "−1".
- **Break:** the actor lunges 4 px toward the target; a black 3 px line cuts diagonally across the target's tile, then
  a white 1 px line along it (120 ms later); AMBUSH and the term (×2.9) above the target, in white; the wisps on the
  actor are drawn into the cut and vanish; then Found plays on the actor.
- **Found:** three outline strips peel off the token outward and fade; the fill returns; the word FOUND (or HIT /
  CLOSE); with P10 a thin red `#F65157` line from the finder's eye to the token for 500 ms.
- **Shade Step / Fade / Vault:** the token flattens into its shadow ellipse, a dark smear (an 8 px stroke at 30%)
  runs to the new tile, the token rises out of its ellipse there.
- **Tag:** a short hook line from the actor to the foe; a small X in Perception ink stays on the foe's corner; TAGGED.
- **Lift / Cut Strap / Plant:** the hook line, then the word (LIFTED, CUT, PLANTED) at the foe; Lift draws a small
  arc back to the actor's pile.
- **Decoy:** a ring at the sound's tile that ripples twice (two expanding circles); a "?" above each foe that will
  search there.
- **Hide spots and Who sees you:** the shaded tiles at 18% Perception ink with a 1 px inner border; the eye lines 1 px
  `#A3AEC2`, dashed, with the count.

**Icons** (Wick, white flat ink, for `art/briefs/ART-REQUESTS.md`; monoline placeholders until delivered): **Hide**
(Wick crouched behind a crate, his flame turned low); **Shade Step** (Wick stepping into his own shadow on one side and

## 9. Saves

Shadow, Hidden, Tag's `marked` and the reaction live only in a fight; no saved fight state changes. A character keeps
every verb it had (the rungs still open automatically). It gains `c.practice.ingenuity` (P2, empty) and the
`shadow_way` fork pick (P8, empty); a character already past Ingenuity 3 or 7 is owed those picks on its next level-up
card (as the Figments and Bonds drafts). Proof: `test/old-save.test.ts` with an Ingenuity 7 Operative fixture.

## 12. Questions for Leon

1. **Hide at 1 AP for the door only, or for everyone?** `actions.json hide.ap` is 2 and provisional. Recommended: the
   door only (item 5), so Hide stays a real price for the others and the door feels like the stealth door.
2. **First Shade:** a free Shadow on the first Hide of each fight puts a Break on turn 1. Recommended: yes, once a
   fight; the alternative is every Hide, which roughly doubles the Breaks and needs Break's 0.7 cut.
3. **Survival maps are never dark** (0 of 80 stage-1 fights): Douse, darkness halving sight and dark sight do
   nothing early. Should `survival.json` roll some dark maps (a field on the map settings)? Recommended: yes, from
   stage 2.
4. **Ways on top of the automatic rungs, or instead?** Recommended: on top (every save keeps its verbs); the
   alternative makes the rung verbs part of the Ways and needs a migration that takes nothing away.
