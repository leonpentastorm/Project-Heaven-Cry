### 2.2 What the gun measure cannot show

1. **One target.** The range has one enemy. Area guns (breacher radius 1, thumper and the frag rounds radius 2, the
   flamer's cone) get no credit for a second or third body. On the cluster (2.4 at radius 1, 2.8 at radius 2:
   `balance.yardsticks.area.cluster`) the breacher would read about 19% × 1.7 = 32%, the thumper about 37% × 1.9 =
   70%: still under. Estimates, not measured.
2. **The walk in.** The target stands 13 tiles off. Every gun with a range under 13 (all pistols, SMGs and shotguns,
   the flamer at 3) spends AP walking in on turn 1. That tax is real in a fight, but a short-range gun's trade is
   meant to be paid back by cover and corners the range does not have.
3. **Nobody shoots back.** Range, cover and exposure are free here. A long rifle's range is worth nothing on the range
   and a lot in a real fight; a shotgun's exposure costs nothing here and a lot there.
4. **A rifle build's hands.** Every gun gets the Marksman's rifle techniques (`GUN_TECHNIQUES`): pistols, shotguns
   and heavies are measured in hands not built for them.
5. **The free hand.** A one-handed gun leaves a hand free (a second gun, a focus, a shield). Only the hand cannon
   was paired.
6. **Recoil barely bites.** The build's Dexterity cancels floor(Dexterity ÷ 2) points, so its plain shots rarely pay
   recoil. A per-weapon recoil number (section 3) would need a low-Dexterity row to show at all.
7. **Masterwork.** The loop measures at Masterwork (the contract's reading for the best gun). Grade buys accuracy
   only, so the "vs rifle" column is little changed by it; not re-run at standard.

## 3. The levers

### 3.1 Today's levers, where they live, who can see them

"Card" = the shop card and loadout card (`gearPreview` lines, the first four or five); "compare" = the shop's compare
panel (every `gearPreview` line, both columns); "sheet" = the weapon line on the character sheet's GEAR tab
(`apps/web/src/screens/main.js`); "fight" = the attack's hover card and to-hit breakdown.

| Lever | Where in data | Card | Compare | Sheet | Fight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | `weapons.json` `damage` (a number, a falloff table, or a Raw Power formula) | yes (per hit, after armour, at your level) | yes | yes (raw) | yes |
| To hit | the character, `accuracy` on the weapon, `rarity` acc | yes (vs a level Soldier) | yes | no | yes (every term) |
| AP a shot / a Burst / a strike | `ap_cost` | yes | yes | no | yes (cost chip) |
| Magazine | `magazine` (and `mod_extended_mag`) | yes | yes | yes (loaded/held) | yes (meter) |
| Range | `range_tiles` | 5th line | yes | yes | yes (the range bands) |
| Heat limit | `heat_limit` (stat `heat_limit`) | **no** | **no** | no | yes (meter) |
| Armour penetration | a property (`armor_ignore_5`, `armor_ignore_10`) or op `ignore_armour` | only as a trait chip | trait chip | trait list | yes (in the damage) |
| Recoil | **no field**: one global rule (combat.ts), flag `no_recoil` on four bows, `mod_compensator` | trait chip "No recoil" only | no | no | **only** as the "recoil" term in the to-hit breakdown |
| Durability | `durability`, `rarity` | yes | yes | yes (wear) | no |
| Reload | `reload`, the `reload` cost site | no | no | no | yes (cost) |
| Hands | `hands` | no | no | no | no |
| Weight | `weight_kg` | no (weapons) | no | no | no |

**Reading.** A player who compares two guns sees damage, to-hit, AP, magazine and range. Heat, recoil and armour
penetration, three of the levers that actually separate an SMG from a rifle, are invisible or a chip of jargon.

### 3.2 Recoil as a per-weapon number (needs a rules revision)

**Today (rules spec item 88, line 589):** each shot after the first in a turn is −1 to hit, cumulative, less
floor(Dexterity ÷ 2); halved braced or Emplaced; none in a volley, a reaction, full auto; none for bows. The engine
has it as one literal (`packages/engine/src/combat.ts`, the `recoil` line). Two defects found on the way:

- **The compensator disagrees with itself.** Its card says "Recoil −1 per shot"; the engine zeroes recoil entirely
  with it; the spec says both (line 707 "−1 per shot", line 2327 "removes it").
- **Two data levers do nothing.** The Emplaced condition's `stat_mult` on recoil and the Both Hands perk's
  `recoil_mult` are read nowhere; the code tests the condition and a hook flag directly.

**The proposal (rules 1.5x, "Recoil 2.0"):**

1. **The rule in words:** "Each shot after your first this turn is harder to hit with: your gun's **Recoil** for each
   earlier shot. Your Dexterity steadies floor(Dexterity ÷ 2) of it." Braced or Emplaced halves it, as now; volleys,
   reactions and full auto, as now.
2. **The data field:** `weapons.json` `recoil`, a whole number a shot, default 1. Bows 0 (the `no_recoil` property
   retires into it). Proposed by family, for the fit to confirm: pistols, SMGs, rifles, carbines 1; the long rifle,
   the hand cannon, shotguns, the battle rifle, heavies unbraced 2; bows, the core rifle 0.
3. **The compensator** becomes "Recoil −1" on its weapon, not "no recoil" (one reading for card, spec and engine).
4. **What shows where:** a `recoil` line in `gearPreview` ("Recoil: −1 a shot after the first", better lower) so the
   shop card's fifth line, the compare panel, the loadout card and the reward pick all show it with no client code;
   the sheet's weapon line adds "recoil 2"; the fight keeps the to-hit term and the shot's hover card names it.
5. **Measure:** the gun loop needs a low-Dexterity row (a normal share at Dexterity 3), or recoil never shows.

**Built from**

| Rule | Carried by |
| --- | --- |
| The penalty, its Dexterity relief, braced and Emplaced halving | the existing recoil line in `combat.ts` (one formula, the literal 1 replaced by the field) |
| No recoil on bows | the field at 0; the bows' no-recoil property (`data/weapons.json` `properties`) retired by the validator |
| Volleys, reactions, auto: none | unchanged (`previewAttack` `noRecoil`) |
| Card, compare, loadout, pick | `gearPreview` lines (`gearpreview.ts`): the client draws whatever lines come |
| The fight's breakdown | the to-hit terms list, unchanged |
| A variant that kicks less | the variant's field change (section 4) |

**Needs a new primitive** (for the lead to approve): `recoil` (a new field on a weapon row, read by the one recoil
formula); a `recoil` line in `gearPreview`; the compensator read as a number instead of `hasMod`.

## 4. Variants

### 4.1 What a variant is

**A variant is a named item: a base item plus one or two modifiers.** "Hellraiser 'Long Belly': holds 8 more rounds,
kicks less." A modifier is one entry from a **short closed list of kinds**; each kind is one sentence a player can
read, and each maps to a piece the engine already runs. A designer adds a variant by writing one line.

**What it must NOT become:** random rolls, affix tables, prefix and suffix pools, item level, a variant with three or
more modifiers, numbers a player must compare across six lines, or a modifier kind that needs code for one item.
Loot may **pick** a variant from a hand-written list; it never **makes** one.

### 4.2 The closed list (12 kinds)

| # | Kind | The sentence | Carried by | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Harder** | "Lands 10% harder." | op `damage_mult` on `dealing`, predicate `with_this` (as `prop.keen`, `prop.biting`) | exact |
| 2 | **True** | "+1 to hit." (a focus: "and the saves it forces are 1 harder") | op `roll_mod` on `to_hit`, and on `save` with roll "tn" (as `prop.true`, `prop.deep`) | exact |
| 3 | **Quick hands** | "Reloading costs 1 AP less." / "Swapping a core costs 1 AP less." / "Your first Vent a turn is free." | op `cost_mod` on `reload`, `swap`, or the cost sites `swap_core`, `vent` (as `prop.quick`, `prop.balanced`) | exact |
| 4 | **Rider** | "A hit makes the target Bleed." / "A hit pushes 1 tile." | op `condition` or `displace` on `hit`, with `with_this`; the riders a short list in data | exact |
| 5 | **Kind** | "Its heals and shields are 15% stronger." | op `mend_mult` on `heal` and `shield` (as `prop.gentle`) | exact |
| 6 | **Deep magazine** | "Holds 8 more rounds." | the variant's own `magazine` field | with a data field |
| 7 | **Long reach** | "Reaches 2 tiles further." | the variant's own `range_tiles` field | with a data field |
| 8 | **Runs cool** | "Takes 2 more Heat before it must vent." | the variant's own `heat_limit` field | with a data field |
| 9 | **Kicks less** | "Kicks less: Recoil 1 lower." | the variant's own recoil field | needs the recoil field (section 3.2) |
| 10 | **Holds more Flux** | "Holds 2 more Flux before it overloads." | the Flux limit: today a literal in code (`FLUX_MAX` 6) beside `rules.json sigil.flux.safe_max` 6 | needs a general addition |
| 11 | **Deep cell** | "Each core gives 2 more charge." | the core's charge: a literal (`CORE_CHARGE` 10) beside `rules.json cores.standard.charge` | needs a general addition |
| 12 | **Steady heart** | "Your Strain limit is 1 higher while you hold it." | stat `strain_limit` | needs a general addition (a stat answer only while in hand) |

**Held back** (candidates for a 13th, not proposed): "its glyphs cost 1 less charge" (`cost_mod` on `glyph_cost`,
exact), "more armour while held" (stat `armor`), "+damage on a crit" (stat `crit_damage`). The list stays short on
purpose: every kind is a line on the card and a cell in the measure.

**Why kinds 6 to 9 are "fields".** A variant is its own row (its own id, name, price and art fallback), made at load
from the base row plus its field changes. Everything that reads `magazine`, `range_tiles`, `heat_limit` or the recoil
field already reads the variant's row: the card, the compare panel, the fight, the measure. No op is needed.

**Why 10 to 12 need an addition.** The Flux limit and a core's charge are typed in code twice (a copy in `magic.ts`
and a copy in `rules.json`): a defect under the "one copy" rule even before variants. A stat answered by an item
counts wherever the item is carried, including the pack (`statQuery` reads every held item, with no hand test), so
"while you hold it" needs the stat answer to honour `with_this`.

### 4.3 The data shape

One line per variant, in a mode-neutral file (proposed `data/variants.json`; not Survival's):

```json
{ "id": "hellraiser_service_rifle.long_belly", "name": "Hellraiser 'Long Belly'", "base": "hellraiser_service_rifle",
  "mods": [{ "kind": "deep_magazine", "value": 8 }, { "kind": "kicks_less", "value": 1 }], "provisional": true }
```

The kinds themselves are a table in the same file: each kind's sentence (a `{=value}` text), its carrier (a field
and how it changes, or an effect template with `with_this`), its `worth` per unit in percent of a rifle-turn (as
`relic_props` carry `worth` today), and what it `fits` (guns, magazine guns, melee, foci, one door).

**Price:** the base price × (1 + worth ÷ 100), through `priceOf`: no second formula.

**The validator** refuses: a third modifier, an unknown kind, a kind that does not fit the base, two of one kind, a
total worth over a cap (proposed 20% of a rifle-turn for a hand-made variant).

### 4.4 How it shows, with no code per variant

| Where | What the player sees | Carried by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The card and loadout | the name, the base's lines with the changed number already in them, then one line per modifier, its sentence | `gearPreview` (the field kinds change the numbers; the effect kinds add their text) |
| The shop's compare panel | the changed numbers marked better or worse against what you hold | `compareGear` rows, unchanged |
| The fight | the numbers in the to-hit and damage breakdown, the modifier's name on its term (an op's `label`) | the item ops' `label` / `say` fields |
| The balance measure | the variant's id in the gun or melee loop like any weapon | `GUNS_MEASURE_WEAPONS` or `MELEE_MEASURE_WEAPONS` with its id |
| Loot and the roguelike | a list of variant ids, picked by weight | the loot tables' ids, unchanged |
| Art | the base's icon; a variant may name its own later | `art/catalog.json` fallback to the base id (to confirm) |

## 6. Casters in Survival

**Why the bot's numbers mislead** (Leon, 14 Nov; tested live on 0.26.0: a normal FIRE-SEEK hit is about 93):

1. **Its armour.** The test build wore the lightest armour and took 14 damage a round against the gunner's 2.5.
2. **Its play.** It walks off its glyphs, abandons about a quarter of them, never uses area words.
3. **Its count.** The measure counts HP actually removed: a 93 hit on a 15 HP rat counts 15. One big hit loses far
   more to overkill than several small shots.

**The fixes, in order, before any caster is judged:**

1. **Area words.** The Sigil policy (`policies.ts`, `run-policies.ts sigilRun`) plays only single-target damage Lines
   with SEEK (`NOT_PLAYED` says so). Teach it SPREAD, ARC, CHAIN when two or more enemies are in the shape (the
   cluster yardstick says what a shape catches).
2. **Stay on the glyphs.** Evoke from within 1 tile of the Line before moving; never walk away from drawn glyphs with
   charge left; count abandoned glyphs in the run's report.
3. **Armour in its build.** The Survival bot's caster buys armour with the camp budget as a player would (Class 2 or
   3 if Pain Threshold allows), as the gunner does.
4. **Count overkill apart.** `sim.ts` keeps `dmg` (uncapped) beside `hpRemoved`; `run/tally.ts` adds uncapped damage.
   Every report used for casters prints both, and the overkill share.

**Only then the question that remains:** does a **level-1 Sigilist** have a good answer to **several weak enemies at
once**? Measure: a level-1 Sigilist (Brain Power 1 to 3) against 3 to 5 vermin or minions, the area words in hand,
overkill counted apart, against the level-1 gunner on the same fights. **Measure on 0.28.0 first**: slower XP keeps a
Sigilist at Brain Power 1 to 3 for the whole first stage, which makes this question bigger, not smaller.
