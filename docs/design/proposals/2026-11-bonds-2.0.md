# Bonds 2.0: a thread you play every turn (proposal, full size)

15 Nov 2026, first draft. Status: **design, nothing built**. Every number below is PROVISIONAL and a data field
(`data/tending.json` unless named); if accepted, the spec (rules 1.33) changes first, as a new revision, with its
numbers as spec items 2200 to 2249. Every item is `adjudication: engine`. Sister draft: `2026-11-figments-2.0.md`
(origin/wip/design-figments-2); this one reuses its P1 to P3 and adds as few pieces as it can.

Leon, playtest 15 Nov 2026, verbatim: "anything to spice up that class aside from some funny big number when you link
more bond ?" and "bond also have no selfish option which will make it unsuable for solo play i put in enemy bond on
purpose cause of this".

The lead's direction: a Bond is a **thread between two bodies**; the fun is what you do with a thread, not how many you
hold. Every addition below is an order with a cost, a choice of kind, or a positional consequence; none is a passive
percentage. The Mender stays a healer first; the threads are its teeth.

## 2. The thread's kinds (picked each time you Bond)

A Bond is made in one **kind** you know, picked on the Bond action (the `pick` of `tend_bond` and `tend_bond_free`, as
Inspire picks a feeling; read with the predicate `choice`). The kind is a condition on the far end (`thread_spite`,
`thread_leash`; Mercy is the plain Bond and carries none), so every rule below is a target-side predicate
(`target_has`, `actor_has`, `target_lacks`). A unit bears one kind: a second Bonder's kind replaces it. When `bonded`
ends, its kind ends with it (trigger `cond_ended`, `if: {cond: [bonded]}`, op `cleanse` of the two kind rows).

| # | Kind | Rule a player reads | Data | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mercy** (Empathy 1; both sides) | Today's Bond: Tending through it ×1.2 at any range; on an enemy, your heals echo into it. | today's `tend.threads` rows; the through rows gain `target_lacks: [thread_spite, thread_leash]` | `mend_mult`, `deal` on `heal_given` with `delay`, `target_lacks` |
| 2 | **Spite** (fork A; enemies only) | Harder to tie (its save +2); +2 more to hit it, and each blow it lands on you hurts it a quarter back. | Bond's `condition bonded` row with `if: {choice: [spite]}` and `roll_mod {when: cond_incoming, value: 2}`; `thread_spite` row; `tend.threads` gains `roll_mod {when: to_hit, value: 2, if: {target_has: [thread_spite]}}` and `deal {when: damaged, if: {target: self, actor: foe, actor_has: [thread_spite]}, amount: spite, target: actor}` (`amounts.spite`: `event:amount` × 0.25, soul, armour none) | `choice`, `roll_mod` (`cond_incoming`, `to_hit`), trigger `damaged`, op `deal` (`target: actor`) |
| 3 | **Leash** (fork A; both sides) | An enemy: after each move of its own it is dragged 1 tile back toward you (once a round). An ally: one free Tug a turn. | `thread_leash` row; `displace {when: moved, if: {actor: foe, actor_has: [thread_leash], actor_tied: bond}, how: pull, tiles: 1, target: actor, limit: {per: per_round, n: 1, each: actor}}`; Tug's `cost_mod {value: -1, min: 0, if: {action: [tend_tug], target_has: [thread_leash]}, limit: {per: per_turn, n: 1}}` | trigger `moved`, op `displace` (`pull`), op `cost_mod`, `limit` (`each`) |

## 3. Orders along a thread (the turn)

Each is one `grant_action` row in `tending.json` (the shape of `tend.guide`: `ap`, `res.mp`, `if.self_holds {slot:
hand, door: empathy}`, `reach` with `beyond: "bond"`, nested `effects`). The Attuner stays required for every one.

| # | Order | Rule a player reads | Data (sketch) | Built from | Builds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Tug** (E1; 1 AP, 4 MP) | Pull a Bonded unit 2 tiles toward you; an enemy saves (Body), an ally comes willing (3 tiles). | reach `{sight: true, side: any, tied: bond}`; enemy: `condition reeled {via: empathy, family body}` then `displace {how: pull, tiles: 2, if: {target_has: [reeled]}}` (Bond's own save-then-act pattern); ally: `displace {how: pull, tiles: 3}`; a Boss moves 1 | op `displace` (`pull`), op `condition` (`via`), forced movement (a hazard tile works as for any pull) | yes |
| 5 | **Snap** (E1; 1 AP, 0 MP, once a turn) | Break a Bond on purpose: an enemy takes a jolt and is Dazed (Heart save); an ally is healed. | enemy: `deal {amount: snap}`, `condition dazed {rounds: 1, via: empathy}`; ally: `mend {amount: snap_heal}`; then `tie {kind: bond, clear: target}`; `amounts.snap` base 3.0 and `snap_heal` base 3.0 on the `tend` profile | op `deal`, op `mend`, op `condition`, op `tie` (`clear`), `limit` | yes |
| 6 | **Snap, the Mender's** (Mender rank I) | Your Snap also empties the Ledger into it: half its worth, no roll. | the Mender's Snap amounts gain `per: [{of: "res:ledger", value: 0.5}]`; then `resource {resource: ledger, set: 0}` | number block `res:ledger`, op `resource` | yes |
| 7 | **Draw Taut** (E4; 1 AP, 6 MP) | Pull one thread tight: enemies standing on it are cut and Slowed; allies on it are mended. | reach: a unit tied to you; `deal {amount: taut, targets: {of: self, shape: between, side: foe}}` + `condition slowed {rounds: 1, via: empathy}`; `mend {amount: lane, targets: {of: self, shape: between, side: ally}}`; `taut` 2.0, `lane` 1.5 | op `deal`, `mend`, `condition` with `targets`; **P5** (`shape: between`) | P5 |
| 8 | **Take It On** (fork B; 1 AP, 5 MP) | The newest harm on a Bonded ally moves onto you, rounds and all. | `cleanse {newest: true, count: 1, onto: self, target: target}` | op `cleanse`; **P7** (`onto`) | P7 |
| 9 | **Pass It On** (fork B; 1 AP, 5 MP) | Your newest Body harm (Bleeding, Burning…) moves into a Bonded enemy, no save. | `cleanse {newest: true, count: 1, family: body, onto: target, target: self}` | op `cleanse`; **P7** | P7 |
| 10 | **Forewarn** (fork B; reaction, 4 MP) | A Bonded enemy swings at a Bonded ally: shield the ally before the blow lands. | `mend {when: attack_roll, if: {actor: foe, actor_tied: bond, target: ally, target_tied: bond}, amount: forewarn, layer: base, limit: {reaction: 1, kind: protect}}`; `forewarn` 2.5 | trigger `attack_roll`, op `mend` (shield), reaction `limit` | yes |
| 11 | **Knot** (fork C; 2 AP, 10 MP; two enemy Bonds) | Until your next turn, a blow on one of your Bonded enemies hurts each other one for 30%. | self gets `knotting` (1 round); `deal {when: damaged, if: {self_has: [knotting], target: foe, target_tied: bond, by_hit: true}, amount: knot, targets: {of: self, tied: bond, side: foe, not: target}}`; `knot` = `event:amount` × 0.3, soul; a knot share is `shared` and never knots again | trigger `damaged`, `deal` with `targets`, `DamageSrc.shared`; **P6** (`not`) | P6 |

Two of these answer the lead's seeds that were cut or reshaped: **a tether between two enemies** became Knot (the Mender
is the hub, so it rides today's ties; a tie between two units neither of whom is the holder would need a second end on
the `tie` op, and Knot gives the same play); **strain by distance** is cut (no predicate measures a unit beyond N tiles;
Leash's drag gives the positional pressure instead).

## 7. The solo loop (Leon's enemy Bond, kept and made better)

Alone, a Mender: Bonds the most dangerous foe for free (Spite to duel it, Mercy to echo, Leash to keep it out of
cover); Heartbeat shows whom it will hit; it Mends itself (the echo and the Ledger fill), Passes its Bleeding on,
Tugs the foe out of cover or onto a burning tile, and Snaps the thread for the Ledger with no roll; next turn the free
Bond ties again. Against a healer line it Bonds the medic and Steals the Mending (`survival.json healer_line`). A foe
that falls passes the thread on. Every step is a decision with an AP or MP price; none is a percentage.

## 8. Balance

Snap, Draw Taut and Knot are the teeth that replace the passive `per_bond` (question 1). Every new amount block
(`snap`, `snap_heal`, `taut`, `lane`, `spite`, `knot`, `forewarn`, `stolen`) rides the `tend` profile and is fitted with
`test/balance/tending-measure.ts` (the healing turn against `balance.ceilings.tending_turn`, the offence against
`mender_offence` 70, the Ledger window against `mender_window` 100) and the healer-stall measure (`DEFENCE_PARTS=fire`).
The Mender and a solo Mender get rows in `survival-run.ts` (`mender`, `solo_mender`), missing from the 0.29 baseline.

## 9. Saves

Ties live only in a fight, so no saved Bond changes; a Bond with no kind condition is Mercy, today's Bond. A
character keeps everything it had. It gains `c.known.thread_tricks` (P1) and `c.practice.empathy` (P2), both empty; a
Mender or door user who has passed Empathy 3, 5 or 7 is owed those fork picks on its next level-up card (as the
Figments draft's Riggers). Proof: `test/old-save.test.ts` with a fixture of an Empathy 7 Mender.

## 10. Needs a new primitive (general pieces only; each the lead's to approve)

Reused from the Figments draft: **P1** (one `unlock` op over named lists), **P2** (one practice ledger by door),
**P3** (the seat; here it also takes a tied ally). New:

5. **P5, `TargetSet.shape: "between"`:** the units on the tiles strictly between `of` and the event's other end
   (`tilesLine`, ends excluded). Draw Taut; later any thread or beam that is a line between two bodies.
6. **P6, `TargetSet.not: "target" | "actor"`:** leave the event's unit out of the set (`unitsIn` already takes `not`).
   Knot; any "the others" rule.
7. **P7, `cleanse.onto: "self" | "target" | "actor"`:** the conditions it ends are put on that unit with the rounds
   they had left, no save (`applyCond`). Take It On, Pass It On.
8. **P8, one fork action for every door** (`door/fork {door, fork, pick}`): the Figments draft's `figments/fork` and this
   door's are the same piece; build it once.

No new trigger, predicate, resource, stat or cost site: the orders are `grant_action` rows that price themselves, and
the kinds are condition rows (`thread_spite`, `thread_leash`, `reeled`, `knotting`: data).

## 12. Art

**The thread on the map (the most important):** it already exists (`rules.json ties.kinds.bond.line`: Insight ink
`#EB95B7`, dashed `5 4` to an enemy; `screenViews.ts tieLines`). Bonds 2.0 adds, all SVG on the map layer: Mercy as
today; **Spite** a barbed line (`stroke-dasharray` "2 3" with a small tick `marker` every 3 tiles); **Leash** a double
line (two 1 px strokes 3 px apart). **Taut:** the line goes from 1.5 px to 3 px for 400 ms, and the tiles between
(P5's) light in the ink at 25%. **Snap:** the line parts at the middle and each half retracts to its end
(`stroke-dashoffset`), a 12 px ring at the far end. **Echo and Ledger:** a 4 px dot runs along the line (`animateMotion`)
when a heal echoes or a Snap empties the Ledger. **Knot:** a curved arc between the two knotted enemies through the
Mender's tile, gone at its turn start. Each kind's look is a field of the kind's data row (`line.kinds`), not code.

**Icons** (white-ink Wick mode, `art/briefs/ART-REQUESTS.md`; monoline placeholders until delivered): Tug, Snap, Draw
Taut, Take It On, Pass It On, Forewarn, Knot, Last Thread, Steal the Mending, Pass the Thread, Weaver's Hands, and the
three kinds (Mercy, Spite, Leash).

## 13. Questions for Leon

1. **Retire `per_bond`?** It is the "funny big number" (each Bond held makes your blows bigger) and the Mender's
   steady-offence dial (68 / 71 against 70). Recommended: retire it in slice 3 and fit Snap, Draw Taut and Knot to carry
   that offence instead.
2. **Kinds per Bond or per character?** Recommended: picked at every Bond from the kinds you know (a choice every
   time); the other way, a Mender is Spite or Leash for good once it takes the fork.
3. **Pass It On** gives your own wounds to a foe. Is a healer's glove passing on its Bleeding in tone for the
   Attuner, or should it wait for a faction's Attuner?
4. **Enemy Menders** (step 9): should a medic foe Tug and Snap the players back, or do enemies stay without threads?
