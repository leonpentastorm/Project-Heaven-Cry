# The curve: what growing stronger means in PHC (the lead's design, 21 Nov 2026)

For Leon to tear into. Nothing here is built. The ceilings are being deleted separately (rules 1.94). Facts about today come from checked maps of the code (`docs/design/proposals/2026-11-21-maps/`: each `map-*` is the first reading, each `check-*` corrects and completes it); every proposal number is the lead's and is a starting point to play, not a line.

## 1. What is wrong today, in facts

1. **Levels cancel.** A character's damage rises x7.5 by level 20 and an enemy's HP x8.5. The enemy table was fitted so a Soldier takes about 4 hits from a plain rifle at every level. Levelling changes nothing a player sees.
2. **Skill points add no damage.** Raw Power's card says +5% physical damage a point; on every melee weapon the engine adds 0. Dexterity adds 0 to gun damage. A skill point is +1 to hit.
3. **Attribute points add a sliver.** Strength's card says +10% physical damage; a point gives about +3.8% of a hit. Agility gives guns about +2.5%. Intelligence, Expression and Determination add **nothing** to Sigil, elemental or Soul damage: their cards say +10%, +10% and +5%.
4. **A specialist is defined as 1.25 times a generalist**, by a line in the data.
5. **Grades add no damage.** Fine is +1 to hit, Masterwork +2. A Relic is a Masterwork with one property at three times the price. A Celestial is a Relic with a bigger price.
6. **Perks that raise damage:** 10 of 90, all conditional, +5 to 8% at tier I, +12 to 15% at tier II.
7. **Bonuses against a target share one cap** of +100%; criticals are capped at x2.
8. **A mod** is +1 to hit in one mode, or no number.
9. Inside a weapon family damage barely follows price (rifles 27 to 38 for 650 to 2,100 credits).

So the systems exist and almost none of them moves a number the player would notice. That is what the ceilings bought.

## 2. What each system is FOR

| System | What the player should feel | Where its power lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level** | "I last longer and I have more to spend." | HP and points. Not damage. |
| **Attribute** | "This is what I am." A 7 in Strength is a different animal from a 1. | The size of everything in its domain, as its card already says. |
| **Skill** | "This is what I trained." | Hitting with it, its door's steps, and its card's own damage line. |
| **Perk** | "This changes how I play." | A new verb, or a big pay-off for doing the build's thing. Never a small tax rebate. |
| **Class rank** | "Nobody else can do this." | Moves that beat a plain attack when their set-up is met. |
| **Weapon** | "This one suits me." | Its role: range, hands, speed, magazine, what it is good at. A sidegrade inside its family. |
| **Grade** | "I found a better one." The loot chase. | The weapon itself hits harder and holds more. |
| **Mod** | "I tuned it." | One visible trade each. |
| **Boon, training, shrine** | "This run is about this." | Run-defining, bigger than a perk tier. |
| **Enemies** | "The stage got meaner, and I answered." | Numbers, kinds, kits and levels by stage. They do not shadow every step the player takes. |

Two rules follow. **Power comes from choices, not from the level number.** And **a reward that cannot be felt is not a reward**: a step should move its number by a tenth or open something to do.

## 3. The proposals

### 3.1 Make the cards true (attributes and skills)

The cards were the design. The engine flattened them. Proposal: the engine does what the card says.

| Point | Card says | Engine today | Proposal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strength | +10% physical damage | about +3.8% on Strength weapons | +10% on melee, thrown and hurled |
| Raw Power | +5% physical damage | 0 on melee weapons | +5% with heavy melee and bare hands |
| Intelligence | +10% Sigil power | 0 | +10% |
| Expression | +10% elemental | 0 | +10% |
| Determination | +5% Soul | 0 | +5% |
| Insight | +6% healing given | built | as it is |
| Agility | movement, Initiative | also a hidden +2.5% on guns | **guns stop reading Agility** (the card never said it) |
| Dexterity | +1 to hit, +2% critical damage | as the card | add **+4% damage with guns, light blades and thrown**, and say it on the card |
| Perception | +3% critical damage | capped at x2 | uncapped |

What that does to a build: a Strength 7 / Raw Power 7 Breaker hits **about twice** as hard with a maul as a Strength 1 / Raw Power 1 character swinging the same maul (1.6 x 1.35), where today the gap is about a third. That is the 7/1/1 archetype meaning what it says. A gunner's damage comes from Dexterity (training) and from the gun (gear): +24% at Dexterity 7, and the rest is the loot chase below. Guns keep range and safety, which is why their slope is the gentlest.

### 3.2 Grades are the loot chase

| Grade | To hit | Damage | Durability | Mod slots | Also |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | +0 | x1 | x1 | the row's | |
| Fine | +1 | x1.10 | x1.25 | the row's | |
| Masterwork | +2 | x1.20 | x1.5 | +1 | |
| Relic | +3 | x1.30 | x2 | +1 | one Relic property |
| Celestial | +4 | x1.40 | x2.5 | +2 | two Relic properties |

Armour: the same steps on its armour value (x1 / 1.10 / 1.20 / 1.30 / 1.40). Prices stay x1 / 3 / 8 / 25 / 50: now they buy something.

### 3.3 Perks pay for the build's thing

A conditional perk's condition is the gameplay (keep Guard up, stay Hidden, hit a Bonded foe). Meeting it should be worth it:

- The ten damage perks: tier I **+15%**, tier II **+30%** while the condition holds (today +5 to 8% and +12 to 15%). The per-stack ones (Guard held, Figments kept) scale to the same tops.
- Bonuses against a target are no longer pooled under one cap: each does what its card says.
- A perk that only gives a small number with no condition is a candidate to become a verb. That is a list to go through with you, not a sweep.

### 3.4 A class move beats a plain attack

A move that costs AP and a resource and does less than a plain attack is a button nobody presses (Slam: 2 AP and 2 Guard for no damage, beside Shield Bash at half the price and 30 to 44 damage). Rule of thumb for a first pass: **a move whose set-up is met is worth about one and a half plain attacks of the same AP**, in damage or in what it denies. The class sheet (`2026-11-20-class-moves-sheet.md`) is the list; Slam gets its damage first.

### 3.5 Weapons: role, not rank

Inside a family the three forms differ by what they are for. Price buys options (mod slots, magazine, handling), not raw damage; raw damage comes from the grade. The 72 new rows stay at their designed numbers. A weapon's number changes only when one row is simply better than another at the same price, or cannot be used.

### 3.6 Enemies answer by stage

With 3.1 to 3.3 a built character is far stronger than today, and that is the point. The challenge comes back through Survival's stages, which already have the dials: how many, which kinds, their kits, their levels (`survival.json stage`). Intent, to be read from simulated runs and from play, never fitted to a percentage:

- **Stage 1 teaches.** A squad with any sensible build clears it most of the time.
- **Stage 2 tests the build.** A squad that invested clears it; a squad that spread thin struggles.
- **Stage 3 and on need the squad to work together.** How far you get is the score.

When a stage reads too easy or too hard, **the stage changes** (enemy numbers, kinds, levels). The player's rewards are not taken back.

## 4. The order I would build it in (on the unreleased 0.34 branch)

1. Grades (3.2): one data table and its reader.
2. The cards made true (3.1): the damage profiles' slopes and the card texts.
3. The ten perks and the target-bonus cap (3.3).
4. Slam (3.4), then the class sheet with you.
5. Read Survival runs by stage (clears at stages 1, 2 and 3 for each class, solo and four) and move the **stages**.

Each step re-records the damage tables on purpose and is one data change to undo.

## 5. What I need from you

1. **Is this the direction?** Cards made true, grades with damage, perks that pay, enemies answering by stage.
2. **Levels.** Today a level's damage and an enemy's HP rise together and cancel. I would leave that tide alone (it keeps fights the same length as numbers grow) and put all the felt growth in points, perks and gear. The other way is to let levelling itself out-run same-level enemies. Which do you want?
3. **Guns.** Is Dexterity +4% a point, with no Agility on gun damage, the gunner you mean?
