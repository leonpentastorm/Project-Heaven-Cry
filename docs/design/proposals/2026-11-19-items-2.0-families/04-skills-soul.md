# Design notes moved out of the artist file 04-skills-soul.md (19 Nov 2026)

Rules text and decisions removed from the artist file. Sources: attribute-drafts/determination.md, insight.md, virtue.md.

## What each picture stands for

- Two Schools (Will Power 5 verb): a design carries a second school at half strength, +10 MP.
- Poker Face (Composure): -3 to anyone questioning you or reading your lies; tier II +2 on Brain saves. Stand Still: until you first act foes are -2 to hit you, the first foe to miss gives you a free attack. Pure Heart (Composure, moved from Will Power 5): once a fight, the Strain that would Mana Short you is refused and your Strain falls to your limit; tier II twice a fight. The picture was redrawn to show that overflow being refused (the old draft text about reading ancient script and an upper-realm parley is not in the sheet).
- Warding Word (Lore Master `lore_ward`): 1 AP, an ally within reach gets +3 on their next save against a supernatural effect. Recitation: once a turn a Call you make calms 1 Strain (2 at tier II).
- Blood Price (Determination fork two): each time your heart burns you, your next Release does x1.25 damage (PROVISIONAL).
- Hand-Off: once a round a friend beside you who is attacked uses your Defense if higher (tier II reaches 3 tiles). Nobody Left Behind: Stabilise costs 1 AP less, a Downed friend beside you takes 50% less damage; tier II adds Raise. Steady Hands: Settle (+2 on saves) and Soothe (a harmful condition comes off). Pull Together: Tug and Guide. Quiet Thread: you are never Traced (tier II also your Bonded friends). Veiled Insult: 0 AP, once a fight, a foe makes a Heart save or is Taunted for 2 rounds (twice at tier II).
- Swift Psalm (`fork.prayer.held_breath`, id kept, renamed): Hymn wait cut by 1 round (2 at least). Righteous: +20% prayer heals and shields on an ally at half HP or less (+40% tier II). Foresight: once a fight the first blow that would drop an ally within 4 tiles leaves them on 1 HP. Five Stars: +1 AP for the squad in the first round; tier II shakes rank-and-file foes. Commanding Voice: +3 on saves against Silenced, Shaken and Fear; tier II immune to Silenced.
- Blessed Aim (condition `blessed_aim`, a copy of Canticle Rising: +1 to hit for 2 rounds, from the Benediction fork, for you and allies within 2 tiles).
