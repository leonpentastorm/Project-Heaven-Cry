## The four Visualization forks, as ruled (section 5b)

The list has rows for these four (proposed ids). The rulings of proposals section 5b changed what they do, so the prompts below follow 5b and replace the list's one-liners where those differ: Hard Light is now "Figments take -30% damage and +1 AP next turn when one is destroyed" (the list says "they do not snap"); Sharp Light is "+20% Figment strike damage, and after a Figment drops a foe your next Figment order costs 0 AP"; Many Hands is "+1 Figment held, Picture costs 1 less charge"; Masterwork is "+2 to hit and -25% damage on your named Figment, once a round one order to it costs 0 AP" (the list's "+1 trait" is not built). The four must read apart at 32 px: Hard Light is a thickened, armoured contour; Sharp Light is an edge; Many Hands is several; Masterwork is one singled out.

## What the other pictures stand for

- Field Repair: 2 AP and 1 spare part: clear a jam, +10% durability, or repair 15 (a machine or Figment).
- Tuned: main-hand weapon never jams or breaks; tier II Load the Visualizer costs 1 less AP.
- Jury-Rig: when a Figment of yours is destroyed, +1 AP next turn (tier II also +1 Visualizer charge). The old picture (a jammed gun firing once more) lies and is replaced.
- Smooth: a failed lie makes them suspicious, not sure.
- Parley: before a fight, 0 AP, talk them down (the leader stands down or the ranks walk). The old table-flip picture is replaced.
- Showman: Picture costs 1 less charge; tier II one Figment order costs 0 AP once a turn.
- Quick Sketch (Rigger rank I move): once a turn, Picture costs 1 AP less.
- Index: once a scene, ask the Narrator one factual question; tier II Brain save bonus after an answer. Proven Formula: +3 on saves against a spell or condition that already hit you this fight. Objection!: expose a contradiction (absorbs Burden of Proof). Opening Argument: debate the enemy leader before a fight, every foe -1 AP in round 1. Thinking Aloud: after a Call, your next Evoke this turn costs 0 AP. Backdraft (Sigilist rank II): when the Evoker overloads, foes next to the caster take the same damage (replaces Overcharge). Running Script: after an Evoke your next glyph this turn costs 0 AP and adds no Flux (picture edited to show the next glyph under way).
- Quick Read: the first Analyze each fight costs 0 AP. Read Them: foes you analysed deal less damage to your squad. Darkened: Lightkiller puts it on the user: foes see him 3 tiles less far for 2 rounds (-3 PROVISIONAL).
