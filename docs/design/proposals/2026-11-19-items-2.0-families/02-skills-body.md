# Design notes moved out of the artist file 02-skills-body.md (19 Nov 2026)

Rules text removed from the artist file, and the rows decided.

## What each picture stands for (sources: attribute-drafts/strength.md, agility.md, endurance.md)

- `point_man`: allies within 3 tiles roll their Fear saves with your Guts; the nerve of the one in front is lent to those behind.
- `unflinching`: at x3 Momentum or more, melee blows against you are harder to land (-1 to hit): a defence you hold by keeping on.
- `marrow_talk`: a threat that fails in talk still leaves the listener Shaken (-2 on their next roll against you); in a fight too, a Threaten they save against still shakes them for a round.
- `stare_down`: before the first turn, one enemy that sees you saves or loses its first turn, and is Shaken for 3 rounds.
- `headlong_paid`: marks that this turn's Charge was paid for with 2 Momentum instead of AP, so Rolling Start and The Charge Carries build nothing from it.
- `untouchable`: with no torso armour, attacks on you roll twice and take the worse.
- `shoot_first`: you act in the surprise round even when surprised.
- `cut_in`: once or twice a scene, cut in on someone mid-sentence or mid-action and act first.
- `still_standing`: when your HP runs out, one more turn with 3 AP before you fall Downed, once a fight.
- `read_the_air`: read one tell of someone close: a bonus on your next social roll against them.
- `tremor`: nothing moving on the ground in your Vision surprises you, so you act in the surprise round; tier II also starts the fight with Guard.
- `reforge`: at the start of your turn, when you hold 1 plate or none, you gain 2 Guard: the plates are mended and set back.
