Ingenuity (Shadow) playtest, v0.31.10, room W7JDNW, seed PHC-W47ET7. Kestrel Vale, Phantom build (PER 7 / Ingenuity 7), traits Ghost Soldier + Live to Fight Another Day.
Result: reached Stop 7/15 at Level 2; won 3 of 4 fights. Lost to a Heart adept after 30 rounds: its Mending pulse heals +36 to +60, and the pistol couldn't keep up.
Verdict: Shadow plays very distinctly and is fun. Hide (1 AP), build Shadow up to 3, 16 Shadow Work actions (Disable, Tag, Read Intent...), and Break spends the whole meter (a 23-damage shot becomes 67–132). Hiding + 0 AP Disengage + smoke make a good hit-and-fade loop. Swingy: a missed Break plus Ghost Soldier's -2 AP is brutal. Echo shows "in a fight: not yet".
Balance: Parlour pistol ammo starvation (2-round magazine, 2 spare mags); the solo Heart adept is a wall; Crossfire and Stim-an-ally do nothing when solo.
Bugs: 1) Can't stim yourself in combat (blocker when solo). 2) Ingenuity's Level-up screen has no FORKS section at all (other skills show stubs). 3) Break damage preview on Strike shows 12–18 but it deals 59–86. 4) Break toggle stays pressed at 0 Shadow. 5) Shadow tab still says UNSEEN after you're Seen. 6) Duplicated error lines. 7) Stale Chosen Action text when switching from Strike to Drop prone. 8) Retreat marks tiles beyond its 3-tile range as reachable. 9) Hidden tooltip says an enemy ending next to you reveals you, but it doesn't. 10) Ghost Kill triggers on melee kills in plain sight. 11) Lobby/Shop centre pane blank on fade-in; Whole map collapses the layout at 1280x900; Shop Sell quantity box overlaps its buttons; tab highlight desyncs; free walk says "Paid 8 tiles"; Load rounds is blocked before contact.
Best moment: with 0 ammo, Disable + Tag + a 2-Shadow Break pistol-whip for 86 (crit) on a Gun drone. Worst: two 65% Break misses while the adept healed, and being unable to stim myself.
