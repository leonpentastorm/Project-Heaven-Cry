### 3. Momentum & Raw Power Feel, Clarity & Balance
- **How Momentum plays:** Builds via plain **Hit** (+1; +2 with Breaker vs Shaken) and **Charge** (with `Rolling Start`: 0 cost, +1 on hit plus distance bonus reliably spiking straight to **5/5 FULL** on 2–4 tile Charges), plus `Read the Swing` (+1 on enemy melee miss). Spends 1 on `Barrel In` / `Riposte`, or all `>=2` on **Finishers** (`Coup`, `Break`, `Cleave`, `Run Through`, `Launch`), chaining into **Follow Through** (keeps 1 Momentum) + **Swinging 2** (next Strike/Opener/Barrel In costs 0 Momentum). Walking tiles or taking hits decays `0.71`→`0.69` Momentum (Guts 5), **except pre-contact 0-AP movement does not decay Momentum**, and **`Guard` (1 AP)** prevents hit decay.
- **Clarity & Distinctness:** The `Opener (Charge / Grapple) → Hit → Finisher → Follow Through + Swinging → Barrel In` loop feels punchy, tactical, and totally distinct from ranged/mana kits. Every Finisher has a clear niche: `Coup` (380–426 dmg nuke), `Break` (219 dmg + Stun + 0 Armour, MVP vs Gate Elite), `Cleave` (shared-roll multi-target wipe), `Run Through` (high dmg + Pinned), `Launch` (2-AP reliable knockback + auto dmg). **However, Momentum previews and chain-lock rules are misleading:** `Charge` previews lie (`Momentum 0 -> 0`), Breaker's +1 vs Shaken isn't shown in `Hit` preview, and ending a chain with a Finisher locks out plain `Hit (3 AP)` (blocking `Swinging 2` that turn) while still letting `Class -> Barrel In (3 AP)` and `Shove (2 AP)` run.
- **Balance vs Ranged Builds:**
  - *Strengths:* Devastating burst/single-target lethality (`Charge` 280–326, `Coup` 380–426) and strong disruption (`Grapple` + `Iron Grip` completely shuts down Boss `Riposte`s and fleeing shooters; 1-AP `Threaten` + `Bone Talk II` AoE Shakes and stops advance).
  - *Pain points:* Walking 4–6 tiles across open mid-combat burns `3/5` Momentum down to `0` while walking into Overwatch/Suppress fire; against heavy firing lines, melee leans heavily on **pre-contact 0-AP scouting** and **Smoke + Hide behind Full Cover** to force ranged AI into melee. Defensively, missing 55%–65% swings against DEF 17 Elites leaves you exposed to massive Burst crits with no in-combat Stims (e.g., Vault guard 19/233 HP scare).

### 5. Bugs & UI Issues (with Repro Steps)
1. **Momentum previews ignore Charge & Breaker Shaken gains:** Queue `Charge` (or `Hit` against a `Shaken` foe) → Chosen action previews `Momentum 0 -> 0` (or `0 -> 1` vs Shaken) → Confirm → actual Momentum jumps to `5/5` (Charge) or `2/5` (Breaker Shaken).
2. **Duplicated validation text in Chosen action:** Trigger any blocked action (`Your chain has ended...`, `An Opener is only the first melee technique...`, `No straight run of 2 tiles...`, `Needs 1 Momentum...`, `Needs 2 AP...`, `Only a Reach weapon can be left in...`, `Hit · 3 AP Needs 3 AP`) → Chosen action renders the identical warning string twice.
3. **Post-Finisher chain lock blocks plain `Hit` & `Swinging 2`, but allows `Barrel In`/`Shove`:** Execute `Coup`/`Break`/`Launch` with 4+ AP left → `Swinging 2` activates, yet plain `Hit (3 AP)` is locked (`"a Finisher is the last melee technique of a turn"`), while `Class -> Barrel In (3 AP)` and `Shove (2 AP)` still execute and consume `Swinging 2`.
