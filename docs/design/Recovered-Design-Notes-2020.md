# PHC Recovered Design Notes: Perks and Magic

> Exported from the living Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/8zMzay23SbWCiPC97cDbuC on 2026-11-05. The Doc is the source of truth; re-export after edits.

2026-11-03 · Leon PT

## What the notes establish

The screenshots come from two Discord channels: #dm-perks (September–October 2020) and #the-dm-wankshack (October 2021). Together they show that the perk system had three layers, and that the two magic schools each had a design of their own.

**Perks came in three layers**

1. **Tier I perks** belong to one skill and carry a type tag, for example *Half Life* [AP mod] [Reaction Speed].
2. **Combo perks** come from two or three skills together, for example Intimidation + Athletics.
3. **Ultimate perks** come from owning two combo perks, for example *Nation at War!* from the two Intimidation combos.

**Rules the perks imply**

- AP can be fractional, rounded down to whole AP, which is why *Half Life* exists.
- Brain Debuffs split into at least a **Left Brain** type, so there is probably a Right Brain type too. A guess: Left Brain covers Intelligence's logic skills, Right Brain covers Expression's creative ones.
- Armour Class works as a multiplier (a shield of 200 × Armour Class).
- Distances are in metres (a 7 m charge), and areas in tiles (3×3).

**Magic had two separate designs.** A stroke-drawing system, with dice deciding which strokes you get, matches the Sigil rules in the guide (Sigil tiers, draw costs, Stay Duration). A build-your-own-spell system, with schools and modules, matches the Soul side, Outbursts.

## Recovered magic designs

Both come from one #the-dm-wankshack conversation on 31 October 2021.

### Custom Soul spells (Levian's proposal)

Soul users design their own spells.

- Spells belong to schools: **Damage, Buff, Control**, and **Status effects** such as blinding.
- A low-level spell belongs to one school. A mid-level spell can combine two.
- Mana cost depends on the school.
- The player describes the spell; the DM sets its damage and area.

Romantic B0ss objected that freeform spells can't be balanced. His counter-proposal was a spell-crafting book: a base spell (his example was Ice Ball) on a hex grid, with modules slotted around it in three families.

| Family | Modules shown |
| --- | --- |
| Behaviours | Move, Duplicate, Bounce, Homing, Swift, Rain, Teleport, Mastery, Ether |
| Augments | Right, Left, Time, Inverse, Size, Random |
| Triggers | Impact, At Once, Expire, Periodic, Proximity |

### Stroke-drawn spells (Romantic B0ss's proposal)

A new class, undecided between Soul and Mind, that casts by drawing runes in the air.

- Dice decide which stroke you get. For example, 1–3 gives a vertical stroke and 3–5 a horizontal one.
- A level 1 spell takes 2 strokes; a level 2 spell takes 3.
- Players can hold one or two strokes in a pool, then draw more at random.
- Players who memorise which strokes make which spell can build them on purpose, "to become big brain."

This is almost certainly the **Sigil** system. It explains the Sigil rules that survived in the guide: Sigil tiers (more strokes per tier), Sigil Draw AP and Core costs, Sigil Stay Duration, and Professor Avdol telling a student to "memorise the sigil's path, draw it." It belongs to Mind, not Soul.

## Problems and open questions

**Problems**

1. **Almost every perk is an AP swing.** All 8 tagged Tier I perks are [AP mod], even on α and γ skills, which the guide gives other perk types. Swings of ±3 AP on a 6 AP turn are 50% of a turn.
2. **"Permanent −1 AP" makes a perk a trait.** Grim Reaper and Used to the Pain take a sixth of every turn, all campaign.
3. **Sugar Sugar Baybe can't run at the table.** It needs the gender of every NPC and the sexual orientation of every enemy, and its last clause turns a sexuality into a penalty.
4. **Perks and traits now overlap.** I'm WALKING Here! duplicates the Right of Way trait, and Tenno Heika Banzai! duplicates Forlorn Hope.
5. **Four perks have no effect written:** I'm WALKING Here!, My Spoon!, Origaman, Detectorative.
6. **The stroke-drawn Sigils stack randomness on randomness:** dice for the strokes, then a d20 to land the spell. The stroke pool is already the right fix; it needs a size and a stat behind it.
7. **The custom Outburst modules have no costs yet.** Each module needs a mana price before the system can be balanced.

**Open questions**

- [ ] Is there a Right Brain Debuff to match the Left one, and which skills land and resist each?
- [ ] Does a combo perk need a minimum rank, say Mastery 1, in every skill it uses?
- [ ] How many Tier I perks does a skill have: one per Mastery tier, or one that grows (like Soulmate's 5 / 10 / 15 / 20%)?
- [ ] Is the stroke-drawing class simply every Sigil user, or a separate class?
