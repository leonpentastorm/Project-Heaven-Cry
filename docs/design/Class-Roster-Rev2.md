## Class rules

Every base class belongs to one Core, and each Core runs on a different economy.

| Core | Runs on | Consumes | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Ammo, weapons, technology | Ammo, supplies, gear | Kills, and the bots every death releases |
| Mind | Sigil magic, through an Evoker | Mana cores | Mana: venting raises the ambient air |
| Soul | Soul magic, through an Emanator | Ambient mana | Mana cores, by crystallising |

Mind and Soul close the loop between them: Mind turns cores into air, Soul turns air back into cores.

**Four roles.** Every mage Core has an Offense, Defense, Support and Debuff class. Body has the same four roles, with Offense split into ranged and melee, because Body is the weapons Core.

| Role | Body | Mind | Soul |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offense | Marksman (ranged), Duelist (melee) | Artillerist | Tempest |
| Defense | Vanguard | Warden | Aegis |
| Support | Combat Medic | Scribe | Muse |
| Debuff | Assassin | Hexwright | Haunt |

**You don't pick a class; your skills earn one.** Every base class has three signature skills, and every one of the 27 skills is a signature of at least one class. At level 3, total your points in each class's three signature skills. The two highest totals are your options and you choose one; a tie for second adds that class to the choice. Nobody is classless, whatever way you chose to play. Your class grows with its signature total: rank II at 15, rank III at 21.

**Advanced classes** mix two base classes. To learn one you need Mastery 2 in one parent's recipe skills, Mastery 1 in the other's, and a tutor from the faction that teaches it. You don't have to hold either base class yourself.

Faith and Authority make no base class. Faith powers patrons and prayer; Authority stays the skill of command and reputation.

## Body classes

Body classes run on ammo, weapons and technology. They don't touch mana directly, but every kill releases bots into the air, and the Body classes do most of the killing.

#### Marksman · Offense, ranged

*Guns for most; bows for the quiet ones.*

**Recipe:** Dexterity + Vigilant + Reaction Speed. Guns or bows: same class, different weapon.

- **Resource: Heat** for guns: each shot adds Heat, each point is −1 to hit, past the limit the gun jams, and Heat bleeds off when you hold fire. Bows have no Heat, are silent, and draw weight scales with Raw Power.
- **I · Fire Discipline:** choose a mode each attack. Aimed: 3 AP, no Heat. Burst: 2 AP, +1 Heat, two hits. Auto: 3 AP, +3 Heat, a cone.
- **II · Special Ammo:** load armour-piercing, incendiary or hollow-point rounds, or arrowheads to match.
- **III · Overwatch:** hold AP to fire at the first enemy that moves into your line.

#### Duelist · Offense, melee

*Guns jam. Blades don't.*

**Recipe:** Athletics + Raw Power + Guts.

- **Resource: Momentum,** up to 5. +1 for each tile you close on a target, and +1 for each hit.
- **I · Lunge:** spend 1 Momentum, move 2 tiles as part of an attack.
- **II · Riposte:** after a successful dodge, spend 1 Momentum to counter-attack for 0 AP.
- **III · Finisher:** spend all your Momentum on one strike: +20% damage per point.

#### Vanguard · Defense

*Someone has to stand in front.*

**Recipe:** Durability + Pain Threshold + Guts.

- **Resource: Guard.** Build it by bracing and blocking; spend it to take hits meant for others.
- **I · Brace:** 2 AP, gain Guard equal to your Durability rank.
- **II · Draw Fire:** spend 1 Guard: one enemy must target you next turn, or take −2 to hit anyone else.
- **III · Reactive Plate:** your armour is bot-woven technology that hardens when struck. Every 3 Guard you lose sheds bots: the ambient mana rises one step.

#### Combat Medic · Support

*Nobody stays behind.*

**Recipe:** Sensory + Camaraderie + Proficiency.

- **Resource: Supplies:** stims, kits and med-drones, restocked at rest.
- **I · Triage:** 2 AP, heal an adjacent ally.
- **II · Stim Throw:** 1 Supply, heal an ally up to 4 tiles away.
- **III · Field Surgery:** out of combat, restore a broken limb or clear every Body Debuff from one ally. Robot medics repair machines the same way.

#### Assassin · Debuff

*Stealth, poison, and the patience to wait for the right moment.*

**Recipe:** Ingenuity + Intimidation + Reaction Speed.

- **Resource: Marks,** up to 3, and toxin doses restocked at rest.
- **I · Mark:** 1 AP, mark an enemy you can see. It can't hide from you, and your squad gets +1 to hit it.
- **II · Toxin:** coat a blade, dart or arrow. Your next hit applies a Body Debuff (Weakened, Slowed or Bleeding).
- **III · Execution:** a marked enemy below a quarter of its HP dies to your next hit. Bosses and Celestials are immune.

## Mind classes

Mind classes load mana cores into an Evoker and write spells as command words. A spell's tier is its word count, and each word burns Core.

**Shared rule: Flux and venting.** Every spell leaves excess bots in the Evoker as Flux. Too much Flux overloads it. Any Mind class can **Vent** (1 AP): dump all Flux into the air and raise the ambient mana one step. That's how Mind creates mana, and why the squad's Soul mage wants a Mind mage nearby.

#### Artillerist · Offense

*FIRE – CONDENSE – PUSH, at range, as often as the cores hold out.*

**Recipe:** Brain Power + Visualization + Vigilant.

- **I · Barrage:** your damage words (FIRE, SHOCK, FREEZE) cost 1 Core less.
- **II · Overcharge:** once per combat, add one extra word to a spell for free.
- **III · Payload:** double a spell's area, for double the Flux.

#### Warden · Defense

*WALL. HARDEN. HOLD.*

**Recipe:** Rumination + Composure + Analysis.

- **I · Raise Cover:** a WALL sigil that creates cover on up to 2 tiles.
- **II · Ward:** shield an ally with HP equal to 10 × the Core you spend.
- **III · Seal:** mark a zone where enemy spells cost double.

#### Scribe · Support

*Most sigils are written in a hurry. A Scribe's are written to last.*

**Recipe:** Proficiency + Eloquence + Rumination. Sigils are words, and a Scribe is the best writer in the squad.

- **I · Quicken:** a sigil that gives an ally +1 AP next turn.
- **II · Inscribe Gear:** write a sigil onto an ally's weapon or armour: +10% damage or +1 Armour Class until the next rest.
- **III · Stored Spell:** write a whole spell onto an object. Anyone can trigger it later, for 1 AP.

#### Hexwright · Debuff

*BIND. BLIND. UNMAKE.*

**Recipe:** Analysis + Charisma + Eloquence. Find the weak point, then talk the bots into turning on it.

- **I · Bind:** a sigil that leaves its target Pinned.
- **II · Blind and Slow:** sigils that inflict Blinded or Slowed, or a Left Brain Debuff.
- **III · Rewrite:** corrupt an active enemy buff into its opposite.

## Soul classes

Soul classes pull ambient mana into the heart and think spells into being, built from a school (Damage, Buff, Control, Status) plus modules. The richer the air, the cheaper the spell.

**Shared rules: Strain and crystallising.** Each cast adds Strain, the bots gathered in your heart. Past your Will Power rank, every cast risks a Mana Short. Bots stored in your Emanator can pay for a spell without adding Strain. Any Soul class can **Crystallise** (3 AP, or out of combat): lower the ambient mana one step and make a mana core. That's how Soul creates cores, and why the squad's Mind mage wants a Soul mage nearby.

#### Tempest · Offense

*Imagine the storm. The world agrees.*

**Recipe:** Will Power + Empathy + Visualization.

- **I · Shape:** Damage-school spells cost less Strain.
- **II · Storm Front:** one Damage spell hits up to three targets.
- **III · Heart Surge:** cast at double effect, for double Strain.

#### Aegis · Defense

*If you can imagine it whole, it stays whole.*

**Recipe:** Lore Master + Conscience + Faith.

- **I · Will Shield:** shield an ally out of the ambient air.
- **II · Drink It In:** an enemy spell that hits your shield feeds its bots into your Emanator instead.
- **III · Sanctuary:** a zone where allies take half damage until your next turn.

#### Muse · Support

*Your belief makes the squad better than it is.*

**Recipe:** Camaraderie + Implication + Authority.

- **I · Inspire:** +2 on an ally's next roll.
- **II · Mend:** heal flesh by imagining it whole; stronger in rich air.
- **III · Gift:** hand an ally one spell you designed; they cast it once, on your Strain.

#### Haunt · Debuff

*The bots can build a fireball. They can also build what someone fears most.*

**Recipe:** Intimidation + Implication + Lore Master.

- **I · Dread:** inflict Fear.
- **II · Mind Fog:** inflict a Left or Right Brain Debuff.
- **III · Puppet:** take control of an enemy's next action.

## Patrons and prayer

Blessings aren't a class. Anyone can pray, if they have a patron and enough Faith.

- **A patron** is a Celestial who has taken you into a pact, playing a theme: a saint, a war god, a trickster. You gain one in play, through a vow, a shrine or a quest. The Narrator plays them.
- **Devotion** is your standing with your patron. Keep their rites and stay in their costume to earn it; break them and lose it.
- **Every prayer ships bots out of the world:** the ambient mana drops one step.

| Faith | With a patron, you can |
| --- | --- |
| 3 (Mastery 1) | **Pray:** call a Blessing. How generously it's answered depends on your Devotion. |
| 5 (Mastery 2) | **Be Witnessed:** after a prayer, your patron watches through the bots' camera feed until your next turn. Actions on their theme get +2; actions against it cost Devotion. |
| 7 (Mastery 3) | **Ask for a Miracle:** once per session, spend all your Devotion for an answer far beyond a Blessing. |

The Lumeri Templar is the class built around a patron, but any class can carry one.

## Advanced classes

Each advanced class mixes two base classes and is taught by the faction that invented the mix. It needs a signature total of 15 in one parent, 9 in the other, and a tutor. Any pairing can become a class once the world gives it a home; these 15 are the ones the setting already has.

| Class | Mix | Taught by | What the mix does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gunslinger | Marksman + Duelist | The Old Drifter's road (Ambrosius Costeau) | Gunplay with a swordsman's footwork: the Draw, Six for Six, Called Shots |
| Hellraiser Heavy | Marksman + Vanguard | Hellraiser Divisions | Emplaced, belt-fed fire that makes ground impossible to stand on |
| CCEC Ballistician | Marksman + Assassin | Central City Espionage Commission | Ballistics and weak points; one shot closes a case |
| Banner Dancer | Duelist + Combat Medic | 237th Hellraiser Division | Armourless skirmisher whose bot-thread banners shield and rally allies |
| RTC Contractor | Marksman + Combat Medic | Rigaz Trade Company | Credit buys supply drops, strikes and extractions |
| Blade Scribe | Duelist + Artillerist | The southern sects | Evoker blades: each sword form draws a sigil |
| Siege Engineer | Vanguard + Warden | Central Command Center | Deployable cover and turrets reinforced with WALL sigils |
| Spellslinger | Marksman + Scribe | Kortev Utility Shack | Sigils inscribed on rounds: every magazine carries a stored spell |
| Storm Hand | Duelist + Tempest | Kemizuchi Military Academy | One element infused into a weapon through an Emanator gauntlet; kills charge it |
| Adamantile Knight | Vanguard + Aegis | Adamantile Brigade | Plate that hardens when struck and turns shed bots into shields for allies |
| Shade | Assassin + Haunt | Smetanita Conjunction | Silent kills that leave fear behind; marked targets see their nightmares |
| Lumeri Templar | Vanguard or Duelist + Muse, with a pact | Lumeri Church | The paladin: Blessings fired as shells, a patron watching, Judgement |
| Stroke Gambler | Artillerist + Hexwright | Black Scavengers | Random half-sigils stitched into whole spells |
| Devoted | Tempest + Muse | Arkvelton witches | A few spells, loved and boosted beyond anyone else's |
| Circuit Mage | Scribe + Muse | The Institute of Forgotten Knowledge, in secret | Vents and crystallises in one body: the only caster who needs nobody's cores. Can fuse a Sigil's shape with a Soul school |

The Circuit Mage is a problem on purpose. A caster who closes the mana loop alone is exactly the kind of NPC the Celestials would want to watch.
