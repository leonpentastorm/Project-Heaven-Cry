# Design decisions log

Why PHC is the way it is. Newest last. Each entry: what was decided, why, and where it lives.

## 2020 (original team)

1. **Disco Elysium's skill web, with combat.** 27 skills under 9 attributes under 3 cores; skills are also inner voices. The 2020 team's aim was to make that web drive tactical combat too. Source: `Official_EPCCG.docx` (Vietnamese, in the claude.ai Project).
2. **Points cap downward.** Core caps Attribute, Attribute caps Skill (the "Đồng chí mèo" example in the EPCCG).
3. **Traits are big trade-offs.** One large upside, one large downside, max two, for roleplay.

## Nov 2026 revival (Leon + Claude)

4. **Skill track 0–10.** The modifier is −2 at 0 points (the 2020 rule was −5), else floor(points ÷ 2). The harsher −5 punished spreading points and made untrained rolls pointless. Mastery unlocks at 3/5/7/9 points reward depth.
5. **Overreach and Echo.** High mastery can fail spectacularly (Overreach), and choosing to play out a failure earns an Echo reroll token. This keeps Disco Elysium's "your strengths are your weaknesses" in a dice system.
6. **Traits must change a decision**, not just add points. Rebalanced on a price list: net upside about +3 SP, and +1 to a roll costs 2 SP. Kept the deliberate racism trait in clean in-world language and added a xenophobe trait (Humanity First). 74 traits total. All pop-culture references were removed in favour of in-universe text.
7. **Perks:** Acquired (two per skill, Tier I at 3 points, II at 7), Achieved (a combo plus a deed, granted by the Narrator), Ultimate. 76 total. Perks can add at most +3 AP per turn.
8. **Lore locked:** simulation world, nanobots = mana, Celestials (trapped Earth humans) bound by Accords, the Knowing, creation ladder, Evoker = Sigil, Emanator = Soul, heart link for Soul casters.
9. **Class economy:** Body classes use ammo, weapons and tech. Mind classes consume cores and create mana. Soul classes consume mana and create cores. Every martial, gun, bow and assassin class is a Body class. Mage cores each have offense, defense, support and debuff classes. Bows and guns share the weapon-neutral **Marksman**.
10. **Classes are earned, not chosen.** At level 3 your skills give you a base class. If you qualify for two, you choose. Advanced classes mix two base classes and need a tutor.
11. **No classless builds.** The first recipe system left 83% of random builds classless. Fix: each base class has three signature skills covering all 27 skills, plus the closest-class rule (choose among your top two signature totals). Monte Carlo over 100k builds: 0% classless, 3.5 options on average, every class reachable by 26–28% of builds.
12. **Patrons:** anyone with a patron and enough Faith can pray. This replaced a "Faithful" class that fit no economy. Tiers: Devotion, Witnessed (Faith 5), Miracle (Faith 7).
13. **Game-ready format:** one canonical rules spec with every number, plus JSON data. Tactical grid. AI Narrator with any provider (bring your own key). Leon left the stack choice open.
14. **Engine vs Narrator.** The engine owns numbers; the Narrator owns judgement. Every data item is tagged engine, hybrid or narrator. Unknown effect ops fall back to the Narrator.
15. **Sigil words keep the 2020 names** (FIRE, FREEZE, SHOCK, BIND, BLIND, SLOW, MUDDLE, WALL) over newer inventions.
16. **Multiplayer:** up to 4 players per room. A human can narrate instead of the AI.
17. **Speaking order in story mode:** everyone speaks once, then the Narrator answers all of it together. Players can request a turn; the host can skip someone who's away.
18. **Characters lock in before a session** or are saved as drafts and brought into any room.
19. **Character creation flow:** Identity → Cores → Skills → Traits → Kit → Lock in. Separate screens for Cores and Skills, so it's clear which skill belongs to which attribute.
20. ~~No archetype picker.~~ *Superseded by 40.* (Was: you place core points and the screen names your archetype live: Soldier, Scholar, Believer, Operator, Crusader, Mystic, Drifter.)
21. **Skills screen = attribute rows.** One core at a time; each attribute is a row with its three skill cards to the right, each card with room for 4:5 portrait art. Raising a skill auto-raises its Attribute and Core, with the cost shown before the click.
22. **Accepted in rules 1.1:** creation gives only 10 skill points (max 5 in one skill), with attributes and cores following automatically; attribute and core points come only from levelling.
23. **Colour system (art v1.3).** Skills keep 9 attribute colours. Art uses the pigment; the UI uses a contrast-safe ink of the same hue (Expression and Virtue pigments were about 2.2:1 on the UI). Cores keep amber/cyan/violet for core-level UI. State colours moved off the skill hues: success green `#6FEA77`, MP blue `#5C9EFF`, HP/damage `#F65157` (shared with Endurance on purpose), failure muted, Echo white. → `docs/design/colour-system.md`, `art/style-guide/v1.3/palette.json`.
24. **Trait and perk icons are white.** The perk rule that mixed requirement colours into one ink is dropped (mixes collided with real attribute colours). Requirements show as skill chips beside the icon.
25. **Wick is canon:** the EPCCG's mascot, drawn after the 258th Medical Unit's field automata (MPAT's unit). A printed figure, not an NPC. → `docs/lore/Wick.md`.
26. **The EPCCG is the base layer of the lore.** Its English edition is `docs/lore/EPCCG-Player-Character-Creation-Guide.md`; the World Bible is the Narrator's truth layer on top of it. The First-Patch Revival portraits are its 27 quoted characters. → `docs/lore/README.md`.
27. **Skills are abstract figures, not people.** All three Heavenprint portrait sets (Original Heavenprint, First-Patch Revival, New Faces) were painted as skill icons, one character per skill. Playtesters said it made no sense for the player's own skills to be represented by other people in the world, so the skill page moved to faceless abstract figures. The 81 paintings are character portraits in `art/character-portraits/`: First-Patch Revival is the EPCCG cast; the other two are a pool of world characters for NPCs.
28. **One style guide.** Art v1.3 is a single file, `art/style-guide/v1.3/PHC-Style-Guide.md`; the six overlapping v1.2 files kept drifting apart.
29. **One main branch.** The art library (`main`) and the game context (`game-context`) were merged so rules, data, lore, UI and art live in one tree.
30. **Rules 1.1 (5 Nov 2026).** From the gameplay review (`proposals/2026-11-gameplay-fixes.md`):
    - **Skill modifier = points − 1**, so builds show on the dice; Mastered is +9. Dodge, saves, debuff strength and counter use the modifier; crits are natural 20 (19–20 Aimed or from Hidden).
    - **Voices replace Inner Voice:** every trained skill can speak through Narrator hooks and engine passive checks, toned after its EPCCG speaker. Disco Elysium's core, at last.
    - **Overreach is indulge or resist**, no roll: the old 70–90% failure TNs made rolling pointless.
    - **HP:** +15 per Endurance (was +50), 5 + 2 × Durability per level, so tanks last twice as long, not four times, and conditions matter.
    - **XP and classes:** level 3 (the class) at the end of session 1; a 5e-like curve to level 20 at about session 51; class ranks at 13 / 18.
31. **The Unspoken Name** (lore, 5 Nov 2026). Celestials may LARP anything from real Earth, and players can import any character as a Celestial's LARP, but the Accords forbid saying a real-Earth name in the world. References are plain to the players, never to the characters. Official content stays archetypal. Replaces "no pop culture at all".
32. **Tone line revised.** NPCs are funny the way people are; Celestials are funny the way people are when nothing can hurt them. The tragedy belongs to the NPCs. (The EPCCG's NPCs are the funniest people in the book, so the old line was wrong.)
33. **New canon:** Earth is the in-world homeworld (Central City); campaigns usually start with Celestial mayhem; Crawlers are cursed NPC-2 espers; Operation Daggerfall (~3800) nuked their world; people live ~400 years; real Earth is around year 65,000. → World Bible.
34. **More canon:** the 4000s are the Central City founding calendar (history runs back billions of years); the Crawler curse runs one way; the Thousand World Legion is the Thousand-Planet City's army; Bazaim is a human nation of lifted curses.
35. **AHOC's origin:** the Progenitors, a thousand isekai-powered humans who learned the truth ~5,000 years before Central City and were hunted to extinction; their descendants run AHOC now, in decay. Crawlers will be a race option later; humans first.
36. **AHOC is the constant faction:** everywhere, like a containment foundation; chapters vary by leader; players aren't members by default but creation always uses the EPCCG. **Celestials** don't fight each other; the Accords say hands off, create but never erase, appear only by cause. The Veil is the one exception (confirmed in 37).
37. **Canon:** the Veil exception (an NPC who proves the truth may be erased; suspicion isn't proof); heroes are patches (Celestials can only create counters to their mistakes); Celestial politics (mega-powerful humans: no fighting, plenty of politics).
38. **Scale (canon):** several sextillion humans on trillions of worlds over billions of years. Lore is regional: the EPCCG describes one region; Crawlers are known to a tiny pocket. Races in character creation should come from the campaign's region.
39. **Sectors (canon):** each pocket of humanity is a Sector; campaigns are set in one. The EPCCG's is the Earth Sector (name Suggested). Characters pick Sector → origin (no numbers; Narrator-owned local-knowledge rule, provisional) → race (later). The Crawler-Blooded trait needs a review against the one-way curse when races arrive.
40. **Archetypes are presets, guidance only** (Leon, 5 Nov 2026). Creation's Cores screen offers 37 archetypes in 8 families covering the usual RPG roles (tank, striker, sniper, stealth, blaster, controller, healer, face, leader, hybrids, generalist) plus Custom. Picking one pre-fills the 10 skill points. Nothing persists: no stats, no portrait, no sheet label, no change over levels, because the core system is complex and archetypes exist only to guide. Names are generic role words, never class names; the validator checks presets and class leans.
41. **Combined checks of 1–3 skills** (Leon: most requests combine 1–3 skills). Roll with the lowest modifier, +2 per other trained skill in the check; TN unchanged. Provisional, spec item 42.
42. **3D dice for the real client** (Leon, 5 Nov 2026). Dice are thrown on a 3D table with textured 3D dice, and one throw can carry several dice when the situation calls for it (bursts, full auto, initiative, damage, injury tables). The engine's seeded RNG decides every result before the physics throw starts; the animation lands on that result. Not built in the prototype. → ui-screens.md, "Dice table".
43. **Every skill has a "?" that opens its EPCCG entry** (Leon, 5 Nov 2026): the quote, the First-Patch Revival portrait of the one it's about, the explanation, at high levels, good for and bad for. Text lives in `data/skills.json` `epccg`, with real-Earth names swapped under the Unspoken Name. In five entries the speaker talks about someone else (Sazmin about Knight Jiren, Sister March about Grandma Marry, Rémy Étienne about Master Sakamoto, Anastasia Vandenberg about Souji, Brengan Springfield about Hank Ladan); the portrait shows the subject and the caption names both. Confirmed against the 2020 Vietnamese original.
44. **Weapon mastery is back** (Leon: one of the main progression mechanics outside character level). Tracked by kills per weapon family, as on the 2020 sheet: levels 0–5 at 5 / 15 / 40 / 80 / 140 kills, +1 to attack at level 2 and +2 at 4, a weapon perk at levels 1, 3 and 5 (30 perks, 3 per family). Bosses count 3; only XP-awarding fights count. All numbers provisional (spec 14, items 43–44). → weapon_mastery.json.
45. **The Veil is a special equipment type** with its own slot (Leon: code it in and leave it alone). The engine records it and applies nothing; rules to come. The 2020 sheet's slot list is now the spec's.
46. **Essence of Experience** (Leon): XP sealed in mana; needs a mid-level Evoker or Emanator user to crack (drafted as Mastery 2 in Brain Power or Will Power), can go to yourself or a party member, sells well. Drafted at 100 XP, 300 credits, one at creation (the 2020 sheet's starting inventory), one absorbed per long rest. Provisional (spec 14, item 45). → items.json.
47. **The 2020 AHOC seal is back** (Leon made it for the 2020 EPCCG). Rings read "Anti Heaven Operation Commands" and "Department of Extra Celestial Defense"; the centre holds the subject class ("Class C"). In the UI it's white flat ink (red means HP and hostiles); the red original is archived. It heads the creation form and stamps the Lock in card. → art/emblems/.
48. **Creation budgets are back** (Leon: "that is why we had attribute points before"). 1.1's free automatic rise let a build spread over three Cores finish with Cores 4/3/3 while a single-Core build finished 4/1/1: four Core points apart, the whole career's supply. Creation now spends the 2020 budgets: 10 Skill, 10 Attribute, 6 Core points, caps of 5, and an unspent Core point trades for 2 Attribute points. Skills still spend the Attribute and Core points they need automatically, so placing skills first still works. Field Medic and Witch Hunter presets moved one point each to fit 6 Core points. Provisional (spec 14, item 47). Supersedes the creation half of decision 22.
49. **Archetypes spend every point** (Leon: choosing an archetype and then spending points again defeats the purpose; every archetype has all its points spent or it is removed). Each preset now places its 10 Skill points and all 10 Attribute and 6 Core points (`attributes` and `cores` in archetypes.json). The extra points follow each role's priority (tanks Endurance and Strength, snipers Perception and Agility, healers Insight and Virtue...), filled by `tools/archetype_fill.py`; the validator fails any archetype that leaves a point unspent. All 37 fit, so none were removed.
50. **No creation caps** (Leon: "you want a 7 1 1 character, you GET a 7 1 1 character"). The caps of 5 on Cores, Attributes and single skills at creation are gone; only the tree's own caps apply (skill ≤ Attribute ≤ Core ≤ 10). The budgets limit any one Core, Attribute or skill to 7 at creation. Archetype presets stay at 5 or below as moderate examples. Reverses spec item 35 (1.1's 5-point skill cap).
51. **All-in archetypes, and no dump stats** (Leon). A ninth family, **All-in**, has one 7/1/1 build per Attribute: Living Wall, Wrecking Ball, Deadeye, Phantom, Savant, Illusionist, Heartreader, Unbroken, Prophet (a skill at 7, all 6 Core points in one Core). The other presets' spare points, which the old cap of 5 had pushed into unrelated Attributes and empty Cores, now go only into their role's Attributes: `tools/archetype_fill.py` searches for the most even allocation inside the role that spends every point, borrowing a neighbouring Attribute only by the minimum when the arithmetic forces it. The validator rejects a Core raised above what its Attributes need. Several regular presets now finish 7/1/1 as well (Bulwark, Berserker, Mender, Confessor, Witch...); they differ from the All-in set by their skills.
52. **Wick's own mark is the welcome pose** (Leon, 5 Nov 2026): one open hand held out, the other at the lantern heart. It replaces the Mind Without Fog icon (a trait's pose) as the app icon at the top left of the creation screens. White ink on transparency in `art/mascot/wick-welcome.png`. Mind Without Fog stays the style anchor for trait and perk icons.
53. **Wick's modes** (Leon, 5 Nov 2026): flat white ink is for trait and perk icons only (the welcome mark used as the app icon is the one other flat Wick). The Narrator's avatar is Wick **fully 3D and rendered**. Brief: `art/briefs/wick-narrator-3d.md`. Until the render exists, the prototype's speaking order shows a crop of the painted model study.
54. **Movement is tiles per AP** (Leon: "you move 4 squares you pay 4 AP unless you have good agility"). Restores the 2020 rule: Movement = 1 + 0.5 × (Agility − 1) tiles per AP, minimum 0.5; tiles paid for but not walked carry over within the turn. 1.1's "3 + 0.5 × (Agility − 1) tiles for 1 AP" made moving three times too cheap. Armour penalties become −0.5 / −0.5 / −1 Movement; the traits' ±1 Movement (2020 wording) stand. The combat grid shows the cheapest path as arrows and the AP it costs before you commit. Provisional (spec 14, item 48).
55. **Regular archetypes use at least two Cores** (Leon). After the caps went, 15 presets had drifted to 7/1/1 or 6/1/1 and blurred into the All-in family. Each now has a second-Core attribute in its role list (Bulwark, Berserker, Brawler, Scholar: Determination; Fencer: Perception; Sigilist, Mender, Confessor, Captain: Endurance; Enchanter, Jinx, Detective: Insight; Engineer: Agility; Witch: Intelligence; Troubadour: Expression). The validator enforces it; only All-in goes single-Core.
56. **Base classes get plain names** (Leon: basic classes are meant to be barebone; names like Artillerist sound weird). Duelist → Fighter, Vanguard → Defender, Combat Medic → Medic, Artillerist → Mage, Hexwright → Trickster, Tempest → Channeler, Aegis → Guardian, Muse → Bard, Haunt → Hexer; Marksman, Assassin, Warden and Scribe stay. Ids follow the names. The 15 advanced classes keep their specific, faction-taught names. Earlier entries in this log keep the old names as history.
57. **A perk step at every Mastery level; a Mastered perk at 10** (Leon: "a perk every mastery level for each skill"). Before, a skill's perks moved at 3, 7 and 10 points, Mastery 4 gave nothing and Mastered only finished what 7 started. Five new perks per skill was rejected: 81 more perks and icons, perks turning into a stat sheet, and more things stacking into the AP ceiling. Instead each Mastery level (3/5/7/9) is one step through the skill's existing two perks and two tiers, in the order the player picks, and 10 points grants one new Mastered perk per skill (27 in all). The choice survives as order until 9 points. The UI gives Mastered perks a gilded card in the skill's attribute ink. Provisional (spec 14, item 49).
58. **Trait compulsions spread across the voices** (Leon: Composure checks were "fucking everywhere" in the traits). Composure sat in 12 of 74 traits, against a median of about 3; 8 of them were downsides worded as "take a Composure check to resist your own compulsion", so one high-Composure character shrugged off most trait downsides and the other inner voices never got a say in the character's flaws. Each compulsion now goes to the voice that would argue it: Holo-Drama Addict → Visualization (picture it as a scene worth filming), Lunarshine's slope → Will Power, Ghost Soldier resting → Vigilant (watching the doors), Gun Nut → Dexterity (the gun hand has to let go), Rival → Camaraderie, Trench Poet → Eloquence, Market Blood → Analysis (you can't stop pricing it), Heaven's Faithful → Faith. Composure keeps the traits that are really about the face: The Mask, Machine-Faced, Chain Smoker, Daggerfall Veteran's flashback, Born Officer. It now appears in 7 traits, with Authority 8 and Intimidation 7. The living Trait Edition still has the old wording.
59. **Grim Reaper gives +2 AP per kill, not +3** (Leon: +3 is way too strong). With 6 AP a turn and the +3 perk cap, +3 per kill handed an Intimidation build half a turn per takedown and filled the whole perk allowance from one perk; +2 still rewards the kill. Both tiers change.
60. **Rival gets an XP upside, both ways** (Leon: rivals help each other grow). Neck and Neck: 50 XP (source `bond`) whenever your rival reaches a new level, and +10% XP from every source but Essence while they're a level ahead of you; the same applies to them. 50 XP is about a sixth of a session's pacing target, so it nudges without carrying anyone. Provisional (spec 14, item 50).
61. **Combo perks need Mastery 2; every skill is in one; Ultimates are secret** (Leon). Achieved (combo) perks are the 2020 idea of perks unlocked by high levels in two or three skills; they had slipped out of view because no screen showed them. They now need 5 points in each listed skill (3 became too cheap once every Mastery level gives a perk step), and three new combos cover the six skills that had none: Bite the Bullet (Guts + Pain Threshold), Sixth Sense (Sensory + Vigilant), What Could Be (Visualization + Conscience). Each skill's "?" entry shows its combos on their own card style. Ultimate perks stay a secret: nothing lists or hints at them until one is granted, and the grant gets its own award screen. Provisional (spec 14, item 51).
