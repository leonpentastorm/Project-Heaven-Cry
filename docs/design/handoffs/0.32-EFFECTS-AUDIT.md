# 0.32 effects lane: the effect audit (by trace)

Built by `apps/web/src/game/fx/effects-audit.test.ts`: every prayer (7 Whispers, each patron's three Hymns and its
Miracle), every Bond and Tending verb, every Figment action, done **through the reducer** in a fight on the practice
square, and the fx events the engine sent played through `play.ts`'s real handlers (the queue's batch passes included)
on a jsdom grid. Per action: what was drawn on the map (boxes put on the effects layer; whether the caster's token and
each changed unit's token moved), whether every HP and shield change had its number, the sounds, the last log line.
Re-run: `cd apps/web && AUDIT_OUT=<file> npx vitest run src/game/fx/effects-audit.test.ts`.

"Before" is live 0.31.11 (origin/main c22cc8f7b); "after" is this branch (`wip/0.32-effects`). The columns visual,
number, sound are the after run's.

**Counts:** faults before **47** rows; after **4** rows (fixed 43). REFUSED rows are the harness's own set-up, not
faults (Turned Back needs a fall earlier in the fight; Hand over needs a stim on the owner).

Not in the table (found by reading, proved elsewhere): a destroyed Figment took the body-death path (DEAD, body fall)
and came apart a turn later: fixed client-side (fx/figments.ts H.down, BROKEN at once, the later unmake silent;
figments.test.ts). The data `deal` op (Husk Burst, the volatile trait), the `redirect` op (Ward, a Bond's share) and a
Miracle's many heals emit no `hurt`/`heal`: their numbers now come from the engine's hp stamps (fx/silent.ts).
