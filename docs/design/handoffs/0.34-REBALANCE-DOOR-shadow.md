## 2. Not built, and why (for the lead)

1. **The Push, Last Shade (Ingenuity 8).** The bible gives `reach_plus` 1, the shape ("once a turn, a one-use
   condition on res_max:shadow +1 until your next Break") and the price (Traced "until the end of your next turn"),
   but **no AP price** and Traced's rounds are a duration the rows count in rounds (traced's own row is 2). Same gap as
   Overbrace (guard hand-off 2.1). The only Push in data today, Overclock, is a 0 AP grant at will: **one ruling for all
   nine Pushes' AP** would let each lane build its own. Shape ready: a grant at `self_skill ingenuity 8`, `uses
   per_turn`, laying a new condition row `last_shade` (`stat_mod res_max:shadow` +1, ends on the Break); the Break's
   spend lays `traced` if the holder has `last_shade`.
2. **Dead Drop** (rank I, 1 AP, 1 Shadow, a friend within 4 tiles holding the door gains 1, never past its Reach). The
   resource row `actions.json shadow` has no `max`, and no predicate reads "the target holds the Shadow door"
   (`target_not_res` was removed in 0.32): a `resource` op on a friend with no meter would give it an uncapped Shadow.
   Needs `max: "resMax.shadow"` on the row (data) and a target gate (an existing `target_res {shadow: {at: ...}}`
   cannot say "has a top"); its solo half (a Shadow kept in the kit to the next fight) needs a saved field. Not built.
3. **Spotter as the Spotted mark** (fork at 8): today's passive (an ally's first hit a round on a Marked foe is an
   ambush) stays. The new form is an action with no AP in the bible ("while you are Hidden, 1 Shadow: a foe you see
   carries your Spotted mark until your next turn") and a new condition row `spotted` (+20%, squad `mark`). AP missing.
4. **Solo forms** (ruling 32): Lend the Dark on yourself, Spot used by yourself at the step below, Dead Drop's kit.
   Spot's "you can't use the mark yourself" is in `calls/` handlers; not reached.
5. **The keeps tags** (mind.json `keeps_tags`: a non-damaging Evoke, a Figment's blow keep Shadow; Tag, Stalk, Hide,
   Case keep the chain and the plates) are S6 `wear:` contexts at the Momentum and Guard wear sites and the
   `struck` flag's `attack_roll`; not reached.
6. **Shadow's Marks and gifts** (I: start with 1; II: seen wears every other turn; III: Last Shade free once a fight) are
   step 3 (S4).
7. **The Operative's Toxin** in the bible's form (no save from Hidden or a Break; a dose coats two hits; its Bleeding on
   the dealer's column) and **Sabotage's Rig on the factor**: not reached. **Rank levels 3, 5 and 15**: the shared class
   gate, not a door's lane (as the guard hand-off said).
8. **Card `felt` lines** of the bible are not texts in `skills.json`; the cards read their numbers already.
