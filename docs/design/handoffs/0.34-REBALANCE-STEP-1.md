## 3. Numbers the bible lacked, and what the lead must rule

1. **Falling Bites.** For Guard, Momentum, Figments, Bonds and Outbursts the two anchors need a Bite that falls as Reach grows (a unit worth less in a bigger spend). I set Bite on the worth curve W1 x (W10 / W1)^((p - 1) / 9) at every point where Reach or Bite steps (Bite keys added at points 4, 7 and 10), so the **full** cash-out climbs at every point, but a part-filled one is worth less after a Reach step (a lone Snap 2.0 at skill 9, 0.93 at 10; a one-Figment Curtain the same). For Guard, Figments and Bonds no Bite schedule can keep both anchors and a non-falling full cash-out with Bite steps only at 6 and 9 (the full Slam at Reach 4 would beat the skill-10 anchor). Rule: accept, or change an anchor, or give these doors their own growth (`scale.cash_out.grow`).
2. **Sigils at skill 1** ("a little under an Outburst turn"): read as the same share of the Outburst turn at both ends (0.85, from 11 at skill 10), so the two-glyph Line is 1.98 at skill 1. Provisional.
3. **Shadow's fresh ambush** (+50% in its own sum) makes a full ambush Break 13.5 plain shots, not ruling 62's 9. Kept (no reward cut); for the lead.
4. **Bonds' full cash-out at level 20** (Snap All, total) is 7,855: under five digits, a NOTE (ruling 51 says fail; ruling I says no door is compared with a total).
5. **Syphon** makes MP no choice at level 20 for Bonds and Outbursts (check (h) NOTE: the air gives about 74 and 93 MP for 2 AP a fight). A cost or the Syphon draw is a bible number.
6. **Two held Outbursts** (ruling 59) need a primitive not in `08-primitives.md` (the engine holds one heart).
