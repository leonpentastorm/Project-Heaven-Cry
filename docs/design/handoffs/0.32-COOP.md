## 1. The co-op stall: not reproduced

`apps/web/e2e/coop-loop.mjs` (new; one run by default, `RUNS=40` for the hunt) plays two-player fights through the
real room link, each in a new room (a new seed), four ways in turn: plain; fast (the guest acts the moment its panel
opens); reconnect (the guest refreshes mid-turn, an attack picked and not confirmed); background (the host's tab out
of sight whenever it is not the host's turn). A stall is 45 s with nothing moving on either screen.

**42 runs, 41 fights played to the end on both screens, 0 stalls** (plain 11, fast 11, reconnect 9, background 10;
2,825 actions, 1,756 by the guest, 221 refreshes, 172 hides, 0 page errors; one run lost before its fight to a page
refresh that timed out on a busy machine). Fights ran 43 s to 15 min (the bot only shoots or ends its turn). Four
builders shared the machine. With the 34 + 41 fights of `coop-stall.md`, that is 116 fights without the stall.

Found on the way and fixed at the cause (`89abfaf66`): the fight screen's way out (`fx/play.ts waitingIn`) "settled"
a fight the screen had synced silently (a refresh, a returning tab), 6 s after it, with nothing waiting: no screen
change, but a console warning and a count that read as a display stall (6 reconnect runs). It now settles only when an
effect really waits. Test: `stall.test.ts`.

The loop script's own fault, fixed before the counted runs: a refresh marks the player back, which moves the game's
version, so "refresh once a version" refreshed for ever.

## 2. Hop timings (local: one machine, the dev room server, 1,385 guest actions)

Each guest action from its send to the frame that drew its result (`net.ts hop`, opt-in `window.__phcHops`; the room
stamps what it relays, `rt`).

| Hop | Median ms | p95 | Worst |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| guest sent → room received | 0 | 16 | 379 |
| guest sent → host received | 1 | 28 | 430 |
| host received → applied (the engine) | 2 | 4 | 22 |
| applied → view sent | 1 | 2 | 17 |
| view sent → guest received | 11 | 42 | 632 |
| guest received → drawn | 36 | 62 | 150 |
| **total** | **52** | **117** | **675** |

Reading: locally the engine and the link cost almost nothing; the guest's own draw (about 36 ms) is the biggest local
part, and the worst cases are the busy machine. Leon's 2 to 3 s from Vietnam to a US host is therefore network (two
trips through a Cloudflare room far from one of them) or the live room's own place, not the code measured here. Next
step (owed, ROADMAP): the same stamps against the live site from two places.

## 4. "I'll narrate": works end to end, `cc18b7e79`

Works (proved by `apps/web/e2e/narrate.mjs`, two browsers, 19 checks): the host picks it in the room (no key); a guest
plays; the **Narrator's desk** opens on the host's screen only, in the middle column, both side panels kept: the
opening with a Conscience check at TN 16 (the engine rolls it on the guest's screen, HARD 16 shown); the results; the
guest's Clean Hands claim refused (the engine gives the use back); a prepared fight started from the desk; in the
fight the guest's attempt answered with the existing fight-screen Narrator buttons (a check at TN 8, rolled and shown
on both screens). A Narrator with no character reads every sheet and does not speak; the chip says NARRATOR · YOU.

Missing (in ROADMAP, spec 6802): the desk sends only narration, scene title, checks, rulings and a prepared fight (no
hooks, NPCs, objects, hazards, triggers, loot, XP, a made-up fight); the Narrator sees no hidden things in the story
(needs a ruling on what the seat sees). Not tested: the fight's condition, surrender and perk Yes/No answers (built
before 0.32, same code path as the check).
