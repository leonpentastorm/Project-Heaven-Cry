   cloud only blocks sight). Proposed for the lead: `hazard_immune` gas (a lighter mask than the gas mask's toxic).
2. **`blackpoint_wall_gun`** (waits, the lead's rule): no piece fires only while Prone (`requires_braced_or_prone` is
   braced OR prone). Left out of section 7 too.
3. **Signatures with no carrier** (each ships plain; its text promises nothing): G2 revolvers' best critical (needs a
   row-level `crit_min` gear field), G4 Legion (the family has only the bullpup's Shift technique), G11 Brigade's split
   burst, G13 ADCorp's cheaper Forge mods (needs a mod-fitting price factor per row or family), G14 Chezknov's Recoil
   on Bursts (a gun's own `recoil` has no mode limit and a row's modifiers do not reach `gearNum`; its number is not
   given either), G16 Smetanita's Pressure on a hit (the `resource` op carries it; no amount given), H3's burning
   ground, H5's line hit, H8's two targets, the door knocker's ×3 to cover (one grant a row), the throwaway tube's
   single use.
4. **Second-skill effects with no carrier** (item 8035): the drum launcher's Lob 3 to 2 AP (Lob is no cost site), the
   cathedral burner's wall +1 tile, the twin rotary's and the Blackpoint carbine's waived restrictions, the throwaway

   the darts' Toxin (no such condition), and every gun and bow second-skill effect (files 06 and 10 define none). The
   one carried: the siege tube's Raw Power 5 reload (`mod_siege_reload`).

## Calls I made (no number chosen; each reversible by the lead)

- **The grant rule** (item 8020): a grant is copied only if the engine runs its op and only from the row's own family
  (guns, bows, heavy: the family's base row; melee: the copy-from row when same family); not when it needs a mode the
  row lacks or contradicts a given number (the arbalest keeps Aimed 4, so no Bolt Rest). Without it, rows copied across
  families would carry another family's signature (an ADCorp rifle with the CCEC's crit range).
- **`ram_plate` and `barrier_wall`** have no copy-from row in the numbers file: they copy the Guard family's heavy form,
  the pavise, without its Plant.
- **`ork_snarl_knife`**: "the rip-saw's effects, the combat knife's shape" read as: shape = category, hand, AP,
  durability, mod slots, drill family; effects = properties, Rip, damage type, technique (Hamstring); props `light`,
  `edge`, not thrown.
- **`phys.props`**: the tables' `drawn` (a ranged property) and `polearm` (debt) are dropped from the draw blades, the
  spear and the cleaver-glaive (the validator's closed melee list).
- **`legion_spear`** lends Riposte (its "rest" is the sabre's, whose family's form lends it).
- **The arbalest** gets no `cycle_ap` (its copy-from crossbow has none; the stalking rifle copies the long rifle's).
- **Hoods' maker line `shadow`** (the tables' "Shadow line"); Strike `frontier`, Ward `science_team`.
- **Names**: the ruled nine; the rest the id in words. **Albert Town's grant name "Still Turn"** is a placeholder.
- **Ward's predicates**: `damage: [ranged, elemental]`, `melee: false` ("nothing from melee": an elemental melee blow is
  not soaked), `by_hit: true` (a Burning tick is not a hit), `with_this` (the holder's own defence with the shield in a
  hand).
- **Arsenal's −1 has no floor** (the lead gives none; every reload is 2 AP or more, so none is reached).
