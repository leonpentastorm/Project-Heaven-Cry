## Not built, and why

- **Tug, Bond, Snap on an enemy.** The verbs need an Attuner in hand (`self_holds`), and `self_holds` reads a character's
  gear: a unit with no character holds nothing. Needs a general reading of what a unit with no character holds (its
  row's hands); then the Empathy points (`doors.skills`), the MP (a soul caster's) and the `tend` profile at its level.
  Not attempted in this lane.
- **A Figment order.** No enemy Pictures (figments-spec section 8: the Visualist archetype is the casters pass's). The
  door step would run an order as any item action once an enemy holds a Figment.
- **Last Plate.** No such verb since 0.32 (it left the door; skills.json durability's own `source`).
- **Slam on a row.** The only Guard-holding row (the Heartfire elite) has Durability 3; Slam needs 5. Raising it is a
  balance call (its plates soak by the same points). Built: a row with `doors.skills: {durability: 5}` and a Guard
  would Slam (the step reads Slam's own target set: every foe next to it).
- **Retort on a row.** Built (a class move held by key) and taken off the Heartfire elite (item 7103).
- **Stalk.** Holdable, no want given: it builds a Shadow meter no enemy spends.

## Calls made for Leon (reversible)

1. The door step comes after an enemy caster's magic step (its Sigil and Soul steps stay first in the enemy turn): a
   Heart caster heals with its Outburst before it whispers, the Heartfire elite Challenges when its heart does not act.
   Moving the step before the magic would make the casters' doors far more frequent (a balance change).
2. Retort taken off the Heartfire elite (built and tested; one data line puts it back).
3. Patrons: the faithful tag keeps Holy Father Nerukhan; the Heart casters keep Mother Ember (the burning heart).
4. Patron tags, vows, Witnessed and interventions stay a character's: an enemy's prayer sheet earns no Devotion XP and
   brings no patron into the fight.
5. `answer_hold` set to 0.15 (was 0.5 in the first build: it cost four Bulwarks 4 to 5 clears).
6. Only a Soldier skulker wants Tag (a Minion's Tag cost it a shot: four Channelers +3 to +4); a Minion skulker holds
   Tag and Ghost Kill all the same (Ghost Kill works for it).
