## Not built, and why

1. **`run/power.ts FITS`** still reads row fields (`magazine`, `ammo`, `reload`, the melee category regex). Guns,
   magazine guns and heat guns are row fields, not tags; a tag for them would be a second copy of the field (refused
   by the design's own rule). The fits words stay the words; only their reading is per field. Melee could read `kind`
   tags; left as is to avoid a near-copy of `combat.ts meleeArm`.
2. **The shop faces' bias words** (`survival.json faces[].bias`: weapon, melee, thrown, focus, mana, item, core, mod)
   stay words: "weapon" (any weapon row, or a focus) and "item" are not tags. They now read the `kind` tag. Moving the
   faces' bias to tag queries in data is a data change for the lead to rule (it does not move a number).
3. **Client deletions** (`main.js` slot copies, the narrator's rarity list, `actionPickers.ts` explosive list): lane A3.
4. **The `torso_inner` spelling and its load mapping, `wornState`, the class rows as the one copy, class 6, N4, armour
   grades in `gearEffects`, `graded: false`:** lane A2 (spec items 7907 to 7909 are written for it).
5. **Seams left for A2:** a unique's effects held while WORN (N4 reads the same row field, `variant_mods`, of a worn
   piece); a body armour class row as a variants line's base (`variants.ts` comment at the base lookup; today a base is
   a weapons or items row, and `itemTags('armour:<n>')` already reads a class row's `family`, `form`, `line` once A2
   writes them).
6. **`skill` removed from the rows, `phys.teaches` that repeats its family's, the tags on the 72 rows:** lane A4 (my code
   runs on rows with no tags: every row is its own family; proved by the unchanged goldens and replays).
7. **A 0.33 old-save fixture:** not added. A1 adds no saved shape that is written (`GearState.weapon_art` is never
   written); `test/old-save.test.ts` is green unchanged. A2's `wornState` is the saved-shape change; its done-when has the
   fixture.
8. **The new forms' techniques** in the sample families: only the existing rows' techniques are on the families (Cavalry
   dex `riposte`, Axes hybrid `drag`, Axes str `sunder`); the Cavalry hybrid and str forms and the Axes dex form lend
   none from the family until stage B names one (no number or choice of mine).
9. **`data/CONVENTIONS.md`** does not yet describe `families.json` and the tag kinds (not in this lane's files).

  `FAMILY`, `FAMILIES`, `ITEM_TAG_KINDS`), and the unique's `fixedGradeOf` / `gradeFor`. `itemFamilyOf` is named so
  because `magic.ts` exports a `familyOf` (condition families).

- **`GearState.weapon_art`** (optional saved field, never written).
- **`oneHanded(w)`** in place of `ONE_HANDED` (the same count of exports).

(`LINT_RECORD=1` on `lint-item-ops` wrote no change).
