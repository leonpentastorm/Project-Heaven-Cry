# Perk and trait audit

Every perk (106) and every trait (74) was put through one standard fight, and what its card says was set against what the game did. This file is written by a tool (`packages/engine/test/perk-audit.test.ts`), so it can be run again after any fix:

```
PERK_AUDIT_OUT=docs/design/handoffs/perk-audit.md npx vitest run --root packages/engine test/perk-audit.test.ts
```

## Summary

| Shelf for the new card | Perks | Traits | Total | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| USE NOW | 40 | 20 | 60 | It has a button in a fight. |
| RIDES AN ACTION | 6 | 2 | 8 | No button of its own: a switch on another action (an attack, a move, Pump…). |
| ALWAYS ON | 54 | 44 | 98 | No button, no switch: it works by itself when something happens in a fight. |
| STORY ONLY | 6 | 8 | 14 | Nothing it does can matter in a fight: the Narrator runs it, or it only works on the map or in the story. |
| NOTHING | 0 | 0 | 0 | The game does nothing with it. |

**8 findings.** 85 fight buttons were pressed (a perk with two tiers is pressed at each tier): 42 ran in the plain fight, 2 ran only once the fight was primed, 20 were refused both times (most need something the yard doesn't have: a drone, a door, a bottle, an upper-realm being), 21 never showed their button, 0 crashed. No stand-in buttons are left: every button in the game was built on purpose. In the short fight, 43 of the 180 were seen at work (named in the log, or a number moved against a plain character).

How to read it:

- **The standard fight:** the holder with the skills the perk asks for, a soldier on the next tile, a squadmate on the other side, full AP, the holder's turn.
- **The primed fight** is the second try for a button the plain fight refused: everyone is hurt, the holder and the squadmate are Pinned, and the holder has a class (a Mage for a Mind perk, a Channeler for a Soul perk, a Fighter for a Body perk).
- **A stand-in button** is the one the game makes up when a perk's data says "this is an action" and nobody built that action: it takes the AP and the use, writes "X uses Y" and leaves the rest to the Narrator.
- **For perks with no button** the audit played eight beats (the fight starts, you are shot, you shoot back, you step one tile, a condition is put on you, the squadmate is dropped, you kill the soldier, your next turn starts) next to a plain character with the same skills and the same dice. "Nothing showed" means none of those beats named the perk in the log or moved a number; it does not mean the perk is broken. Many need a spell, a check, a scene or a certain enemy.
- **"Its card doesn't say that"** is a rough word match between the refusal and the card, so read those entries as leads.
- **What this cannot see:** the audit plays the rules, not the screen. "The card" here is what the rules hand the screen (the button's name, AP, uses and text). How the Perks tab draws it, and anything that only goes wrong in the browser, is not covered.

## Findings

1. These 4 buttons work in a fight, take their use and change nothing on the grid: they write one log line and leave a note for the Narrator, and in a fight the Narrator only reads its notes when the fight is over. To the player it looks like the button did nothing: **Established Fact**, **Closing Argument**, **Their Own Idea**, **Occultist** ("Look it up"). They belong on the STORY ONLY shelf during a fight, or need an answer the player can see.
2. **Wall Run** (Tier II) shows free on its button ("Wall Run strike") but took 3 AP (6 AP before, 3 after).
3. **Company Man** shows free on its button ("Call RTC extraction") but took 6 AP (6 AP before, 0 after).
4. **Flat Line** (Tier I) runs on the soldier and changes no number, condition or position: it only wrote "Tester 1 fixes Soldier with a flat stare (Flat Line).". That line is all the player gets: fine if the line is the whole point (information), a gap if it was meant to set something up.
5. **Daggerfall Veteran** runs ("Seen It Before"), takes its use and changes no number, condition or position: it only wrote "Tester 1 has seen it before: Soldier holds and goes for Tester 1, Squadmate.". That line is all the player gets: fine if the line is the whole point (information), a gap if it was meant to set something up.
6. **Sleight of Hand** could not be used ("Plant an item") in the plain fight or the primed one: "Nothing like that in your pack." Its card doesn't say that.
7. **Lunarshine** could not be used ("Drink") in the plain fight or the primed one: "No bottle." Its card doesn't say that.
8. These 10 are on the USE NOW shelf, but their button only shows up at its moment (after a catch, with a drone out, with a glyph on the ground…) and neither fight brought that moment up, so the button itself was never pressed: **Catch**, **Shadow Step**, **Jury-Rig**, **Rite in the Field**, **Clean Draw**, **Case Closed**, **Twin Discipline**, **Oil in My Mind**, **Oil in My Soul**, **Old School**. On the new card they read better as ALWAYS ON with a button that appears.
