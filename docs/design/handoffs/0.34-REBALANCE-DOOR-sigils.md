## 1. Built

- **Reach (item 8370).** `character.ts maxSigilWords` is Brain Power's Reach (`door.ts reachOfRes(skills, 'glyphs')`:
  2 / 3 / 4 / 5 at 1 / 4 / 7 / 10, the family gates of ruling 13) plus `max_sigil_words`; with no Brain Power the shortest
  Line (`grammar.min_glyphs`). Intelligence's `sigil_max_words` derived line is gone (item 8378). `sigils.json grammar`:
  `max_glyphs` 5 (the top Reach), `push_past` 1; `sigilwords.ts LINE_MOST` (6) is the structural limit (strokes, the
  grammar's absolute refusal, the stroke cap, the planners); `MAX_GLYPHS` stays the longest un-Overclocked Line. The
  word-cap refusal names the Brain Power point that raises it (`sigil.ts wordsAt`; the web's `shortWhy` reads it).
- **The Evoke on the cash-out (item 8371).** `sigil.ts lineCash(skills, n)` = Bite x S(n) at the door's step (no
  "1 +", the bible's formula); 1 with no Bite. `lineMultFor` reads the caster's character (or `mg.lineSkills`, written
  by `magicOf` through the new `door.ts doorSkillsOf`, for a unit with no character) and pays for at most `mg.maxWords`
  glyphs, so a stored spell longer than a new Reach goes off at the Reach's worth (ruling 39). The Margins add to it as
  before. `grammar.line_mult` (by_glyphs) is retired; `grammar.line_cash` holds the text (the glossary reads it).
- **One Subject (item 8372).** `grammar.repeats.other_share` 0 (`sigilwords.ts OTHER_SHARE`, the old typed `/ 2`); a
  damage type whose Subjects add nothing is no part (no 1-damage hit of its own). CONDENSE: `mult` 1, `per_copy.mult` 0,
  its inflection a new word stat `charge_free` (a Mastered CONDENSE costs 0 charge; `sigil.ts glyphCost`;
  `sigilgrowth.ts INFLECTION_STATS` and the validator's list gained it).
- **Words held (item 8373):** `grammar.known.base` (4) + Brain Power's `per_point.numbers.words` (now 3) a point.
- **Brain Power 2 / the Push (item 8374).** `charge_at` [2, 5, 9]. Overclock moved from `unlocks.2` into `unlocks.8`
  (the fork row now carries Overclock's two effects and `item: true`; its `uses` is `per_turn`; a `per_point` row "at 8"
  shows it on the ladder). The handler (`perks/skills.ts`) finds its unlock by its `grant_action` (no point typed), reads
  the extra charge from the row, caps at `LINE_MOST`, and on the Evoke of the Line holding the Overclocked glyph calls
  `magic.ts flux` to overload by one (the one overload path; Backdraft reads it as before). Keys renamed:
  `actions.json` free list, `art/catalog.json` data_ids, `lint-item-ops.baseline.json` (a rename, same count).
- **Rumination (item 8375):** `per_point.numbers` `feed` removed, `feed_flat` 1, `keeps` 1 from 10.
  `effects/feed.ts` accepts a beta with only a flat term; `magic.ts fluxMaxOf` = safe_max + `feedStep` (+ items).
  Rumination 10: `sigil.ts fadePattern(cb, p, keepFirst)` leaves the Line's first glyph (`door.ts keepsOf(.., 'glyphs')`).
- **Brief (item 8376):** `calls.json` Brief `aim: self-or-ally`, `self_step_less` 1, `feeds` {resource `flux`, `when:
  "given"`, from Eloquence 6 value -1, its log `line`}. `calls/core.ts`: a feed with `when: "given"` runs after the Call
  is made; `callFeed` gives a resource with a setter through `resGive` (Flux's setter). The vented Flux does not go into
  the air (the setter's negative path); see 2.6.
- **Forks (item 8377):** Overflux 1.1, Written in Advance 1.1 (as multipliers: A8's sum is not built).
- **Proving test:** `packages/engine/test/door-sigils.test.ts` (10 tests: the top on the load path at every row with and
  without the family; real Lines of 2 to 5 through the reducer equal the strongest Subject x lineCash; a long Line pays at

## 2. Not built, and why (for the lead)

1. **The Sigilist's ranks re-ordered** (Stored Spell and Barrage at 3, Bleed the Flux and Core Change at 5, Payload and
   Backdraft at 15): moving moves between ranks takes a move from a live Sigilist below the new rank (ruling 15/39);
   it needs the migration (step 6) and the shared rank levels (step 3). The dial at 1 (inert) is left.
2. **Sigils' Marks and gifts, the word Marks, the found-spell book** (S4, S9): step 3.
3. **The keeps tags** (a Line step keeps Momentum, a non-damaging Evoke keeps Shadow, Vent eases Strain, a turn of
   Guard / Whisper / Figment order keeps the glyphs' rounds): S6 contexts at other doors' wear sites; not reached.
4. **The Evoker's grade on a Line's damage** (A9): a focus's grade gives its door's to-hit (`focusAccTerm`) but has no
   damage half; the grades for foci are step 5. The seam: `sigilDamageTerms` has a free term slot beside `level`.
5. **An enemy caster's Margins by tier** (step 2b's item 8): not reached.
6. **Brief's vent into the air**: venting through the Flux setter lowers the Flux but does not raise the mana meter as
   Vent does. The bible says "vents"; whether the vented Flux goes to the air is for the lead (one line in the setter).
7. **Card `felt` lines** are not texts in `skills.json`; the Sigilist's `resource.shared_rule` text still says the 0.33
   Flux (6, 10 a point over): a text for step 5 (ruling 71).
8. **Overclock as a condition** (the bible's shape: a one-use condition with stat_mod and cost_mod, the handler gone):
   the handler was kept and moved (its key renamed, its numbers read from data); turning it into rows is a rebuild of a
   working piece, left for the consolidation pass.
