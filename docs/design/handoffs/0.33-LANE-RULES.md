## Not built, and why

- **Group F leftovers 1, 8, 11, 14** (spec 7013): 1 (forced moves on a Boss typed per item) is one rule in
  `forcedRefusal` that would move the golden forced-move table and Slam's "pushes a Boss" (a ruling); 8 is client fx code
  (the effects lane's); 11 is two tests calling inner functions (`open-hand-2 usable`, `shadow-2 hideBlock`); 14 is the
  enemy AI's search fallback (the enemy lane's). Group F 2, 10, 12, 17 were not checked.
- **Mastered perks that are Narrator-only** (Poker Face I, Old Tales, Between the Lines I, Foresight I, Rank Has
  Privileges): each tier I half is story judgement (a true answer, a meaning, a worst outcome, a reasonable order) with no
  trigger the engine can detect; their combat halves are engine already (Poker Face II's Brain saves, Between the Lines
  II's Bond TN, Foresight II's blow). Old Tales II's parley in a fight ("holds its attacks on its next turn") needs a
  condition that forbids every attack: no condition mod reads a general attack forbid today (`condtable.ts` reads only
  the reaction forbid). Listed under "Needs a new primitive" below; not built.
- **The sight checks still asked without the looked-at unit** (an ally's eyes on an ally, tile-only checks): the call
  made for Leon is that `seen_range` (Prone, Chain Smoker) is about a hostile's eyes only, so those checks stay as they
  are. No code changed.
- **Warding Word**: built in 0.32 as "the next Brain or Heart save" (PROVISIONAL); nothing owed beyond Leon's ruling.
- **Bare fists in the glove family (P11) and the thrown Relic property**: rules 1.77 item 6516 put both in Items 2.0
  ("not approved, left as they are"), so both need an Items 2.0 decision first. Not touched.
- **Per-sheet open items that are rules**: the solo Bulwark's rank II Second Wind fallback has no written design in the
  repo (the Endurance sheet names it only); the Guard cap of 4 against "+1 plate" is a balance number. Neither built.
- **Enemy use** of any of this (Hurl, Stand Behind Me, surprise on the AI's side beyond losing its turn): the enemy lane.
- **Balance fits**: the class dials (all 1); Athletics from 1 (it fails `test/balance/defence-measure`, 4.3 rounds
  against 4.2, the Survival guard seed, and moves the yardstick 65 to 60: the enemy table's refit, then the switch); the
  Operative under the Silent rule (then `hidden_attack.gives: tile`): the balance lane. Both switches ship OFF.
