## Not built, and why

- **A grade colour.** The brief says "a frame and colour"; the design's lead's call says "no new colour until the style
  guide gives grades one", and no grade colour exists in `palette.json` or `colour-system.md`. Built: the frame (2 px
  plus 1 a mark, the panel ink) and the word with its marks beside it everywhere. A colour per grade is a style
  decision handed back (see numbers).
- **The second skill on the card**: in the view-model (`secondSkill`, `built: false`); the card draws it only when an
  effect reads it (design item 10), so nothing shows this pass.
- **The Weapon Art line**: `weaponArt: null` in the view-model, not drawn (design section 6).
- **Body armour's family line**: the class rows name `family: vests` (A2) but `data/families.json` has no `vests` row
  (A4 did not add one), so body armour's card has no family name. A `vests` (and `plates`) family row needs its maker
  line, which no table gives: **for the lead**.
- **The picture chain's last step "a plain glyph for its kind"** is the general placeholder (the item's initials,
  `markIcon`) as the brief says, not a per-kind glyph (a per-kind glyph table would be a client copy of the kinds).
- **The sheet tip's durability "worn" word** (the client's `d <= m * 0.25`) was dropped rather than copied; the engine's
  `wearState` is not read there yet (BROKEN still shows).
- The pack rows of the sheet do not draw the grade frame (28 px rows); their tip names the grade.
