## Not built, and why
- **Sounds: a divine impact, a lightning sound apart from the thunder, and per-patron prayer sounds.** These need downloads. A downloaded file needs the user's explicit word, and a builder agent cannot get it. Nothing was downloaded.
- **`fieldRepair` bypasses `mend()`**: `mend` applies the receiver's healing multipliers and the gate. Rules 1.34 says a repair is not a heal. Routing it through `mend` would change a standing rule, so this needs a ruling: for example, a `repair` profile with no receiver multiplier.
- **Hallow and Into the Wall icons**: these are Wick move icons, not monoline line icons, so I cannot draw them. They stay on `art/briefs/ART-REQUESTS.md`.
- **Camera jump after an illegal far click** and **forks at camp on MAGIC**: neither was reproduced. The camera is the speed lane's plumbing.
- **LEVEL UP banner over the Forge**: not seen in the before pictures (1280-052..055).
- **Narrator's desk: the left sheet shows the guest's character**: this is by design (rules 1.80 item 6801: a Narrator with no character reads every sheet).
- **The bottom hint box over EQUIPPED (Proving Ground)**: it is the scroll edge of the middle column. The box has its own close.
- **Bond verbs in the effects audit**: touch, settle, soothe, tug, guide and raise are refused in the harness ("You don't have that"). Since rules 1.75 they are perks; the audit's Mender has no perks. That is the harness, not the game. Left for the effects audit owner.
