# 0.33 lane C: playtest bugs, the screen side (`wip/0.33c-bugs-ui`)

Builder's hand-off, 20 Nov 2026. Base: `origin/wip/release-0.33` at `1a4bffd05`. Source: five outside playtest reports of
live 0.32.0 (Durability, Raw Power, Dexterity, Ingenuity, Sigil mage), read as observations and reproduced on this branch
at 1280x720, 1280x800 and 1280x900 before anything changed. The reports are not copied into the repo. Pictures (not
committed): `.check/playtest-033b/before/` and `.check/playtest-033b/after/`. No rules changed, so no spec items were
used.

## The cause behind the HIGH reports

Four of the five testers played in a browser that keeps the page "visible" but draws no frames. In that browser,
`requestAnimationFrame` never fires and every CSS animation stays on its first frame. Anything that slides or fades in
from opacity 0 stays invisible. Reproduced two ways:

1. in the desktop app's hidden browser pane: `document.visibilityState` reads `visible`, no frame callback ran in
   1.5 s, the test animation stayed at `currentTime 0`, and the sheet's STATUS, PERKS, GEAR and MAGIC content measured
   opacity 0;
2. headless, with the page's frames stubbed out and its animations frozen through CDP.

In both, the sheet's tabs were blank (`before/frozen-sheet-*`). An action tab's panel was empty after a switch, opacity
0, offset 64 px (`before/frozen-fight-tab2`). The whole turn box was missing too.

This one cause explains these reports:

- the blank sheet tabs;
- the empty action panel after a tab switch;
- the level-up choosers clipped under the sticky header (at 1280x800 with frames, the fork and perk choosers are fully
  in view, `before/r1-levelup-*`);
- most of the 1280x900 overlaps: "ATTACK & DEFEND" losing its A, FREE ROAM over MANA FORECAST, YOUR TURN and MOMENTUM
  over the You card. A slide-in frozen at its start sits 64 px off its place;
- the portrait's Close not closing, because its out animation waits on a timer that page throttles;
- the map hover tip lagging, because the hover resolves on a frame.

With frames drawn, none of these happens. The other cause behind (b) is real in any browser (see below).

  the frozen frame.

  4000 close, which the room sends when the same player connects from a second tab.
- **Pad buttons with an empty accessible name**: not reproduced. On the Lever carbine pad every chip carries
  `aria-label` ("Aimed Shot, 3 AP, 1 round"), and no button anywhere on the fight screen had neither a name nor

- **The enlarged portrait's Close**: works on click. In a page with no frames it now closes at once (see the first
  row of the Built table).
- **The splitter collapses the Turn panel**: not reproduced. Dragging the splitter to either edge and shrinking the
  window to 1000 px keeps the aside at its minimum (`fight-panel` checks the split by share).
- **Narrow windows push Save and Main menu off screen**: not reproduced in the fight down to 560 px. Both stay in
  view.
- **The sticky tab row hides scrolled buttons**: the tab row is no longer sticky on this branch.
- **The Techniques toggle stays pressed after a tab switch**: by design. The pick holds across tabs within a turn, and
  the tab that holds it wears a white dot. The toggle's pressed state is the chosen action. It now clears at the next

  yourself or an adjacent ally" and no foe card.

## Not built, and why

- **"The Narrator decides the rest." on Challenge and Slam.** The flag is honest about the engine. The Durability
  door's item is bound, so `defaultActions` feeds the door's passive `roll_with` into every move's run. `defaultRun`
  cannot run it, so it posts a Narrator note and says "uses Guard" (the log line the Durability report saw). Fixing
  this means changing what a bound item's actions run, which is engine behaviour and could move saves. This is for the
  engine bug lane: `own` in `defaultActions` should leave out the item's passive ops (those with a trigger `when`).
- **CALLS wrapping at 1280x900**: not seen with frames drawn (six tabs fit on one row, `before/r2-phantom-fight-*-900`).
  It was most likely the frozen entrance.
