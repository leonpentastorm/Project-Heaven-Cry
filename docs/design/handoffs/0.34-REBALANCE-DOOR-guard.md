## 2. Not built, and why (for the lead)

1. **Overbrace (the Push at Durability 8).** The bible gives `reach_plus` 1 and `hp_share` 0.1 but **no AP price**
   and no duration (S5 says "+1 for one action"; the price text says "at the start of your next turn when the extra
   plate was spent"). Both are numbers/rules I may not choose. Shape ready: a grant at `self_skill durability 8` laying
   a condition with `stat_mod res_max:guard +1`, and at `turn_start` a self `deal` of `self:max_hp` x 0.1 (true, nothing
   soaks) if the plates held are under the raised top.
2. **Hold Their Eyes** (a Taunted foe's blow gives a plate, once a round from each foe): no predicate reads "the actor
   has condition X" (`cond` reads the event's condition). **Needs a new primitive** (an `actor_has_cond` predicate) or a
   ruling; `limit {per: per_round, each: actor}` exists for the once-a-round-each.
3. **Reforge in its new form** (fill to half your Reach when holding half or less): "half" of an odd Reach (3, a gated
   7) is not rounded in the bible. Today's row (+2 at 1 or less) kept.
4. **Rank levels 3, 5 and 15** (ruling 12): the class rank gate is shared by all nine classes (`classes.json`
   thresholds, the class code); not a door's lane. Ranks still open at today's thresholds.
5. **Solo forms of Interpose, Cover Them and Stand Behind Me** (ruling 32): each redirects a hit on an ally; their
   solo halves ("a plate soaks with nothing kept back", "soaked with your reaction instead of a plate", "one plate
   fewer than it needs") are new rules in the soak path (`guard.ts guardSoakOf`). Not reached.
6. **Guard's keeps tags** (Challenge on a Pressed foe holds its Pressure; Slide and Head Down shed Pressure without a
   plate; Duck and Roll keeps plates on the step) belong to the Pressure door's wear site (`pressureFilter`, S6); left
   for the Pressure lane.
7. **Guard's Marks and gifts** are step 3 (S4).
8. **Drive's plate** at most 2 a turn (body.json `builds`): today's Drive gives a plate a floored foe with no cap; the
   cap is a `limit` on its row: not reached.
9. **The card's felt lines** (`felt` in body.json per point) are not in `skills.json` texts; the Durability and Pain
   Threshold cards read their numbers already.

## 3. Numbers the bible lacked (nothing chosen)

2. Reforge's rounding of half an odd Reach (2.3).
3. Slam's "foe you face" when the grant names no target: the first foe beside you in the fight's order (the grant has
   no `reach`, so the screen sends no target; a target named beside you is used).

   the hit; if the mark should reach it, the weight moves inside `attackDamage` (a `plus` on the amount, which
   `closeAmount` already has). For the lead.
