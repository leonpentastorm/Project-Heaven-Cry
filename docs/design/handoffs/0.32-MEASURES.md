## 1. The Sigilist and the Operative: the cause, and what the fix reaches

**Sigilist.** Turn logs of PF-1 to PF-8 (`PF_TRACE=1`, new: every fight action with HP, AP, tile, charge, cores, Flux,
the refusal and the fight log it wrote). Causes, in order of cost:
1. It walked its Lines through blades: every step beside a melee foe drew an opportunity attack from each one (four rats
   took 16 to 24 HP a step); then the rats scuffed the glyphs.
2. Four-glyph Lines (about 180 at level 1) at 15-HP rats, a whole turn each; one Evoke in 9 draws.
3. Lines split over turns: the core was swapped mid-Line or after the last glyph with no AP (`Needs 2 AP`), so the Line
   waited, was scuffed, or faded at the 5-glyph cap when the next one started.
4. Lines drawn where the target had cover (SEEK rolls against cover: about half missed).
Fix (`run-policies.ts sigilRun`; `policies.ts sigil` gained an opt-in `noCover`): no step beside a blade (the pistol at
the weakest, Clean Vent first); small game shot; the Line as long as the target needs (its number times what the last
Evoke dealt for its number) and as the turn pays; the core swapped before the Line; Lines placed with no cover between
any glyph and the target when one is in reach; every Line drawn straight away from the target and fired back along it
(`SIG_ALONG`, measured: 0 vs 2 of 40 either way); Overclock when Max Sigil words is under four.

|---|---|---|
| party-fit solo / quad, clears of 40 | 0 / 0 | 2 / 3 |
| depth reached, solo | 3.0 | 6.8 |
| Evokes a run (solo) | 1.9 | 4.4 |
| survival `solo_sigil` (normal share) / `four_sigil` | 5 / 2 | 2 / 3 |
| HP removed from foes a character-turn in Survival (new column) | - | 48 (Channeler 92, Skirmisher 125) |
| range line, the contract's own Sigil policy, all in, L1/5/10/20 (refit `sigil4`) | - | 154 / 179 / 218 / 204% (Outburst steady 152 / 151 / 138 / 138%) |

So on the range the Line is at or above the Outburst line (Leon's playtest); in Survival the bot reaches half the
Channeler's damage a turn and takes the most damage a turn of any caster (14.2 HP). What is left is the AP of the Line
itself under Pressure (Suppress makes each step 2 AP: a 2-glyph Line then costs most of a turn) and its walking into
open lanes: see section 4, item 2.

**Operative.** Turn logs of three seeds: it Broke at 1 Shadow the turn it hid; Breaks missed about half the time
(Dexterity 0: d20 + 0 against 11, and the foe's cover: 30 of 132 Breaks); its camp gun (the Recommended Parlour pistol:
16 damage, two rounds, loaded round by round) spent 2 AP of every 6 reloading; seen with nowhere to hide, it reloaded and
shot from where it stood. Fix (`door-policies.ts operative`): a Break that drops a foe at once (a kill unseen is a
Ghost Kill), else Shadow built to 2 (Stalk, a turn ended Hidden) then Tag and Break on the foe that takes most; a better
unseen angle when the shot is under 60% (rarely found: 1 in 40 tries); Fade with 2 Shadow; the gun when it can't hide.
`OP_BREAK_AT` 1, 2, 3: 0, 0, 0 of 40.

The cause is not only the bot: an all-in Ingenuity sheet has no weapon skill (Ingenuity 7, Vigilant 3, Dexterity 0),
and nothing on the Shadow door adds to hit except Tag's +1. With every Break played well it removes a quarter of what
the Skirmisher does. A ruling for the lead (section 4, item 1).

## 2. Policies brought to the 0.32 ladders (`door-policies.ts`, `door-builds.ts DOOR_PICKS`)

The picks are data (`DOOR_PICKS`: forks at 4 and 8, the four-perk betas and gammas); each door plays `door_<base>`, a
copy of its base build with the picks, so the contract's and the golden tables' builds are untouched.
- Bulwark: Provoke then Drive; Close Guard (now Pain Threshold's) first; Challenge and Slam as before; Retort fires by itself.
- Breaker: Grindstone then Spill; Grapple (with 2 Momentum and the AP for the throw) then Throw Down (3 Momentum) on a
  held foe; Charge priced as any technique (no free Charge since 0.32). Grapple about 5 a run, Throw Down about 1.
- Skirmisher: Walk It In then Lockdown; Slide; Flush Out with AP no shot pays for.
- Rigger: Sharp Light then Many Hands; forms as the build lists them.
- Mender: Short Thread then Long Thread; Pull Together bought first (Tug is a Camaraderie perk in 0.32: `perk:pull_together#tend_tug`); Shield (Empathy 5) once a turn on an ally it can't Mend.
- Channeler: Slow Burn then Clean Break. No Mana Short in 160 traced fights (the policy never pours over the limit).
- Chaplain: Round of Blessings then Benediction; Name the Sinner on an Elite or a Boss only (naming the nearest foe every
  turn cost the solo Chaplain 15 to 5 clears of 40; `CH_SINNER=0` turns it off: 15 of 40). New: the sparing Chaplain
  (`chaplainSparing`): prays only with an Elite or Boss in the fight, three foes or more, or someone under half HP.

## 4. What looks unfun or broken (priority order)

1. **The Operative can't clear solo or in four** (0/40 and 0/40; 3/40 with the rack's rifle). It removes 13.6 HP a turn
   (Skirmisher 124.8). Its Breaks land 52 to 55% at Dexterity 0; the Shadow door gives no to-hit but Tag's +1. The bot is
   no longer the limit (Break thresholds 1 to 3, angles, Fade: all 0/40). Ruling needed: a to-hit on the door (Ingenuity
   on a Break's roll, as Durability rolls a Guard holder's blows), or the archetype's sheet.
2. **The Sigilist barely clears** (2 solo, 3 quad), at half the Channeler's damage a turn in Survival while its range
   line is above the Outburst's. In fights it is Pressed (each step 2 AP) and walks open lanes; it takes 14.2 HP a turn.
   The bot can still improve (a Line drawn from cover, a stand-off from Suppress) before any number moves.
3. **Quads that do worse than their solo:** Breaker 19 solo → 6 quad, Bulwark 25 → 8 (6 of 40 stalled, 16.7 HP removed
   a turn). Every other door does better in four.
4. **The Mender starves solo:** 10/40 with 16 stalled (it melees an Elite with an empty revolver for 20 rounds); 19 HP a turn.
5. **The two-shield wall outclears every door but the Skirmisher solo** (36/40, rank I) on a Buckler that deals nothing (57 HP a fight).
6. **The Outburst's biggest Release all in is 191 to 226% from L6** (was 96 to 113): the peak far above every steady line.
7. **Reflex measures nothing** (identical with its stat at 0, in the rush too): either the off switch no longer reaches it
   (the 0.32 class rows) or it never fires; the follow strike's −2 and the feeds' 0.01 move nothing measurable either.
8. **The sparing Chaplain is worse solo** (5 vs 11) and better in four (30 vs 26): praying only when needed starves a lone Chaplain.
