## Not changed (reproduced as correct, or by rule)

- **3, Guard spends 1 Momentum:** not reproduced. Through the reducer and the descriptor, at Momentum 0, 1 and 2, in
  and out of the approach, with a buckler: the preview reads "Momentum n → n" and the Guard technique spends none
  (techniques.json `guard` is a Link; `momentumCost` charges Strikes and Openers only). Test "item 3".
- **4, Brace in the approach:** by spec item 5011 an action that is not the approach's joins the fight first and is made
  only on that unit's turn (else "moves to act" in the log). On this branch the surprise round (rules 1.82 item 7004)
  gives the unseen squad the first turn, so Brace is made. Test "item 4". If the screen's Confirm sends something else,
  it is the UI lane's.
- **6, melee "Attack an object" on a barrel:** not reproduced: the descriptor sends `combat/shoot-cover` with the barrel's
  tile and the reducer smashes it (hand wraps and the Ork machete, square and diagonal). "Nothing there to shoot." is
  the reducer's answer to a tile with no live object: the screen sent another tile. UI lane.
- **9, the Lifted magazine and the drop vanish:** by rule. In a run a foe drops materials only (rules 1.54 item 3); the
  Lifted magazine stays on the field and is taken with the field's pickup (test "item 9"); the field's piles left
  untaken stay behind (rules 1.55, item 36's "the field's piles stay behind"). The field's Take all takes the pile at or
  next to your unit only: walking to the others is the design; the screen could say how many piles are left (UI lane).
- **11, a gun emptied in one fight starts the next empty: INTENTIONAL** (Leon, 20 Nov 2026: "no empty gun keep it like
  that it's intentional"). Nothing changed, no top-up built. Spec item 7414.
- **17, Echo in a fight: not built.** The spec (one line, the body's "Echo") does not define what a fight needs: which
  d20s (attacks, saves forced on you in a foe's turn, death saves, each roll of a Burst), when the choice comes (the
  reducer resolves a roll and what it sets off, a jam, an Answer, a Riposte, in one action: a reroll after seeing it
  needs a held roll the engine does not have), what a reroll undoes (a natural 1's jam and Heat), and how a Survival run,
  which has no sessions, refills Echo. Spec item 7415 lists it for Leon. The sheet's "in a fight: not yet" stays true.

## Calls made for Leon (reverse any)

1. **The follow strike and a fumble** (item 7401): a follow comes after a hit or a miss (your design), never after a
   natural 1; the log says which.
2. **Essence at the altar is not the shrine's one thing** (item 7413): consuming it leaves the rite, lore or token open;
   the Essence's own once-a-rest limit still holds.
3. **Rigid from the first fight** (item 7408): with no rest yet, the words known as the mission begins are the practised
   ones (at most Max Sigil words).
4. **Ghost Kill reads "seen" as well as "in view"** at the roll (item 7405).
5. **`gear/ammo` returns the gun's rounds** to the pack as their own type (item 7406); loading "standard" back still
   only switches the type (unchanged; a loaded special round switched back is read as standard: not touched here).

## For Leon (the tester's numbers; nothing changed)

- **Slam vs Shield Bash:** Slam 2 AP + 2 Guard, no damage, a 1-tile push and Off Balance; Shield Bash 1 AP + 1 Guard,
  30 damage (44 on a crit with a head injury), a push and +1 Momentum.
- **The 0 Guard accuracy cliff:** hand wraps 80% to hit while holding Guard (Durability 7's roll), 40% at 0 Guard.
- **Guard and Brace overlap:** two full-Guard buttons with overlapping cost and cooldown read as confusing early.
- **A plain Hit locks out Charge** ("An Opener is only the first melee technique of your turn"): no gap-closing Charge
  after clearing an adjacent rat.
- **Stage 1 finisher starvation:** the Ork machete's plain Hit deals 70 to 20-70 HP rats, so Momentum rarely reaches 2
  with AP left; swarm hits drain 0.88 Momentum a hit at Guts 2.
- **XP pace to level 3:** 35 XP a Stage 1 fight; 105 / 250 XP after Stop 5 of 15, short of the Stop 8 gate.
- **Early prices:** a Stop 2 Forge Standard → Fine at 1,200 credits against 120 in the purse; 6 Parlour pistol rounds ran
  dry on one 5-armour Trooper; 27 gunpowder + 27 casings with no Forge to make rounds.
