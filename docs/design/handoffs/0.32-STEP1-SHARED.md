## Needs a new primitive

None was built beyond the list. Open points for the lead:
1. **E, the 80% cap:** the brief says gear may take elemental resistance to 100%; Leon's words and the ruling layer say
   80% in all. Built as 80% in all (one number, `rules.json combat.elemental_taken_cap`). No armour or gear gives
   elemental resistance today (sources: Pain Threshold, two traits at 10%), so nobody passes 70%.
2. **D, Composure's feed retired:** the Strain limit is a step table now, so Composure is no beta feed of Will Power
   (its per-point row is "+1 Strain limit", `grows: strain_limit`, shown 1 to 8 with (max) at 8; it also shows on 3, 5
   and 7, where nothing moves: `ladderCaps` gives only the last point). The Determination step decides Composure's page.
3. **F:** forms known are chosen on the level-up screen (a TO CHOOSE chip per door that is no fork) and on the Figments
   tab; Visualization has no fork step until Expression's step.
4. **P4:** `seen_range` is read by the side's sight (fog, `sideSees`, `sideSeesUnit`, the shadow sight words, the
   seen-by-foes test). About 70 direct `unitSees(cb, u, tile)` calls (a perk's own sight checks) still ask without a
   target and do not hear it.

## What the nine attribute builders need

**The perk remap table (C).** On the skill's row in `data/skills.json` (betas and gammas only; an alpha needs none):

```json
"perk_remap": { "rev": 1, "perks": { "used_to_the_pain": "steady_nerves", "scar_tissue": null }, "passives": { "3": "numb", "5": "still_standing" },
                "note": "0.32 Endurance step: Numb and Still Standing became perks; Used to the Pain is removed" }
```

`perks`: every old acquired perk of the skill that is renamed (to its new id) or removed (`null`; a removed perk with
no replacement is dropped and its step owed again). A perk kept as it is needs no line. `passives`: each named passive
that was free at those points and is now one of the four perks. Every target is an acquired perk of the same skill
(the validator checks). Bump `rev` if a later change remaps again. On load, a save with 3+ points in the skill holds
the old perks mapped (tiers kept), then the passives, as far as its perk steps reach, and gets one free re-pick.

**The schedule (B).** `skills.json ladder` holds the points; an unlock row with `mastery` 1-4 must sit on the schedule's
step (the validator checks: an alpha's unlock at 5 has `mastery` 0 now). A beta or gamma may list two or four acquired
perks; nothing in code counts them.
