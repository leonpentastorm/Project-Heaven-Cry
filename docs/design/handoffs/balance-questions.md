# Balance pass: the questions for Leon (11 Nov 2026)

Stage A measured the game and changed no numbers. These are all the questions it raised that only Leon can answer. **Each has a recommendation, and the recommendation is what gets built if no answer comes** (Leon: "build whatever does not need me approving if I reply late"). Answer by number, e.g. "A2 no: …"; anything not mentioned takes its recommendation.

## Leon's answers (11 Nov 2026)

Everything not named here takes its recommendation.

- **A1 (what "3 turns of focus" means):** the recommendation: 3 rounds under fire from half the enemies of a standard fight for four (2 soldiers, or 1 elite and 1 soldier).
- **A2 (where the optimised gun's 150% comes from):** a gun is physical too, **so its ceiling is a bit lower than 150%**. Only weapons with ludicrously expensive ammunition, like a rocket launcher, get the full 150%. The rest as recommended: base damage per weapon family is settled first (the rifle family within about 20% of the yardstick at the same AP), and techniques, perks and quality carry the rest. *Reading, to confirm in the damage step:* ordinary guns top out at about 135 to 140% optimised; the 150% is kept for launcher-class weapons whose price is the ammunition.
- **A3 (should a ranged technique ever beat the plain shot):** yes, by 10 to 25% when its condition is met, and one shooter can meet Dead Centre's condition alone.
- **A5 (Momentum drained by being hit):** yes: a hit costs Momentum at most once a round (settled in the resources step).
- **B3 (Burst against the plain Shot):** Burst is **better in damage and worse to hit**. It may out-damage the Shot for its AP; it pays in accuracy.
- **C2 (the 90% target):** restated as "no rule is ever left to the Narrator". The three labels stay; the report shows the label share and the effect-line share side by side.

## A. The yardsticks (from the fight simulator)

Read with: docs/design/handoffs/balance-sim-baseline.md.

A1. What is "3 turns of focus"? One enemy's turns, or rounds in which a group shoots one character? The two disagree: one soldier needs 3.9 turns at level 1, but four soldiers drop the same character in round 1.6. Recommendation: 3 rounds under fire from half the enemies of a standard fight for four (2 soldiers, or 1 elite and 1 soldier).
A2. Is the 150% for "optimised physical" meant to come from the weapon or from play? Today the gun gets 173 to 215% from the long rifle's base damage alone, and its techniques add nothing. Recommendation: settle base damage per weapon family first (rifle family within about 20% of the yardstick at the same AP), then let techniques, perks and quality carry the rest to 150%.
A3. Should a ranged technique ever beat the plain shot for its AP? Today none does unless the target is Pressed, so a gun build's best turn is three plain shots. Recommendation: yes. Each technique should beat the plain shot by 10 to 25% when its condition is met, and one shooter should be able to meet Dead Centre's condition alone (for example Suppress, then Dead Centre, over two turns).
A4. Which reading of the Sigil's "150% on average over the fight" do you want measured: a fight of the target length (4 to 6 rounds) or 8 turns? The report now shows both (47 to 90% over the first 4 turns, 58 to 111% over 8). Recommendation: the first 4 to 6 turns, since that is the fight the other targets describe.
A5. Should enemy fire drain the Fighter's Momentum as hard as it does (empty on 50 to 90% of turns under fire)? Recommendation: a hit costs Momentum only once a round, or never below 1; decide it in the resources step (angle G), not now.
A6. Is a tank meant to be able to wear Class 5 plate with a riot shield? Today that needs Strength 6, which an Endurance build does not have. Recommendation: leave the weights; decide in angle E whether the Defender class should raise the carry limit.
A7. Do you accept the reference builds' assumptions as the yardstick for the whole pass: drill mark I, II and III by levels 3, 6 and 9; Sigil word picks 0/2/4/6/8/14 by level; Devotion 3/3/5/7/7/10; Masterwork gear for the optimised builds; armour never heavier than the build can carry and walk 1 tile an AP in? Recommendation: yes for now; they are one line each to change and the report re-runs in 10 minutes.
A8. Should the optimised gun be re-run with the hardest-hitting weapon the rules allow (breacher tube, longbow) as a second "worst case" row? Recommendation: yes, as a separate row for the ceiling check only, after the enemy table exists; the long rifle stays the reference for the ratios.

## B. The AP economy (from the action price list)

Read with: docs/design/handoffs/action-price-list.md.

B1. Is "1 AP = half a plain attack" the yardstick you want? Recommendation: yes. It holds for techniques, class moves and Sigil spells (the middle one pays 1.0 to 1.1 times), so it is a fair ruler for the weapon side.
B2. Should talk be measured with the same ruler? Your answer 13 says a gamma point is worth about half an alpha point in direct effect. Recommendation: yes, the same ruler, with the target for a Call at 0.5 times at 1 point rising to 1.0 times at 7 points. Today it rises to between 1.5 and 3.0 times.
B3. Should Burst stay better than the plain Shot per AP? Recommendation: no. Either Burst goes to 2 rolls on every weapon, or the third roll costs a 4th AP.
B4. Should the 3-turn cooldown also cover the AP sources that are not perks (Rally, Brief, Order, QUICKEN)? Recommendation: no cooldown for them. Put Order and QUICKEN inside the same +3 AP a turn that Rally and Brief already obey.
B5. Is the walk between glyphs meant to be part of a Sigil's price, making Agility matter to a Mind caster? Recommendation: keep it, since it is the system's identity, but let the first step of a Line be free so a 2-glyph spell fits beside a move.
B6. For rows that wait for a trigger (Overwatch, Riposte, Killzone), may the fight simulator measure how often they fire before they are judged? Recommendation: yes. I left them unflagged until then.
B7. Is a point of HP worth the same healed as dealt? Recommendation: yes, keep it equal. The enemy table should then be set so an enemy attack is worth about 24 HP, which makes both ways of counting agree.

## What the measurements say, in short

**The yardsticks (from the fight simulator)**

1. Enemies die in far fewer attacks than the targets, at every level. The yardstick (service rifle, normal share) needs, in hits that land: level 1: minion 1, soldier 2, elite 3, boss 6; level 20: minion 1, soldier 1, elite 2, boss 4. Target: 2, 3 to 4, 6 to 8, about 20. Enemies have no levels, so the gap widens as characters grow.
2. Fights are a third to a half of the target length, and squad size hardly matters. A squad of four wins the standard fight (3 soldiers and 1 elite) in 1.5 to 2.4 rounds on open ground and 2.0 to 3.4 with cover; the boss fight in 1.4 to 3.2 and 1.7 to 3.3. Targets: 4 to 6 rounds, boss 8 to 10. From level 12 the optimised gun alone wins the standard fight 100% of the time, in about 2 rounds.
3. Damage a turn against the plain rifle (100%), normal share, on the shooting range over 8 turns, lowest to highest across levels 1 to 20: optimised gun 173 to 215% (target 150%); optimised blade 105 to 152% (target 150%); Sigil 58 to 111% (target up to 150%); Outburst 66 to 98% (target 125%); Faith 69 to 92%; plain blade 87 to 99%; tank 52 to 73%; Calls leader alone 20 to 30%. Under fire the picture is the same, except the optimised blade swings wider (94 to 160%) and the tank drops to 37 to 61%.
4. The optimised gun is strong because of its weapon, not its techniques. With the same sheet and gear it used no technique at all on the range: no ready technique beat the 2 AP plain shot for its AP. Its lead is the long rifle's base damage (50 against the service rifle's 30, at the same AP a shot). The optimised blade's techniques add 9 to 34% over its own plain cuts.
5. The gun's dearest move was never made by one shooter. Dead Centre needs a Pressed target (2 Pressure); the reference gun has no Suppress ready and its hits give 1 Pressure a turn. It was used 0 times in 120 peak fights. In a squad it does get used.
6. Biggest single hits in a fight: 487 (Sigil Line STONE·FIRE·CONDENSE·SEEK, critical, level 20 all in), 460 (Coup with 4 Momentum, critical), 448 (long rifle plain shot, critical), 386 (Outburst stage 4, critical). Nothing passed 500 in a fight, and nothing passed 350 without a critical. On the engine's card with the price fully met, criticals do pass 500: Dead Centre 663, Coup at full Momentum 538.
7. A critical multiplies the yardstick's damage by 2.4 at level 1 and 2.9 at level 20 (target 1.5, to 2 with perks and gear). Criticals were 6 to 25% of the weapon builds' damage.
8. Hit chance of the yardstick's first shot at 4 tiles, open ground: level 1: minion 70%, soldier 60%, elite 55%, boss 50%; level 20: 95%, 90%, 85%, 80%. Target: about 65% at equal level. Enemy Defense never moves. Sustained rifle fire then loses accuracy to Heat (1 to hit per 2 Heat): the rifle's to-hit bonus fell from +3 to 0 by about turn 6 in the fight I watched.
9. One enemy cannot threaten a character; a group can at low level. A plain enemy hit before armour is minion 20, soldier 30, elite 28, boss 25, so a boss hits softer than a soldier. One soldier needs 3.9 of its turns to drop the level 1 yardstick, one boss 7.9; at level 20 an elite needs 27 and a boss 52. With 4 soldiers on it, the yardstick goes down in 100% of fights at level 1 (round 1.6), 80% at level 9 (round 2.4) and 0% at level 20.
10. The tank, now in armour it can carry (Class 4 from level 3), takes 18 to 62% of the rifleman's damage from an elite per enemy turn. It deals 52 to 73% of his damage on the range and 37 to 61% under fire. Under fire it spends 8 to 24% of its AP walking and about 20% on Guard. The old report's "42% of its AP walking" was the overburdened build.
11. Class 5 plate cannot be worn with a riot shield by a normal tank. The kit weighs 43.6 kg and needs Strength 6 to carry; the reference tank has Strength 4 (35 kg).
12. The Fighter's Momentum is drained by enemy fire. It was empty at the start of 50 to 90% of its turns under fire, against 28 to 40% on the range. Every hit taken costs 1; only a plain cut that lands gives 1; a greatsword makes 2 cuts a turn. A Finisher was used 44 times in 120 peak fights.

**The AP economy (from the action price list)**

1. The yardstick holds for weapons, not for talk. The middle action pays 1.0 times the yardstick for melee techniques, ranged techniques and Sigil spells, and 1.1 times for class moves. Calls at 1 and 3 points pay 0.5 times; Soul Outbursts pay 0.5 times. Part of the Call result comes from the conversion itself: anything that goes through a save is halved, and +1 to hit counts as a thirteenth of an attack.
2. Burst is better than the plain Shot on the yardstick rifle. For 3 AP it rolls three times (60%, 55%, 45%), against one roll at 65% for 2 AP. That is 1.6 times the damage per AP, paid with 2 more rounds and 2 more Heat. It breaks proposed target 2 of the AP angle (nothing strictly better than the plain attack).
3. Calls flip from the dearest thing in the game to the cheapest at 7 points. Every Call is dear at 1 and 3 points (2 AP for about 1 AP of effect). At 7 points the price halves while the effect grows: Brief pays 3.0 times the yardstick, Rally 2.5, Spot 1.8, Order 1.5.
4. Guard is the cheapest defence. The Guard technique costs 1 AP for 4 Guard at Durability 4 (40 HP soaked) plus 2 Defense against melee: 3.7 times the yardstick. The Defender's Brace gives the same Guard for 2 AP. The basic Brace gives 2 Guard for 1 AP to anyone with Durability. The pool holds only your Durability points (4 here) and Guard can be used once a turn.
5. Healing is cheap only for the Bard and for stims. Stim Throw: 51 HP for 1 AP and 1 Supply (3.9 times). Rally: about 34 HP to each of 3 allies for 2 AP, once a scene (3.9 times). A plain stim: 30 HP for 1 AP (2.3 times). Sigil heals and shields sit at the yardstick once the walk and the missing Empathy are counted (MEND + SEEK 1.0 times, WARD + SEEK 1.2 times). The earlier claim that healing and shields are the cheapest things in the game was partly a method error.
6. A Sigil Line costs a walk, and that makes Agility a hidden Sigil stat. FIRE + SEEK is 3.4 AP at Expression 4 and Agility 4 (two glyphs at 0.7, 1 AP of walking, 1 to Evoke) for 65 damage: 0.9 times the yardstick. The data file's own example puts SPARK + SEEK at 4 AP at Agility 1 and Expression 1. A character with 4 Intelligence holds 3 words in a Line, so the 4-glyph Line is out of reach.
7. A Soul Damage Bolt is dear at every stage when cast in one turn: 25 damage for 3 AP at stage 0, 0.4 times the yardstick. In play the first Pump of a fight is free and Empathy 3 gives one free Pump a round; with both, a stage 2 Bolt did 43 damage for 3 AP. So the Outburst is priced through free Pumps and holding, not through its listed AP.
8. Two once-a-fight moves are far above the rest. Hymn II Lightning Rod does 68 and 33 damage with no roll for 3 AP at Faith 4 (2.7 times); it needs Devotion 5 and a round's wait before the next Hymn. Earthbreaker hits everyone on 3 by 3 tiles for full melee damage with no roll and knocks them Prone with no save, for 4 AP (2.0 times with two enemies). The peak rule may allow both, but neither is its class's dearest move.
9. AP sources, confirmed in code. At most over a 5-round fight: Sweet Talker II 15 AP (once a turn), Brief 15 AP and Order 15 AP for an ally, then Grim Reaper, Got You in My Sight, Overruled!, Talks Broke Down, Forlorn Hope and Rally at 10 AP each. With the 3-turn cooldown the +2 perks fall to 4 AP and Sweet Talker II to 6. Rally and Brief already count inside the ally's +3 AP a turn. Order, QUICKEN, free Pumps and held shots do not, and the cooldown ruling does not name them.
10. Today's weak enemies distort every defensive row. A soldier's shot is worth about 10 HP to a character (15 damage at 70%), while a character's rifle shot is worth 26 HP to a soldier. Rows counted as "an enemy attack prevented" (Disengage, Draw Fire, Foreknowing, Sanctuary) are therefore worth about 0.4 of what is listed today. They become right once the enemy table puts an enemy attack near 24 HP. Rows counted in HP (heals, shields, Guard) are right today.
11. Data and engine disagreements, unchanged from the first report: Unbroken (once a scene in data, once a fight in text); Reactive Plate (a reaction in data, no reaction in text); Hide costs 2 AP in code and is missing from the action list; 8 perk and trait buttons have an AP only the code works out. New: Last Round's card text does not say its +50% is for revolvers only.
12. The Fighter's Finisher is worse than the Coup technique anyone can learn. Finisher: 3 AP, +20% per Momentum, 62 damage at 3 Momentum against a plain 37. Coup: 2 AP, +25% per Momentum, 58 damage at 2 Momentum. This is the clearest "expensive but not powerful enough" case under the peak rule.
