## Not built, and why

- **Echo does not reroll the ambush.** The Echo reroll path is a character's check (`check/reroll` on a `Check`); the
  ambush is one roll for the squad with no owner. Left out on purpose, said in the spec (item 7503).
- **The alarm at the fight's start.** Item 5521's alarm told the foes where the squad stood when a fight was joined from
  the approach; a fight that opens joined already seeds each side with where the other stands (`combat.ts` sight
  set-up), so the alarm had no job left and is removed with its CONTACT! event (rules 1.78 item 6603). Its `alarm` fx
  kind stays for the area map's alarm.
- **The fumble damage in the measure's "damage a head".** party-fit counts damage inside the fight; the fumble lands in
  `runFight` before the counter starts. The fumble's own mean is the table's last column.

Read (one standard error of a 40-seed share is about 8 points): **walking in is no worse than the approach for anyone**
and better for the walk-in classes (solo Breaker 42 to 70%, solo Chaplain 28 to 42%, solo Bulwark 57 to 62%; the
approach's free walk mostly helped the foes find a squad strung out). **Always ambushing is a losing habit for every
class but the Operative**: at level 1 most characters roll the squad average near -2 against TN 12 (35% a fight) and
16 or 20 at Elites and the Boss, so about 70% of tries are lost; a lost four gives the foes a full round (quad Chaplain
80 to 42%, quad Bulwark 45 to 30%); a lost solo eats 15% max HP (17 to 29 HP a fumble, about 70% of fights) and clears
less (solo Bulwark 62 to 32%). The Operative wins about 78% of its tries (Ingenuity 7 alone, and +2 Scout at 5) and gains
(quad 57 to 70%, damage taken 25 to 16 a head). So the choice is real: walk in unless the squad's number is high or a
scout is along. The bots do not choose (walk or always); a player choosing by the odds shown does better than either.
For the lead: TN 16/20 at Elites and the Boss make the ambush almost never right there for a mixed squad; the TNs and
the 15% are the dials (`survival.json ambush.tn`, `rules.json output.amounts.ambush_fumble`).

## Calls made for Leon

1. The host chooses Walk in or Try an ambush for the squad (as Go now); guests see it greyed with "The host chooses".
2. "The squad rolls somehow": one d20 + the average (rounded down) of each living character's best of Ingenuity,
   Athletics, Senses, against Standard 12 / Hard 16 (Elite, gate, vault guard) / Heroic 20 (Boss).
3. A free turn is a full turn (full AP); `rules.json combat.free_turn.ap` softens it.
4. Elites and Bosses are caught like anyone else when the roll is won (the TN is their protection).
5. The solo fumble is 15% of max HP (Leon said "eat some dmg"), floor 1 HP, nothing reduces it; squad size 1.
6. Tremor: +1 at I, +2 at II on the roll (the best Tremor in the squad, not the sum), not caught, no fumble damage.
   Shoot First keeps its 3 / 4 AP, now in the hostiles' free turn.
7. Echo does not reroll the squad's roll.
8. Scouting: +2, never two scouts (the best bonus). The Ingenuity step's place is **Ingenuity 5** (beside Fade, on the
   door's own ladder); **no perk slot of a beta or gamma skill was used**. If Leon prefers a perk: Read Intent II (Vigilance,
   gamma, the Operative's attribute; it already reads what foes will do) would take it as an added line; not moved.
9. The alarm at contact is removed with the approach (the opening already tells each side where the other stands).
10. The banner art: SQUAD / ENEMY ADVANTAGE use `banner-fight-start` until `banner-fight-advantage` is drawn
    (`art/briefs/ART-REQUESTS.md` 0c, replacing the retired `banner-fight-approach` row).
