## What it is

1. **The item system.** Every item carries tags from one list (family, form, maker line, grade and more); code reads tags, never an item's id. Families hold their techniques. Grades are one rule computed from the Standard row. A unique later is one data row; a Weapon Art later plugs into an empty slot saved on every weapon. No unique and no usable Weapon Art ships, as you ruled.
2. **Armour.** The body armour classes are one copy in data; a sixth class, the breacher plate; grades on armour (they scale armour value only, the column is 1 for now); worn pieces' effects are heard (hoods, optics).
3. **One item card** everywhere an item is drawn. An item with no picture shows its initials on the grade's frame.
4. **84 new rows on placeholder pictures:** 72 weapons and 12 worn pieces, typed from the lead's numbers. Two are held back: the breather (smoke harms nobody in the engine) and the Blackpoint wall gun (nothing makes a weapon prone-only).
5. **The camp is unchanged.** Exactly the 18 weapons the camp offers on live carry the `issue` tag; new weapons reach a run only through the shop's rolled gear and the reward pick.
