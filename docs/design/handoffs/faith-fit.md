# The Faith fit: prayer measured and fitted (rules 1.21)

12 Nov 2026. For Leon and whoever picks the balance pass up next. The rules are in
`docs/rules/PHC-Core-Rules-v1.0.md` ("Rules revision 1.21", section 14 items 1260 to 1274). **Nothing here is committed
by this session; nothing was built with `npm run build`, deployed or run in a browser.** The heal and shield pass
(rules 1.20, `heal-shield-pass.md`) ran in the same tree at the same time: every heal and shield number of a prayer is
its, not this pass's.

Leon's question: *"I also see nothing listed on Faith yet, is it viable? Faith have heal and shield too, and does a pure
faith class stand up?"*

## 1. The answer

1. **Before this pass: no, not as a way to fight.** Two of the eight patrons had a prayer that deals damage (Old
   Overcast, Holy Father Nerukhan), neither before Devotion 5. From level 6 those two were at 84 to 116% of the plain
   rifle. At Devotion 3 (levels 1 to about 5) nobody had a prayer that hurts, and the other six patrons' devoted dealt
   15 to 53% of a rifle while they prayed.
2. **There is no "pure Faith" turn, before or after.** A prayer-led turn is a Hymn (3 AP), a Whisper (1 AP) and 2 AP
   left, which go to a weapon. The weapon is in weak hands: the points are in Faith, so one shot is worth 17 to 20% of a
   rifleman's whole turn. What stands up or falls is the 4 AP of prayer.
3. **After this pass, two patrons stand up as damage** from Devotion 5 (about session 4): Old Overcast at 127 to 143%
   of the rifle on one enemy and 159 to 177% on two; Nerukhan at 127 to 144%. That is the Outburst's place as Leon
   ruled it on 12 Nov 2026 while this pass was running (steady about 135%, peak about 165%; balance pass, section 16).
   The pass was first fitted to the Outburst's earlier 125% and 150%; that fit is in section 4 and is four numbers away.
4. **Mother Ember stands up as the healer**, and by the heal pass's own yardstick is above the Outburst healer,
   because her Hymn is prayed every turn (question 2: this needs one decision across both passes).
5. **The Laughing Mask and the Counting Weaver are about a rifle** (100 to 110%, priced, not measured); the Weaver is
   stronger than that from Devotion 7 against two or more enemies.
6. **The Quartermaster and the Matchmaker are under** (about 80 to 95%, priced). **The Toll-Keeper is insurance**, not
   a way to spend every turn.
7. **Devotion 3 is still under the rifle for everyone** (Old Overcast 89 to 91%, the rest 52 to 93%). A new devotee's
   first Hymn is a small one.
8. **A gunner who dabbles gains almost nothing**: with 6 AP and three shots there is no AP for a Whisper, and trading
   a shot for prayers loses damage. He gains Rebuke on a turn that leaves 1 AP over (20 damage).

## 2. Inventory: every prayer

Blessing power is 1 + 0.1 × Faith (×1.3 at Faith 3, ×2.0 at Faith 10). It multiplies damage, heals and shields, and
nothing else. Heal and shield numbers below are the rules 1.20 numbers as they stood in the tree when this was written
(they also grow with the caster's level, rules 1.20).

**What does not grow with level at all** (the enemy table of rules 1.15 does):

1. Nothing a prayer does grows with level by itself (rules 1.20 adds that for heals and shields). Damage, heals and
   shields grow with **Faith** through Blessing power: ×1.54 from Faith 3 to Faith 10. The plain rifle's damage a turn
   grows ×1.6 from level 1 to 20 (measured: 70 to 111), so **a damage prayer of a character who raises Faith keeps its
   place against the rifle**; no level rule was added for damage. It stops growing at Faith 10 (level 12 to 20 for a
   normal share), as the rifle does from level 13.
2. A character who leaves Faith at 3 has prayers that never grow: by level 12 Rebuke is a sixth of a rifle's turn.
   That is wanted (item 8 of the answer).
3. Conditions: the save is against 12 + (Faith − 1). Enemy save bonuses are by tier (+0 to +3) and don't rise with
   level, so control does not fade. It does the opposite: a Soldier fails 82% of the time at Faith 8 and 93% at Faith 9
   and 10 (measured, Cut Short on the range target).
4. Flat and small at high level: Last Crossing against a boss (40 × BP against 1,738 HP at level 20); Luck's +2.
5. The Miracles' damage was set against the enemy HP of before rules 1.15; The Saints' Verdict is corrected, The Sky
   Answers still did its job.

**Devotion by level** is worked out from the spec's own pace, not assumed: a character starts at 45 Devotion XP and
earns about 18 a session (tags at most 6, a vow kept +3 a fight, two rites +4 each, the Narrator at most +10; the pace
simulation of item 717 gives 18). Levels come at 300 XP a session after the first. So: 171 XP at level 6 (Devotion 6),
306 at level 9 (Devotion 8), 459 at level 12 (Devotion 10: the first Miracle can be asked right there). The step-1
simulator assumed 3 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 7 / 10: the same Hymns at every level, except that it had no Miracle at level 12.

**How a prayer-led turn is played** (the `prayer` policy in `policies.ts`): the highest Hymn within standing that deals
damage, when the cooldown allows one (every turn); if the patron has none, its highest Hymn anyway (on itself: the AP
is spent as a prayer-led turn spends it, and what the Hymn is worth is priced in section 5, not counted as damage);
then Rebuke; then the rifle with what is left (one shot). It never asks above its Devotion and never prays by dice.

**The yardsticks.** The plain rifle's damage a turn on the range, 30 fights a level: 69.7 / 80.8 / 97.3 / 96.4 / 112.2
/ 110.9 at levels 1 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 20. One Soldier-turn (for heals and shields) is the heal pass's: 18.5 / 21.5 /
25.4 / 32.4 / 37.8 / 60.2, measured on the old 16 × 11 ground with two Soldiers firing. This file's own cross-check
(`soldierTurn`: one Soldier, the same old ground, 20 fights) reads 15.2 / 18.2 / 21.5 / 25.2 / 34.2 / 50.3: one Soldier
alone is slowed by the rifleman's fire more than two are. The heal pass's number is the one used. **No number here was
measured for incoming damage on the 26 × 26 ground.**

needs or a bonus nobody uses. The rule for each Hymn is one line in `supportWorth` (`faith-measure.ts`). The exchange:

squad counts as one plain rifle's turn. Each is the worth of the Hymn **when it is needed**.

## 10. For the lead: prayer heals and shields

Not changed by this pass. What this pass found that bears on them:

1. **A Faith build prays a Hymn every turn.** The heal pass's band for a healing Hymn (125 to 150% of a Soldier-turn)
   was reasoned as "half a turn, but not every turn". With rules 1.20's numbers as they stood in the tree, Warm Hands
   is 121 to 147% and Health 30 to 37%: **together 150 to 185% of a Soldier-turn every turn**, with 2 AP still left.
   The Outburst healer's whole turn is 125%.
2. **What I think they should be**, if the Hymn stays every turn: a healing Hymn and a Whisper together at about 125%,
   the Outburst healer's rate, with the shot left over as it is for the damage patrons. That is **Warm
   Hands about 13 to 14 × Blessing power (20 now), Health 5 as it is; Saint's Shield about 16 (24 now), Protection 6 as
   it is; Absolution about 10 (15 now)**, all with the level factor of rules 1.20. Hearthlight and Coin for the Ferry
   as rules 1.20 has them.
3. The other way to reconcile is a floor of 2 rounds on the cooldown. That halves Old Overcast and Nerukhan too (a
   Hymn turn at 127 to 143% and a turn of Rebuke and two shots at about 62%: about 100% on average), so the damage
   Hymns would have to go up by half and their peak with them. I built to the Hymn every turn (question 2).
4. `prayer.intervention.amount` (20 + 4 × Devotion) is read by Nerukhan's and Old Overcast's damage interventions.
   Rules 1.20 gave Mother Ember's heal its own numbers; if that is ever folded back into the shared number, the two
   damage interventions move with it.
5. Feed the Fire's +2 AP (this pass) is on Mother Ember's list: her devoted now has a second use for a turn in which
   nobody needs healing.

## 11. Questions for Leon

Each recommendation is what is built.

1. **One damage Whisper for every patron (Rebuke), or a different small prayer for each?** Recommendation: one. The
   six Whispers are already shared, the patron changes the look and the line, and it gives all eight a floor at once.
2. **A Hymn every turn, or a set-piece?** The rules already let a Faith build pray a Hymn every turn (cooldown 1 from
   Virtue 4). Recommendation: keep it, and fit the numbers to it (done for damage; section 10 for heals). The
   alternative, a floor of 2 rounds, makes the Hymn a set-piece but leaves a prayer-led character with 1 AP of prayer
   on every other turn and a weapon he is bad with.
3. **Is Devotion 3 allowed to be under the rifle?** Recommendation: yes. It is levels 1 to about 5, the first three or
   four sessions, and the character has support Hymns from the start. If not, the lever is each patron's Hymn I, one
   by one.
4. **Should Faith be held to the Outburst's targets at all?** Built to them as you ruled them on 12 Nov (steady 135%,
   peak 165%; the brief this pass started from said 125% and 150%, and that fit is kept in section 4). My case for a
   lower target, for you to rule: prayer has no resource to run out of (no ammo, MP, Strain or cores), a Hymn is there
   every turn with no set-up, and its damage never misses and ignores armour (Soul) or half of it (lightning), so on
   Elites and Bosses it is worth more than the range shows. The Outburst pays Strain for its 135%. I would hold the
   damage patrons at the first fit (about 120 to 125% on the range, peak about 150%) until Elites and Bosses are
   measured.
5. **Cut Short's limit, or one rule for every lost turn?** Recommendation: Cut Short only, for now. No other source of
   Stunned in the game has a limit; a general rule ("an enemy can't lose two turns running") is a decision for the
   whole control pass.
6. **Witnessed gives Old Overcast's devoted +2 to hit with every attack** (the "kill" tag is read as every attack);
   the other seven patrons get it rarely or never. Recommendation: left as found and flagged (item 1273); it is a
   reason a gunner might swear to Old Overcast at Faith 5.
7. **The Quartermaster and the Matchmaker in a fight.** Recommendation: leave them until squads are measured. Their
   Hymns are worth what the ally they land on is worth, and this pass could only price that.
8. **The Saints' Verdict at 130** kills a Soldier of the level it is first asked at and not an Elite, as The Sky
   Answers already did. Recommendation: that is the line for a Miracle that hits everything in sight.
