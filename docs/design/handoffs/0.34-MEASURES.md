# 0.34 measures: the nine classes after Items 2.0 (integration, 20 Nov 2026)

**Measured, not fitted.** The lead fits (Stage C). The baseline is 0.33's "walk in" column
(`docs/design/handoffs/0.33-LANE-AMBUSH.md`, "The measure"; raw runs `E:/phc-wt/w0311/.check/ambush-033/walk-*.json`).

## How

Stage 1, 40 seeds a cell (PF-1 to PF-40), the party-fit cells, gear route, bots walk in (the default):

    PARTY_FIT=1 PF_CELLS=<cells> PF_SEEDS=40 PF_FROM=1 PF_OUT=x.json npx vitest run --root packages/engine test/balance/party-fit.test.ts

Two columns:

- **0.34 release** = `wip/release-0.34` (lanes A1 to A5, B0, B2 merged; the 12 new worn pieces and the six modifier rows
  in the pools; the 72 new weapon rows NOT in, B1 was not merged).
- **trial with B1** = the same tree plus `wip/0.34-b1` merged (a local trial branch, not pushed), to show what the 72 new
  weapon rows in the shop, loot and camp pools would do. Not the release.

- **0.34 final** = `release/0.34-final` (the integration, rules 1.93: B1 merged with the lead's rulings, the camp kept
  as 0.33.0's by the `issue` tag, Smetanita's Pressure, the consolidation). Same command, same seeds (run 21 Nov 2026).
  Column 4 adds "as the release": seeds whose run ends exactly as in column 2.

Each cell: stage-1 clears of 40 / damage a head a fight (from foes) / Downs a fight, then the change in clears from 0.33,
and "same run" = seeds whose run ends the same as 0.33's (cleared, depth, fights). One standard error of a 40-seed share
is about 8 points (3 clears). **FLAG** = more than 3 clears from the 0.33 number.

## Flagged

- **0.34 final (column 4; `release/0.34-final` at `55ee7a78f`: B1 merged with rules 1.93, the camp the issue rack), solo_rigger
  21 to 25 (+4): not the new rows.** All 40 seeds run exactly as on the 0.34 release (B0+B2, no B1, column 2), so this
  is that column's +4 (below), unchanged. The B1 trial's two flags are gone: solo_operative 12 (-1, 36 of 40 as the
  release) and solo_channeler 16 (-2, 40 of 40 as the release). The trial's flags came from the camp rack moving
  (cheapest-first put `albert_town_brush_carbine` and `smetanita_pocket_pistol` on it); rules 1.93 item 8100 keeps the
  camp's 18 rows, so the new rows now reach a run only through the shop's rolled gear and the reward pick
  (`rolledGear`, rules 1.91 item 7920; Survival's loot is materials only and the Forge's stock is mods and worn
  pieces). Where a final run differs from the release, the new rows in those two pools are the only change in play.
- Near the line, not flagged: quad_bulwark +3 (21; 33 of 40 as the release, 7 seeds moved by the shop and reward
  pools), quad_operative -3 (20; 32 of 40 as the release), solo_chaplain -3 (14; 39 of 40 as the release, so already
  the release's -2 plus one seed).

- **0.34 release, solo_rigger 21 to 25 (+4).** Checked against live 0.33.0 (`origin/main` `547c4f92c`, the same cell and
  seeds; party-fit's SURPRISE import patched in a throwaway worktree, since main's party-fit crashes without A5's
  stand-in): main clears 21, the same as the 0.33 column, so the +4 is the release's. 36 of 40 seeds run the same; the
  four that differ (PF-9, 12, 15, 33) die at the Elite gate (depth 8 to 10) on main and clear on the release; their
  first differing fight is the 2nd to 4th, so a pool change (the new worn pieces or modifier rows in rolled gear or
  rewards) is the likely cause. Not traced further.
- **trial with B1, solo_operative 13 to 8 (-5)** and **solo_channeler 18 to 14 (-4).** Only with the 72 new weapon rows,
  which change the camp rack (`archetypeWeapons` sorts cheapest first, so new cheap rows become the free and the
  better weapons: the gunner's rifle becomes `albert_town_brush_carbine`, the better pistol `smetanita_pocket_pistol`)
  and the shop and loot pools. Not traced further; for the lead with B1.

Drift: the "same run" counts below 40 in unflagged cells mean seeds take other paths with equal outcome counts; every
other cell is within 3 clears. Stalled fights (a fight that did not end in 20 rounds, a simulator limit): 42 of 720 runs
on the release, 48 with B1, 42 on the final, 49 in the 0.33 column.
