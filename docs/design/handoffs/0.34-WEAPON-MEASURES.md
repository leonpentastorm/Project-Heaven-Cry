# 0.34 weapon measures: every holdable weapon row against the plain rifle (Items 2.0, 21 Nov 2026)

**Measured, not fitted. No data changed.** Tree: `wip/0.34-measure` = `origin/wip/release-0.34` at `9c0c9b67c`
(release/0.34-final) plus the measuring script `packages/engine/test/balance/items2-measure.test.ts` (not run by the gate).

## How

    ITEMS2=1 ITEMS2_N=16 ITEMS2_PARTS=l1,l10,l10n,mods ITEMS2_OUT=x.json npx vitest run --root packages/engine test/balance/items2-measure.test.ts --silent=false
    ITEMS2_SHIELDS=1 ITEMS2_N=40 npx vitest run --root packages/engine test/balance/items2-measure.test.ts --silent=false

- The yardstick is the project's own: `guns-measure.ts rifleYard` (the plain rifle, normal share, plain shots, at its pin;
  rules 1.61 R1) on the shooting range (`sim.ts`: one Soldier-numbered target, 5,000 HP, 13 tiles off, never acts; 8
  turns; the walk in counted). Plain rifle damage a turn: **L1 71.6, L10 181.3**. Every % below is damage a turn over
  that. Both sides spend the same turn, so % of the rifle's turn is the damage-per-AP ratio.
- Guns: `guns-measure.ts` (the optimised gun build `opt_gun`, Marksman), the best of its four rotations (plain, step1 =
  the optimised policy, gunner, dump); the rotation is named in the cell. Melee: `melee-measure.test.ts`'s rule (the
  optimised blade build `opt_blade`, Fighter; a Dexterity row in the Dexterity twin, Marksman). **Added here:** a
  Durability twin (Defender) for shields, which hit with Durability (the melee tool would judge them in a Strength build).
- Columns: **L1** = level 1, normal share, standard grade (no investment; the cell's bracket is damage a turn).
  **L10 normal** = level 10, normal share, Masterwork. **L10 all in** = level 10, the tool's all-in build, Masterwork.
  **+ mods** = L10 all in with each of the row's mod slots filled greedily by the mod that measures best on top of what
  is fitted (`measures-032.ts modRow`'s method, extended to melee and to every row).
- A row the build refuses for a skill need (`Needs raw power 3.`): a twin of the build with that skill second in the
  all-in order (and a 0.3 normal share). At L1 even the twin does not reach 3, so those rows read "not held" at L1.
- Noise: 16 fights a cell. Melee cells replay the same seeds bare and with mods (the bare reading matched L10 all in
  within 0.5 points on average), so melee mod gains are real. Gun mod cells run the L10 best rotation's policy; the same
  gun bare differs from the L10 column by 5 points on average (25 at most), so **a gun mod gain under about 10 points is
  noise**, and the greedy pick is biased upward.
- Damage a magazine is raw (data damage × magazine; a shotgun's `within_4`; a bow's 40 is its quiver). Turns to empty: 6
  AP at the row's fastest mode (Mag Dump 12 rounds + a 2 AP shot if it has auto; else 2 Bursts if it has burst; else
  single shots at its AP), no reload counted.
- Not in the table: grenades and the satchel (thrown category, no tool), companion bites and claws, bare fists, and the
  ten variant rows (`variants.json`, e.g. `hellraiser_service_rifle.long_belly`).

**Tool limits.** The range target never acts and stands 13 tiles off: reach (2 tiles: `legion_spear`, the polearms,
`ork_cleaver_glaive`), Guard, a Ward's damage reduction and any defensive value are not measured; shotguns and flamers
are measured at their fall-off as the policy walks in (they read low: the tool's own result). Rocket tubes and launchers
with a 1-round magazine pay a reload a shot (16 to 38%). Bows with a Raw Power term (`recurve_bow` 20 + 5 × Raw Power,
`longbow` 25 + 6 × Raw Power): the recurve is measured in the gun build at Raw Power 0 (arrow 20); the longbow only in the
need twin, where Raw Power is second in the all-in order and filled to its ceiling: that is what reads 244 to 297% (the
arrow at Raw Power R is 25 + 6R). Read the longbow as raw damage, not against the line.
