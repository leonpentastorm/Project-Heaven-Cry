## Results in one line each

1. **Sigilist:** the bot is not the limit any more. 14 of 160 solo clears before its new habits, 16 after; the Line from
   cover cost clears (5 of 80 against 9) and is off; Lines across 4, the longest Line always 10. Its damage a turn is 48
   (Channeler 83): what holds it is the Line's shape (a straight row with the target, a step a glyph, two Lines a core),
   not where it stands. Party fit solo 6, quad 2. **Not reached: a player's rate.** Leon and a friend played it "at about
   Outburst level"; the bot reaches a third of the Channeler's clears. Next step for the bot, if wanted: Lines fitted to
   the lanes of the along band (a target one lane off the axis is in reach; `fit()` asks for the exact axis), which needs
   the band's geometry read from the engine (a hypothetical glyph on a copy) rather than retyped in the bot.
2. **Calls:** at 3 AP no Call speeds a fight; Rally and Brief now break even at 1 to 4 points (7.1 rounds against 7.0)
   and are the only ones under 7 at 8 points. The other seven slow the fight 0.8 to 1.1 rounds at 3 AP. The enemy's
   Rally reads the same rows and is stronger.
3. **Name the Sinner:** the price was the problem, not when to name: at +40% naming the Elite or Boss is 35 of 80 against
   33 not naming (at +20%: 23). The player's rule stays simple: name the Elite or the Boss.
4. **Silent:** every door within 3 clears of its number with the switch at `reveal` (solo and four, 40 seeds); nothing
   tuned. **Athletics from 1:** the guard's idle pair 4.9 → 5.3 rounds to the first Down (40 fights); the guard's bound
   of 4.2 on its own 4 fights fails as the rules lane found; OFF, waiting on Leon.
5. **Against 0.32:** no class fell more than 3 clears because of 0.33; the Operative (0 → 21), the Bulwark quad (8 → 15),
   the Breaker quad (6 → 13) and the Chaplain solo (11 → 18) rose and were kept. Two old run-policy Survival cells fell
   (`solo_rigger` 18 → 12, by the surprise in the approach; `four_casters` 20 → 13, cause not found): they are the old run
   rigger's cells, not a class, and were not tuned.
