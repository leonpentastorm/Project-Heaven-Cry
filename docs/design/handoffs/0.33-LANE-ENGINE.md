  with contact made and the dice forced; I stopped rather than guess. The test is unchanged.
- **Group F item 12:** real, not fixed. The Outburst preview (`magic.ts previewOutburstPlan`) sizes damage with
  `outburstDamage` and runs no `dealing` / `damage_out` hook at all, so Hot Blood (x1.05, `damage: [outburst]`) and any
  other dealing modifier are missing from the chip. The door word itself is right in the live Release. The fix is to run
  the dealing hook in the preview with `src.door: outburst` (one preview context): a contained piece for the next lane.
- **Item 7, Old Tales II's parley:** already the engine's since rules 1.75 item 6206 (`perks/g.ts` `speak_fight`:
  Commanded `hold`, no save, once per being). Commanded `hold` is the piece that forbids the attacks, so no new condition
  mod was added (Leon's rule: nothing new where a piece nearly does it). Left: it is per-item handler code, not data.
- **`combat.hurl.never`** (tier `boss`) stays a second reading of "a Boss" beside `bosses`; the exact difference is in
  spec item 7405 (a Celestial of another tier would be refused at the pick instead of lifted and then refused).
- **`faith.ts` still names condition ids in code** (Courage's `fear` / `shaken`, Feed the Fire's `weakened` / `slowed` in
  the story half): ids, not numbers; outside this item.
- **"Bonded (99 rounds; ...)"** in the log when an enemy Bonds a character (and a character an enemy): the tie's hold is
  said as a count, as Together's was before rules 1.85 item 7303. Polish.
