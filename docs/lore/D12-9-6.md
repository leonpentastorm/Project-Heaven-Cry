# D12.9.6

*Agent of the CCEC (Central City Espionage Commission).*

D12.9.6 was the speaker of the 2020 EPCCG's Analysis entry (Article 5, entry 17). With rules 1.13 that skill became Acuity and took Demitri Kortev's entry, so D12.9.6 no longer speaks for a skill. The agent stands now as a character of the world: the text below is the character's own, kept as it was in the game. The 2020 wording is still in the [EPCCG](EPCCG-Player-Character-Creation-Guide.md), and the portrait is `art/portraits/epccg/analysis.png` (catalog id `first-patch-revival--analysis`).

## The quote

> "Look at the crime scene again. First: this man was never shot. He may be stuck to a wall full of bullet holes, but bullets didn't kill him. Why? Pick up a casing and look closely. Not an enhanced round; nobody is crazy enough to load enhanced rounds into an SMG. It smells of Outburst mana, a foreign Outburst to be exact. Second: the body stayed stuck to the wall until we pulled it off. Some strong force pushed him from the opposite side, and it wasn't an enhanced round. One .700 Blackpoint? Maybe. Fifty .32 Decrkm? Definitely not. From the mana, the victim was hit by an Outburst. The casings and holes say the killer wanted us to believe he died to bullets, enhanced rounds specifically. Send the apology letter. We need to find a spy from Bazaim."

## The entry's intro (2020, Analysis)

Analysis is your ability to break the world down: work through any situation logically and land on the most correct answer.

- **At high levels:** you over-analyse everything until your view narrows to a slit. Once you pick a detail, your brain glues itself to it and forgets a case usually has more than one.
- **Good for:** detectives, hunters, assassins, secret agents. **Bad for:** Orks, Rockmen, barbarians.

## The voice it lent

The Analysis voice card was toned after D12.9.6. It is kept here for whoever writes the agent as an NPC.

- **Persona:** A forensic machine of a mind that reconstructs events from casings and stains.
- **Wants:** The one correct answer, assembled from evidence.
- **Style:** Clinical, enumerated, deductive: 'First... Second...'
- **Never:** Never lets go of the first good clue.
- **Notices:** "First: no casings. Second: the wall is dented from the far side. He wasn't shot."
- **Pushes:** "That first clue is the clue. Ignore the rest."
- **Quiet:** "Send the apology letter later. Find the spy now."
