## Briefing

Project Heaven Cry (PHC) is a tabletop RPG that grafts Disco Elysium's skill psychology onto squad-level tactical combat. This is the English edition of the *Emergency Player Character Creation Guide* (EPCCG), Starter Edition, rev 1.7/163-24/c, issued by Anti-Heaven Operations Command, Department of Extra-Celestial Defense. Approved training manual, internal use only.

**The pitch (reconstructed).** By the 41st century humanity spans a Thousand-Planet City, and it is at war with Heaven: something from the upper realm, above the sky. The only weapons that hurt it come from that same upper realm. Every soldier who fires an Outburst pulls power through their own soul's link to Heaven; every nun who calls down a Blessing prays to the side she is shooting at. That is the cry in Heaven Cry.

**What the source material actually establishes**

- **Three schools of power, one per Core.**
  - *Sigils* (Mind): engineered magic. Patterns drawn through an Evoker, fuelled by MagiCore. Its users call themselves scientists.
  - *Outbursts* (Soul): raw mana pulled across the bond between your soul and its twin in the upper realm, channelled through an Emanator. Overdraw it and you suffer a Mana Short: internal bleeding, or a heart that bursts in your chest. Its users are closer to monks.
  - *Blessings* (Virtue): power granted by saints and gods through Faith.
- **Guns are everywhere.** Revolvers, SMGs, shotguns, enhanced rounds, .700 Blackpoint, .32 Decrkm.
- **Enemies include** Orks, Crawlers, and spies from Bazaim.
- **Operation Daggerfall** is celebrated as a turning point in history. The veterans remember shelling Crawler families.
- **Tone:** grim military sci-fantasy that refuses to stay solemn for more than two sentences.

## Article 1: The stat page and the Core stats

In PHC your stat page is a roleplay tool first and a combat tool second. Build the character you want to play: the system is designed so that every kind of character has a proper way to help the squad in a fight, so nobody ends up dead weight.

**Starting budget**

| Pool | At creation | How you earn more |
|---|---|---|
| Core points | 6 | 1 per 5 levels, or by completing a Legendary Deed |
| Attribute points | 10 | Not stated in v1.7 (see Designer's notes) |
| Skill points | 10 | 1 per level, skill books, quest rewards |

Every Core and every Attribute starts at 1 on the official sheet; every Skill starts at 0.

**The cap cascade.** The bigger stat sets the ceiling of the smaller one:

- A **Core** caps its three **Attributes**: no Attribute can be higher than its Core.
- An **Attribute** caps its three **Skills**: no Skill can be higher than its Attribute.

*[Diagram in the original Doc: PHC skill tree · 3 Cores, 9 Attributes, 27 Skills]*

Read each column top-down: the Core caps the three boxes inside it, and each box's Attribute caps the three skills listed under it.

**Example: Comrade Cat.** Comrade Cat has spent all his points and has 4 in Body. He can raise Endurance, Strength and Agility to 4 at most. However many spare Attribute points he has, Endurance cannot go to 5, because Body is only 4. Skills work the same way: his Strength is 2, so Raw Power, Guts and Intimidation cannot go past 2 no matter how many Skill points he has left. Same story for Sensory under Endurance.

**The three Cores**

- **Body:** muscle, blood, and how well you command your own flesh.
- **Mind:** the brain, the ability to absorb theory and think logically.
- **Soul:** the heart, emotional awareness and social perception.

## Article 2: Traits

Traits are the strongest roleplay lever in PHC. A trait usually carries one big advantage and one big drawback, so you play your character's strengths and weaknesses as written. Pick up to two traits. Trait modifiers can't be used to unlock perks, and nothing can remove them. The full trait list and their backstories live in the *EPCCG: Trait Edition*.

**Example trait: Bottle Butts for Eyes**

> Four-eyes, bookworm, coke-bottle glasses. You've been called a lot of names, most of them accurate, but that doesn't mean they had to say it out loud :(

- **+ Pointdexter:** +2 Rumination.
- **+ Glasses On:** while wearing your glasses, +1 Proficiency and +1 Ingenuity.
- **− Glasses Off:** without your glasses, Proficiency and Ingenuity take −4.

**Example trait: The Man with a Thousand Faces** *(recovered from the group's Discord)*

> Art has no fixed form; it is the one thing only you can understand. The world may not understand you, but you understand the world. What is more artful than a performance?

- **+ Adaptable:** gain 20% more XP from combat, and every skill in your γ branch gets +1.
- **− Numb:** conversation interactions no longer give you XP.

The full list of 28 traits is in Trait Edition.

## Article 3: The nine Attributes

Attributes (the original calls them *Based Attributes*) set the fundamentals of combat: magic power, max HP, mana and more. They do nothing in conversation or exploration. Talking your way past a guard is the Skills' job.

| Core | Attribute | Per point |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Endurance | +50 Max HP, −10% limb damage taken |
| Body | Strength | +10% Physical Damage, +5 kg carry weight |
| Body | Agility | +1 Initiative, +0.5 Movement |
| Mind | Perception | +1 Vision Range, +10% Critical Damage, −10% Critical Damage taken |
| Mind | Intelligence | +10% Sigil Damage; +1 Sigil Tier you can draw per 2 points |
| Mind | Expression | −0.5 AP to draw a Sigil, +20% Elemental Damage |

## Article 4: About the skills

Skills are how you build a specific character and play them. Every skill starts at 0. When the GM calls for a roll on a skill you have at 0, you take **−5 on the d20**. Put 1 point in it and the penalty is gone. From there the skill climbs a 10-point track (below).

**Example: Comrade Doge** has 0 in Durability and 1 in Ingenuity. Rolling Durability, he takes −5. Rolling Ingenuity, he rolls clean at +0.

The original's dice-bot example rolls both skills in one check: 1d20 (11) − 5 + 0 [endurance][ingenuity] = 6. That suggests a single check can call on two skills and add both modifiers.

**The skill track** *(recovered from the group's Discord)*. Skills run from 0 to 10. Even points raise your Dice Score (DS) bonus; odd points from 3 up unlock a Skill Mastery tier.

| Points | Roll | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −5 DS |  |
| 1 | no penalty |  |
| 2 | +1 DS |  |
| 3 | +1 DS | Skill Mastery 1 |
| 4 | +2 DS |  |
| 5 | +2 DS | Skill Mastery 2 |
| 6 | +3 DS |  |
| 7 | +3 DS | Skill Mastery 3 |
| 8 | +4 DS |  |
| 9 | +4 DS | Skill Mastery 4 |
| 10 | +5 DS | Mastered |

**Why raise a skill:** it lets you roll without the penalty, raises your dice score, and reveals extra information about the world once you have enough points. **Why be careful:** every skill turns on you at high levels. Too much of anything is bad for you, same as in real life.

Skills also feed the **Perk** system, described in the *EPCCG: Perk Edition*.

**The three skill classes.** Each Attribute owns one skill of each class.

| Class | Sheet column | What it gives | Perk types |
|---|---|---|---|
| α Alpha | Red | Strong combat bonus; decides which equipment you can use | Active Skill, Combat Interaction |
| β Beta | Blue | Defence, utility and AP; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment | Passive, AP Mod |
| γ Gamma | Green | Strong dialogue and exploration bonus; unlocks conversation options | Combat Starter, Conversation Quirk |

## Article 5: Dude, what do these skills even do?

All 27 skills, three per Attribute, each introduced by someone in the field who has far too much of it. Exact numbers are in Article 6.

### Body

#### 01 · Durability — α of Endurance

> "Yeah, I take hits for a living. Men these days are weak one way or another, and I mostly make my money off the ones who are weak in the heart." — Sanchez of the Burning Bridge

Durability is your metabolism and circulation; what commoners call "being in good shape." Who cares if you can shoot fire from your palms when you can inhale the fireball they just threw and breathe it back in their faces?

- **Effect:** more HP per level-up, Critical Damage reduction; gates endurance equipment.
- **At high levels:** you look like a pro wrestler and weaker folk start trembling. Not sure they'll dare talk to a walking marble statue, though.
- **Good for:** gym rats, tomboys, brawlers who tank for the team. **Bad for:** scholars, exam crammers, light-armour fans.

#### 02 · Pain Threshold — β of Endurance

> "Want to test if your poison's strong enough? Coat a blade and go poke Knight Jiren in the arm. No, I'm serious. Once he sleepwalked into the snake pen, slept there all night and walked out with a dozen Crobar still hanging off his back. THEN HE WENT AND MADE BREAKFAST. Sometimes I wonder if anyone around here is still normal." — Sazmin of the Adamantile Brigade

Pain Threshold is why you can take an arrow and still get up. Or change a lightbulb without flipping the breaker? Who knows. Just remember: not feeling pain doesn't mean you can't die. Don't get delusional.

- **Effect:** Elemental Damage reduction; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** the "threshold" part disappears and you go numb to anything that hurts. And again: A KNIFE YOU CAN'T FEEL CAN STILL STOP YOUR HEART.
- **Good for:** bushido duellists the sneaky types keep poisoning, people who hate pain, the last survivor of a house fire, masochists. **Bad for:** anyone who still likes their limbs and doesn't want to lose one because they "forgot fire is hot."

#### 03 · Sensory — γ of Endurance

> "She can feel it, trust me. Even a single sigh. Back when her eyes still worked and her legs hadn't given out, Grandma Marry was already the Grandma Marry she is now. What I always admired most was how she could read the temperature of the air with one finger raised to the sky. Or the way she sits in her deck chair, eyes closed, smelling the morning, with a smile on her lips. Sometimes she makes a twenty-year-old girl like me jealous." — Sister March of the Lumeri Church

Sensory is how sensitive your skin and senses are to the world: a cold breeze, a scent you can't name, a faint tremor underground. With high Sensory it feels like nature itself is shaking your hand.

- **Effect:** Heal/Shield received bonus; unlocks sensation options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you become one with every movement around you, a little too well. If your ears are that sharp, stay away from concerts. Those speakers don't hit gently.
- **Good for:** people who stroll for the breeze, travellers who go somewhere just to breathe it, scouts and rogues listening for footsteps. **Bad for:** death metal fans, people who can't take the cold, elves of every kind.

#### 04 · Raw Power — α of Strength

> "Back in the army they kept saying my arms were bigger than my brain. Yeah, maybe. Maybe. If I pulled my brain out and measured it with a ruler, my arm probably is bigger. But you can't arm-wrestle with a brain, and when it comes to arm-wrestling I'm the best there is. So I used my arms to wrestle their brains, and a few concrete tables. Nobody said anything after that." — Knight Andre "Giant Fist" of the Adamantile Brigade

Just saying the name makes you feel the strength rising. Raw Power isn't technique, calm or authority. It's STRENGTH: hit harder, bend further, lift higher. Things only muscle can do.

- **Effect:** Physical Damage and Counter Roll; gates strength equipment.
- **At high levels:** you break locks, doors and bones, and laugh at people who can't. But that much muscle makes it easy to hurt the things you never meant to.
- **Good for:** martial artists, berserkers, competitive lifters, guys going for the record on the bar's punching machine. **Bad for:** mages, cat and bunny lovers, high nobility who don't want to be called thugs.

#### 05 · Guts — β of Strength

> "Why has no one else uncovered the secret of Arganzaz? Because they are a pack of faint hearts, that's why. I saw a jade-inlaid door twenty yards wide slowly opening. I heard a thousand wandering ghosts wailing and smelled ten thousand walking corpses. What they did was scream and run. What I did was hold my breath and walk in. That is why they now call me the Keeper of the secret of Arganzaz." — Ragoul the Keeper

Guts is your tolerance for anything spooky or scary: a door with a skull over it, or a pitch-black hole with a sign saying "Hole of Pain." What it is doesn't matter. What matters is that you're not scared of it.

- **Effect:** Body Debuff save; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you feel like an impregnable fortress. But people don't label things "dangerous" for fun, and you not being scared doesn't stop it from chewing your bones.
- **Good for:** thrill-seekers, manly men, manly women, YouTubers filming viral videos in abandoned graveyards. **Bad for:** horror-movie fans, delicate ladies, team leaders and scouts.

#### 06 · Intimidation — γ of Strength

> "Have you ever had the pleasure of tasting animal marrow? Oh, who am I kidding. A merchant from Mandestra must have tasted every pleasure under the sun. Forgive my peasant manners. And you know what, Mr. Galon? If the shipment I'm waiting for doesn't arrive on time, then with these two peasant hands I'll treat you to a very special marrow broth, made from the bones of your arm. Understand? Snap that dainty hand of yours like a chicken neck, and then we savour that faintly sweet flavour together, like business partners sharing a milkshake. Slurp. Slurp. Slurp." — Tommy Avencio, the "Papa" of the Smetanita Conjunction

Intimidation is your control over the fear of whoever stands in front of you. One piercing look, one commanding cough: the person running this conversation is you.

- **Effect:** Body Debuff DS bonus; unlocks threat options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** everyone sees a demon in human skin, whether you want it or not. Whether that's good or bad is up to you.
- **Good for:** people done with smooth talkers' bullshit, people who want every conversation to open with the other guy's trousers turning brown, serial killers. **Bad for:** upstanding middle-class citizens, anyone keeping an angelic image, heartbreakers.

#### 07 · Athletics — α of Agility

> "Armour? What armour? Oh, I left the vest at base. First-floor locker room, locker 16, key's under the red flowerpot if you want it. Natalia still grumbles every time I deploy: 'Are those two banners on your back for a burial shroud or what?' But the armoury boys lean on tech way too much. What I'm fighting out here are Orks. ORKS. They swing junk machetes, and the gunners aren't any better, aiming with both eyes open. If you can dodge everything they throw at you, armour just slows you down!" — Dominik Edelgard, the "Banner Lord" of the 237th Hellraiser Division

Athletics is how nimble and supple you are. It decides how well you pull off anything that needs flexibility: flips, swimming, dodging.

- **Effect:** Dodge Roll and Critical Damage; gates agility equipment.
- **At high levels:** your body is light as a feather and limber as a cat. Until someone ties you down. Then you aren't a person who lost a leg, you're a bird that lost its wings. Prepare for the trauma.
- **Good for:** gymnasts, dancers, guys who spam crouch after every kill. **Bad for:** slow-living types, high nobility, heavy-armour users.

#### 08 · Dexterity — β of Agility

> "No tricks here. I don't even need to open my eyes. I've carried this revolver since the years before Operation Daggerfall, stranger. Call me old-fashioned, call me outdated, but don't take this old body lightly in a gunfight. Wander within 500 metres and if your head doesn't vanish, your hat will need a big patch. These eyes aren't what they were, but these hands have never left the gunbelt. Identify. Squeeze. Breathe the powder smoke. Dig the grave." — Ambrosius Costeau, the Old Drifter

Dexterity measures how reliable you are at anything that needs precision. Anyone can reload a gun or throw a dart. Nobody does it as fast or as clean as you.

- **Effect:** throwing range; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** your hands work like a machine and your trained skills fire almost on their own. So you want to use them even when it's the wrong moment, and announcing your habits to the whole world is never a good idea.
- **Good for:** gunslingers, snipers, blade masters, point strikers. **Bad for:** gamblers, magic users, anyone who doesn't want to be called "hands faster than brain."

#### 09 · Reaction Speed — γ of Agility

> "I shoot first. Honestly, you don't need fancy gear out here. Want it harder? Hand me a couple of daggers. Surviving the lowlands is tough because it has the one thing the desert doesn't: dead buildings. Wherever you go in here, you're swimming through a concrete forest. If they poke out of some dark corner and you don't react in time, a hundred percent you end up strung on a pole. Me, I just throw a knife and duck before they pull the trigger." — Yukimura of the Black Scavengers

Reaction Speed is the time between your eyes firing and your body moving: a glint from an enemy scope, or a strange blink from a partner right before the handshake. Spotting danger is one thing. Reacting in time is another.

- **Effect:** Ambush Damage reduction; unlocks reaction options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you're frighteningly fast and never caught flat-footed. But reacting that fast makes you look plainly impatient, and plenty of times reacting fast just makes things go wrong faster.
- **Good for:** gamers, sailors, gunners, every other kind of -er, gamblers. **Bad for:** calm people, people who need to save face, anyone who doesn't want to look like they're on speed.

### Mind

#### 10 · Ingenuity — α of Perception

> "A thousand gold for two rocks. The number of people still paying in gold coins outside Central City is baffling. Back when I worked the city, I'd swipe a bank card out of some guy's pocket and that was dinner sorted. Back then I still had Travis to crack the cards... People call it petty theft, but the trade takes real skill. First you learn to walk in step with the crowd and spot which pocket bulges most. Then you work out how much weight to slip back in so they don't notice the coins have flown. If you don't know how, don't mess around. The old men at the guard post started carrying shock batons last week, and it hurts like you wouldn't believe." — Lin Shin Long

Ingenuity is the finesse in everything you do: staying unseen while sneaking, picking pockets, that sort of thing.

- **Effect:** reduces Ambush Range, raises Ambush Damage; gates finesse equipment.
- **At high levels:** you turn into a Solid Snake cosplayer, flickering in and out of the shadows. But all that shifty behaviour makes it hard to look a cop in the eye.
- **Good for:** rogues, scouts, bandits, gamblers. **Bad for:** model citizens, uniformed (not undercover) cops, anyone trying to romance a uniformed (not undercover) cop.

#### 11 · Proficiency — β of Perception

> "Yes, yes, a watchmaker and a gunsmith aren't far apart. Lay every part of a pendulum clock on the table and odds are your brain starts shorting out. Guns are the same: picking one up and shooting is a world away from stripping every screw and putting it back. Anyone can read a clock, but how many can fix one? What you need is someone who sees every piece of the puzzle and joins them into one. Like me! Unless you use a digital clock or one of those toy guns from Alcatraz, in which case: scram. Shoo, shoo." — Demitri Kortev of Kortev Utility Shack

Proficiency is your grasp of complex mechanisms, from something as small as a stopwatch to something as huge as a transcontinental cannon. All of it can be taken apart by you.

- **Effect:** Sigil Draw Core reduction; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you see how everything works, man-made or not. That helps with a lot, but you lose interest in anything that isn't screws and gears, and it cuts you off from human society.
- **Good for:** engineers, roboticists, weapon modders, clockmakers. **Bad for:** enchanters, magic users, party people, therapists.

#### 12 · Vigilant — γ of Perception

> "They keep saying I can dodge bullets. As if any human could. They've got a few knives in the neck before they can pull the trigger, that's all. Run enough jobs for old Rourigaz and you'll get used to it: every time someone aims at you, even from the dark, your spine goes cold. Just remember to cut their spine out in return, as a thank-you gift." — Vermilion Jasmine of Rigaz Trade Company (RTC™®)

Vigilant is the survival instinct your ancestors handed down. An enemy in the bushes, a prying gaze in the dark: with eyes and ears tuned to detail, you notice what's off before anyone else.

- **Effect:** Detection Range; unlocks alertness options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you're hyper-aware of everything you see and hear and can sense danger in a heartbeat. But that alert, you're never sure if the danger is real or you're just imagining things.
- **Good for:** debtors on the run from loan sharks, husbands afraid of wives in high heels, grunts terrified of punji traps, snipers. **Bad for:** anyone who doesn't want to be called jumpy or a coward.

#### 13 · Brain Power — α of Intelligence

> "Stop flailing and concentrate. In nearly twenty years of using an Evoker I have never needed to dance a single kata to trigger a sigil. If you want martial arts in your magic, kindly go south and join a sect. I may be a spellcaster, but I am a scientist and they are philosophers. Now breathe deep, memorise the sigil's path, draw it, and press the blue button on the right-hand grip. If you burn through your MagiCore before finishing a Fireball, I'm done teaching you." — Professor Avdol of the Science Team

Brain Power is your brain's processing speed. In PC-parts terms, it's the difference between two graphics cards. It lets you handle information faster than others, and helps a lot with Sigils and Evokers.

- **Effect:** Sigil Damage and Sigil Stay Duration; gates big-brain equipment.
- **At high levels:** your brain is always hunting detail, almost hyperactive. Look at a rock and instead of "where's it from" and "why is it here", you're on "its chemical composition" and "a hundred ways to arrange rocks into a code." Training a brain that big isn't always smart.
- **Good for:** Evoker users, scientists, researchers. **Bad for:** Emanator users, healers, melee fighters.

#### 14 · Rumination — β of Intelligence

> "The knowledge you need to become a Keeper of Books? Seven hundred and forty-five reigns. Two hundred thousand years of history. Five hundred and forty thousand inventions, great and small. Nine million three hundred and fifty thousand species of plant and animal, and hundreds of millions of pages turned. Now kindly erase your presence from my library." — Thalatûl the Librarian

Rumination is how well you absorb and retain scientific knowledge: history, politics, maths, physics, chemistry. It also lets you look at an object and pull up the theory behind it.

- **Effect:** Sigil Range bonus; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you always side with science, experiment and hard data. Great in a lab; uncomfortable with anything science can't explain, like incantations, charms, souls or spiritual links.
- **Good for:** bookworms, hackermen, tech professionals of every field. **Bad for:** lifters who only care about strength, students of the surreal, the devout, Alex Jones.

#### 15 · Eloquence — γ of Intelligence

> "Fine, if you insist, I'll review all the evidence. Aaaand there's nothing worth reviewing. The dashcam at 11:45 shows, plain as day, the defendant stabbing a man to death. And I'm sick of your take-the-punch-take-the-bribe attitude, Your Honour, and your jury's too. The evidence is right there, so why no arrest? Because the defendant comes from a good family? Or because the pile of cash under the table is just too hard to pass up? You know what? As counsel for the plaintiff, I'm going to walk outside and tell the world I won. There is only one truth, and if you disagree it's because you're a pack of nitpicking self-servers. I WIN. BYE-BYE. CLOSE THE COURT." — Xander Badman of Monsieur Saul's Law Firm

Eloquence is your power to argue and debate logically, whether you're stuck in a battle of wits or a high courtroom. Believe in yourself, pick apart every word out of your opponent's mouth, cook up something clever and fire it back with confidence.

- **Effect:** Brain Debuff DS bonus; unlocks rhetoric options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you must win EVERY conversation, and you'll use every trick there is, whether the other side meant harm or not. Good or bad? Depends.
- **Good for:** lawyers, preachers, politicians, keyboard warriors. **Bad for:** quiet types, people who hate arguing, people who'd rather argue with their fists.

#### 16 · Visualization — α of Expression

> "Thinking it in your head is not enough! Master Sakamoto once spent four months mixing just 200 grams of watercolour. He called it 'Thousand-Autumn Red.' And if you must know: I've watched the video of him mixing it over a thousand times, and I still can't make that colour. No one can guess what you might do with a few rose stems, glue and an egg yolk. I wonder what 'Afternoon of Blood' looked like inside his mind. But the undeniable truth is that the way he carried that thought from his head onto paper is irresistible. A beauty only he could paint." — Rémy Étienne, the Second Broom-Bearer

Visualization is your power to picture the world: to take the shapeless thoughts in your head and express them outward as images.

- **Effect:** Sigil Range bonus and Elemental Damage; gates image-making equipment.
- **At high levels:** you see the story behind every landscape and make landscapes out of every story. You risk sinking into faraway worlds and forgetting this one.
- **Good for:** critics, painters, Projector Gear® users. **Bad for:** the near-sighted, people who'd rather feel than describe.

#### 17 · Analysis — β of Expression

> "Look at the crime scene again. First: this man was never shot. He may be stuck to a wall full of bullet holes, but bullets didn't kill him. Why? Pick up a casing and look closely. Not an enhanced round; nobody is crazy enough to load enhanced rounds into an SMG. It smells of Outburst mana, a foreign Outburst to be exact. Second: the body stayed stuck to the wall until we pulled it off. Some strong force pushed him from the opposite side, and it wasn't an enhanced round. One .700 Blackpoint? Maybe. Fifty .32 Decrkm? Definitely not. From the mana, the victim was hit by an Outburst. The casings and holes say the killer wanted us to believe he died to bullets, enhanced rounds specifically. Send the apology letter. We need to find a spy from Bazaim." — D12.9.6 of the CCEC (Central City Espionage Commission)

Analysis is your ability to break the world down: work through any situation logically and land on the most correct answer.

- **Effect:** Analyze Range; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you over-analyse everything until your view narrows to a slit. Once you pick a detail, your brain glues itself to it and forgets a case usually has more than one.
- **Good for:** detectives, hunters, assassins, secret agents. **Bad for:** Orks, Rockmen, barbarians.

#### 18 · Charisma — γ of Expression

> "I asked whether he'd like a glass of water or anything. Honestly, officers, that afternoon was the first time I'd met Mr. Duboi, and everything in my statement is true. Granted, keeping an electric chair in one's office isn't for everyone, but I'm a lover of the arts. I adore bold, satirical work; how could I not display one of the masterpieces of my collection? It was a regrettable accident. That chair was never used until Mr. Duboi volunteered himself into it. How was I to know he would switch the power on himself and die so needlessly? Tell the court I'll be there. A gentleman like me never passes up a conversation he is certain to win." — Damian Nox, SuperUltraHyperExclusive Producer for Sibugar Entertainment

Charisma is the power of words: how well you persuade and steer a conversation, and how warm you come across while doing it. Extremely handy for lying and making things up.

- **Effect:** Brain Debuff save roll; unlocks persuasion options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you can talk anyone into anything, but all that warmth makes some people see you as a spineless bootlicker. Depends who you're talking to.
- **Good for:** politicians, merchants, lawyers. **Bad for:** antiheroes, the antisocial, anyone who hates looking soft in a conversation.

### Soul

#### 19 · Empathy — α of Insight

> "I'd rather have been the one who took the bullet, you understand? Operation Daggerfall really did change the course of history. I know. I was there. What they praise and trumpet is partly true. But what they forgot? Too much.
>
> I saw the eyes of a boy, sixteen or so, half burned black, lying still two metres above a pool of blood. What was he thinking before judgment day?
>
> I saw the eyes of a father, half red, half black, fingers trembling as he cradled the body of a Crawler smaller than the rest. Right before Captain Kain called in another barrage. What was he thinking before those deafening blasts?
>
> I saw. Horror... horror and pain, grief and helplessness. Oh, I'd give my whole fortune to forget, you understand? Forget the horror in their eyes, the horror I still feel in my chest, the horror no words can describe. The horror of an empty face hanging above an iron stake." — Paul Remarque

Empathy is the bridge between one person and another: feeling what someone else is going through, putting yourself where they stand, and understanding at least partly why they do what they do.

- **Effect:** Outburst Cast Time reduction and Elemental Damage.
- **At high levels:** you become one with humanity. You understand everyone's circumstances, and everyone's story gets into you. You cry other people's tears and stick your nose into business that isn't yours.
- **Good for:** therapists, human lie detectors, good alignments. **Bad for:** chaotic alignments, serial killers, non-humanoids.

#### 20 · Camaraderie — β of Insight

> "If you don't want to get brainwashed, I'd stay away from that Souji. 'The power of friendship,' then 'the bond of hearts'; he says that stuff without a shred of embarrassment. He wants to befriend everyone he meets and goes around calling people 'comrade' before he's even graduated. Go into a fight with him and before you know it half the enemy team are on our side. Last week he talked that platinum-haired girl from the rival school onto our team, just like that. Is that insane or what? Jealous? Why on earth would I be jealous? Completely ridiculous!" — Anastasia Vandenberg of Kemizuchi Military Academy

Camaraderie is your teamwork and your solidarity with your squad, plus your knack for making friends and building factions.

- **Effect:** Heal/Shield Power; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you become the heart of any team you join. You start seeing the team as its own little world and judge outsiders harder than you mean to.
- **Good for:** captains, team players. **Bad for:** lone wolves, edgelords, murder hobos.

#### 21 · Implication — γ of Insight

> "Jazmine, Jazmine of the dying afternoon, lead the lost soul away from breaking.
>
> Ask ten people in the recon squad and nine won't want to share a transport with me. They say the way I talk isn't easy on the ears. I can rarely sit with someone ten minutes before something about them grates on me, and saying it plainly would be a little... vulgar. So I use metaphor. A faint sweat stain becomes 'the perfume of corpse-flowers in the Sonz marsh.' An uncleaned rifle becomes 'a homemade death at dawn.' I won't hide it: watching them fume without a comeback always brightens my heart a little.
>
> I've lived alone between earth and sky too long for one or two more haters to matter. But Jazmine is something else. Coarse as an old man from Chezknov, and yet sometimes... gentle as a golden lotus in a stinking marsh. Can your smile melt my heart? Hard to say. The one thing I know for certain: you make me want to be a better man." — Aleksandr Donovik Artem of Rigaz Trade Company ADCorp

Implication is your command of metaphor. You catch people's meaning when they talk in figures, you understand difficult poetry and literature, and it helps a great deal with lying and with flirting. (With literature. It can't generate a credit card.)

- **Effect:** Heart Debuff DS bonus; unlocks metaphor options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** you drown in the meaning behind every word. Every metaphor lands, but you fall into the "yes means no, no means yes" trap.
- **Good for:** poets, writers, artists, the hopelessly lovestruck. **Bad for:** the antisocial, blunt people, paladins.

#### 22 · Will Power — α of Determination

> "Yes, your heart can shatter like silken tofu. Connecting to the twin world is no joke. Pulling a few little sparks over ten minutes, anyone with a cheap Emanator can do that. A bolt over ten thousand volts in two seconds? Try it and you'll learn what a Mana Short is. There was a Linker once who didn't just bleed internally; he shorted so hard his heart burst on the spot and left a hole in his chest. Outburst craft needs a pure heart, the strength to resist temptation, and the understanding to hold the connection. Now breathe deep and meditate until your doubts fall away!" — Nora the "Witch" of Arkvelton

Will Power is the strength of your heart: generosity and spiritual sharpness. A person's soul lives in the heart, and Will Power measures both your mental strength and the strength of the soul inside you. Essential for Outbursts and Emanators.

- **Effect:** Outburst Damage and Mana Syphon; gates heart-based equipment.
- **At high levels:** your heart and soul turn hard and strong. You connect easily with the supernatural, but you slip easily into delusion, because the link to your upper-realm soul is so strong you can't tell this world from that one.
- **Good for:** Emanator users, monks, crusaders, Bonders. **Bad for:** Evoker users, scientists, melee users.

#### 23 · Lore Master — β of Determination

> "Mother lived to hear every story from a thousand worlds. It was a small dream from when I was young and foolish, a spark struck the moment I walked into the modest little library my father kept in secret. It grew into a roaring fire in my heart: a hunger to touch every stream of knowledge that science calls baseless. The marches of the half-horse Zarkast tribe. Tales of wicked witches in the woods outside Albert Town. Rumours of magic gates that transform those who pass. Love stories between humans and beings of the upper realm.
>
> Mother wanted to know, to understand, to hold the chance to prove it true. And after more than a hundred years of study, they chose Mother for this seat. The one who makes the unreal real." — Andrade Lovelace, "Mother" of the Institute of Forgotten Knowledge

Lore Master is how well you adapt to and absorb supernatural knowledge, the kind science dismisses as baseless. You understand how it works without leaning on dangerous shortcuts like charms or spiritual links.

- **Effect:** Outburst Range bonus; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you can tell at once whether something is the work of the supernatural and how to deal with it. Comfortable among mages, but you start rejecting proven science because you see the supernatural threads behind it.
- **Good for:** dreamers, divine casters, spellswords. **Bad for:** scientists, hackers, barbarians.

#### 24 · Composure — γ of Determination

> "In the ring, kid, the first punch you throw doesn't come from your fist. What you need is your eyes! When he walks at you, arms up and chest out, you stand still. When your lips feel his boozy breath, you stand still. When his bloodthirsty stare locks on yours, you stand stiller. Only when his eyes slip off yours do you get to move. The only problem is, the fight ended long before that." — Kaizer Meyer Reinch Wolf of Central Command Center

Composure is your ability to stay calm in any situation. Neither a monster at midnight nor the woman waiting at the end of the alley can move your face an inch.

- **Effect:** Heart Debuff save roll; unlocks calm options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** your face goes stone in every situation. Great when someone threatens or interrogates you; terrible for anyone who just wants to be friendly.
- **Good for:** security guards, police, iron men, lead men. **Bad for:** small bois, shy bois, studs and sluts.

#### 25 · Faith — α of Virtue

> "Holy Father Nerukhan taught that the love between people is everlasting. The world above where He reigns is no different from the world of men. But as a keeper of the saint's faith, I cannot sit idle before their contemptible deeds one more day.
>
> Only the saints may judge and carry out justice. But delivering the sinners into their hands? That is our job.
>
> *racks shotgun*
>
> *heavy metal plays*" — Sister October of the Lumeri Church

Faith is your ability to place your trust in higher powers. In a world where anything can happen, plenty of people face life's cruelty on the strength of belief. And unlike real life, here the odds of a prayer coming true are better than 1%.

- **Effect:** Blessing Power and Blessing cooldown reduction; gates devotional equipment.
- **At high levels:** your bond with your faith tightens and amplifies everything your religion gives you. Everyone else sees an insufferable zealot, or worse, a heretic.
- **Good for:** crusaders, divine classes, cultists, missionaries. **Bad for:** atheists, scientists, smartasses.

#### 26 · Conscience — β of Virtue

> "It is not a question of right or wrong. Nor of left or right. In a world of upheaval and chaos, the only thing that makes a God or a Demon is conscience." — The Azure Blood Holder

Conscience is your ability to tell right from wrong, and to see the good or harm in an act not just now but far into the future, not just for you but for all of humanity.

- **Effect:** more Karma earned; unlocks exploration options and Trio-Scale Equipment.
- **At high levels:** you understand the consequences of everything you're about to do. But you think from one angle only, good or evil, right or wrong, and yours is the only one that counts.
- **Good for:** lawful alignments, living saints, Satan in human skin. **Bad for:** neutral alignments, emotionless robots, weathervanes.

#### 27 · Authority — γ of Virtue

> "I don't need to tell you about Hank Ladan's war record, kid. Everything you need, you'll get from watching how people treat him.
>
> A skinny frame in a plain, no-frills uniform. Easy stance, warm laugh. Nobody would guess Hank Ladan outranks a marshal, and nobody dares look him in the eye, or at the five stars on his shoulder. Not even a guy like me who won three straight wrestling tournaments in the Marine mess hall. Something comes off him that makes your knees want to buckle, out of respect or fear. He has the nepo babies over in the Air branch falling in line, and every agent in the enforcement bureau bows when he passes. A busted-up marine like me? Honestly, I'd rather hunt ten Ork fangs in one afternoon than hear one word of criticism from him." — Brengan Springfield, the "Juggernaut" of the 101st Hellraiser Division

Authority is the measure of cool. Anyone can put on a suit; Authority decides whether you look cool in it. Be the groomsman and still draw more of the bride's eyes and respect than the groom. Oh, so dignified.

- **Effect:** more Reputation earned; unlocks dominance options in exploration and dialogue.
- **At high levels:** people mistake you for a general, a leader or a top CEO, which raises the odds you get investigated and make the news for buying apples at the market instead of the supermarket.
- **Good for:** soldiers, police, security, teachers, anyone who has to be cool. **Bad for:** sugar babies, fuckboys (you risk ending up married instead of a one-night stand).

## Designer's notes

This edition is a translation, not a redesign. Where the original contradicts itself, it picks one reading and records the choice here, so your table can overrule it.

**1. Number conflicts between Article 3 and Article 6**

| Item | Article 3 says | Article 6 says | This edition uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength, Physical Damage | +10% | +20% | +10% (matches Raw Power's +5% scale) |
| Intelligence, Sigil Damage | +10% | +15% | +10% (matches Brain Power's +10%) |
| Intelligence, Sigil Tier | +1 tier per 2 points | +0.5 tier per point | Same rule, kept |
| Virtue, Blessing cooldown | −1 turn | −0.5 turn | −0.5 turn |
| Endurance, limb damage | reduces damage taken by 10% | "−10% Limb Damage Reduction" (sign flipped) | −10% damage taken |
| Faith, Blessing cooldown | reduces cooldown | "+0.5 Blessing Cooldown" | −0.5 turn |

**2. Open rules questions**

- "One Core point represents two Attribute points": is that an exchange rate, or does it mean an Attribute's cap is 2× its Core? The Comrade Cat example says the cap equals the Core.
- How are Attribute points earned after creation?
- What is the target number for a skill roll, and is it d20 + skill?
- What does each Action Point buy (move, shoot, draw a Sigil, cast an Outburst)?
- What is Trio-Scale Equipment?
- What do Analyze, Ambush and Detection Range measure, and in what unit?
- What do Karma and Reputation unlock?

**3. Lore inconsistencies**

- Professor Avdol draws sigils with an *Evoker*, and that is now canon: Evokers draw Sigils, and Emanators serve Soul magic (Outbursts). The rest of this edition follows it.
- The sheet says "Soul Debuff Save Roll"; the skills say "Heart Debuff." Pick one name.

**4. Spelling normalised in this edition**

Outbust → Outburst; Evolker → Evoker; Inteligent → Intelligence; Thresshold → Threshold; Charima → Charisma; Consience → Conscience; Sigil Dawn → Sigil Draw; Based Attribute → Attribute.

**5. Systems referenced but not yet written**

Sigils, Outbursts and Blessings (casting, costs, tiers); weapons and damage; armour classes per body slot; Weapon Mastery levels; race passives; Leadership; *EPCCG: Trait Edition*; *EPCCG: Perk Edition*.

**6. The missing Disco piece**

In Disco Elysium the skills talk. PHC kept the skill web but not the voices. A one-line rule would bring them back:

> **Inner Voice.** When a scene starts, the GM makes a hidden passive check for each player's highest γ skill. On a success, that skill whispers one line to that player only: a hint, a suspicion, or a bad idea. The player decides whether to listen.

**7. Recovered from Discord.** The skill track (Article 4) and the Thousand Faces trait (Article 2) come from screenshots of the group's Discord; the Trait and Perk Editions themselves are lost.

- Partly answered by the Trait Edition: trait modifiers can't unlock perks. Still open: does a trait's flat bonus remove the −5 untrained penalty, or only add to the roll?
- What did Skill Mastery 1–4 and Mastered give for each skill? These were probably the perks.
