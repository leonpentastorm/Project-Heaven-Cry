# Factions: draft web

**Status: Suggested, except where marked canon** (Bazaim and the Thousand-Planet City's naming, accepted 5 Nov 2026). A proposal for how the EPCCG's factions relate, for Leon to keep, change or cut. Canon facts it builds on are in the [EPCCG](EPCCG-Player-Character-Creation-Guide.md) and the [World Bible](Narrators-World-Bible.md). It uses only names and details already in those texts, and adds relationships.

## The five tensions

Every faction sits on at least one of these. Each is a campaign engine.

| Tension | One side | Other side | The skill voices that argue it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Centre vs frontier** | Central City writes the manuals and the laws | The Thousand-Planet City digs, fights and spends robots | Authority vs Camaraderie |
| **Saints vs demons** | The Lumeri Church: pact Celestials are saints | Celestials who cause mayhem are demons, and Anti-Heaven Operations Command fights them | Faith vs Conscience |
| **Profit vs people** | The Rigaz Trade Company and the 093rd's quotas | Robots, conscripts, Crawlers, anyone listed as cargo | Ingenuity vs Empathy |
| **Curse vs love** | Humanity First, and the Daggerfall victory story | Mixed families, and the people who've seen through the curse | Intimidation vs Implication |
| **Science vs the unreal** | The Science Team: magic is words a machine obeys | The Institute of Forgotten Knowledge: the gods are people | Rumination vs Lore Master |

The last row is the World Bible's "each side is half right", made into politics.

## The blocs

### 1. Central City (Earth): the old centre

- **Central Command Center** commands humanity's war with Heaven. Kaizer Meyer Reinch Wolf is its face: stillness as policy.
- **Anti-Heaven Operations Command** *(origin canon)* is older than Central City by about 5,000 years: founded by **the Progenitors**, a thousand isekai-powered humans who learned the truth and were hunted to extinction. Now it lives inside Central City's state as the Department of Extra-Celestial Defense, run by the Progenitors' semi-powered descendants, who fight politics more than enemies and will do anything for power, because without them a Celestial's next visit wipes humanity out. Its mission has decayed from overthrowing Heaven to damage control. It **issues the EPCCG**; Wick is its mascot.
- **Hellraiser Divisions (101st, 237th)** are AHOC's frontline. They fight Orks on frontier worlds and go wherever a Celestial has torn something open. Dominik Edelgard, Brengan Springfield and Hank Ladan.
- **CCEC (Central City Espionage Commission)** hunts spies from Bazaim. D12.9.6.
- **Science Team** is state-funded Sigil research, scientists not philosophers. Professor Avdol.
- **Wants:** order, the Veil kept (without knowing it), Celestials contained.
- **Fears:** the frontier going its own way; anything it can't classify.

### 2. The Thousand-Planet City: the frontier empire

- **Canon:** *Thousand-Planet City* is the faction's name; its army is the **Thousand World Legion** (the EPCCG's "Thousand-Planet City Army").
- A sprawling human polity in another galaxy. It answers to Central City on paper and to its own quotas in practice.
- **The Thousand World Legion** includes the **093rd Regiment**, resource extraction that treats robots as disposable (MPAT, Snowden "Eye" Wright), and the **Outposts** (Outpost-1, Outpost-813, Outpost-921). Colonel Alexei Novikov commands at Outpost-1.
- **Wants:** platinum, cores, quota met.
- **Fears:** the robot question, and Central City auditing what happened at M813.

### 3. The Lumeri Church: saints and shotguns

- **Doctrine:** the saints of the upper realm are real and good, and Holy Father Nerukhan speaks for them. Celestials who cause mayhem are **demons**, false gods. The Church blesses the war against demons with the saints' own power.
- **The irony:** saints and demons are the same kind of being, Celestials in or out of a pact. Under the World Bible, Nerukhan is a Celestial in a pact playing a saint. The Church's whole theology is a sorting rule for Celestials that it doesn't know it's applying.
- **Its arm:** **the Adamantile Brigade**, a knightly order sworn to the Church and lent to the front. Knight Jiren, Knight Andre Giant Fist, Sazmin. Lumeri sisters serve as chaplain-gunners: Sister October, Sister March.
- **Wants:** souls, rites kept, demons put down. **Fears:** the Knowing.

### 4. Money and crime

- **Rigaz Trade Company (RTC™®)** runs armed jobs for old Rourigaz, and its **ADCorp** division arms and supplies both Central City and the Thousand-Planet City. Vermilion Jasmine, Aleksandr Donovik Artem. **Hook:** the platinum on the engineers' last ship out of M813 was RTC contract cargo. A cargo clause outranked twenty-seven robots.
- **Smetanita Conjunction**, a crime family, runs the frontier economy RTC won't touch: gold coins outside Central City, black-market cores and enhanced rounds, "shipments" from Mandestra merchants. Tommy Avencio, the Papa. Supplies what the Thousand-Planet City rations.
- **Wants:** margin. **Fears:** a war that ends.

### 5. Schools and the unreal

- **Kemizuchi Military Academy**, and **its rival school across town**, train officers. **Hook:** they were built for the NPC-2 creator's battle-academy story before the curse. The rival school quietly takes in mixed families' children, curse-lifted, with abilities. Souji's "platinum-haired girl from the rival school" may be one.
- **Arkvelton** is the home of the Soul-magic witches, an autonomous enclave tolerated because the army needs Soul casters. Nora the Witch.
- **The southern sects** fuse martial arts and Sigils (Blade Scribes). Avdol sneers at them.
- **Institute of Forgotten Knowledge** studies what science calls baseless. Andrade Lovelace, Mother. The World Bible's obvious home for a hidden demigod.

### 6. Outside the walls

- **Black Scavengers** survive in the ruined lowlands past the desert, on Earth. **Hook:** the lowlands were wrecked by Daggerfall's spillover, since the NPC-2 world was next door, and nobody rebuilt. Yukimura.
- **Bazaim** *(canon)* is a human (NPC-1) power that made peace with Daggerfall's survivors through mixed families, the nation of lifted curses. Its people include espers, which is why the CCEC finds "a foreign Outburst" at its crime scenes. To Central City it's a spy state harbouring monsters. To Bazaim, Central City is the power that nuked a world of people.
- **Orks** are a Celestial's creation, built from real-Earth games (World Bible, Suggested). The Hellraisers' standing enemy.
- **Crawlers (NPC-2 survivors)** are scattered after Daggerfall, cursed, hunted, sometimes loved.

## Starting a campaign from the web

A Celestial causes mayhem (World Bible, "How campaigns start"). Then ask which tension it lands on:

- A god demands worship on a mining world: centre vs frontier, plus saints vs demons.
- A Celestial "frees" a regiment's robots: profit vs people.
- A Celestial lifts the curse on a whole Crawler town for a day: curse vs love.

The party's factions decide whose side they're forced onto.
