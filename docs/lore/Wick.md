# Wick

Canon decided 5 Nov 2026. Sections marked **Suggested** are proposals for the Narrator, not canon.

## Who Wick is

Wick is the mascot of the **EPCCG**, the Emergency Player Character Creation Guide: Anti-Heaven Operations Command's training manual, "approved, internal use only." Every trait and perk in the manual is illustrated with Wick doing it, badly or bravely.

Command's illustrators drew Wick after the field automata of the **258th Medical Unit**, MPAT's unit. That is where its features come from:

- **Ceramic shell and chibi build:** a 258th field unit, drawn the way armies draw mascots.
- **Screen face:** 258th automata show status and expressions on a face screen.
- **Lantern heart:** where a 258th unit carries its triage lamp. The circle-and-cross on it is the 258th's medical cross.
- **Capelet:** the unit's field cloak, cut short.

## Why it matters

The army that ordered the 093rd Regiment's robots to "shut yourselves down" on M813 prints a friendly robot medic on the cover of its own manual. Wick is cheerful because Command wants it cheerful. It doesn't know what happened on M813. The players may.

That gives every icon a second reading, especially the ugly ones. When the manual illustrates **Humanity First** ("robots are tools") with Wick, the cheerfulness of the drawing against the meaning of the trait is the joke and the wound at once. Don't sand that off, and don't explain it in game text.

## In the world

- Wick is a **printed figure**, not an NPC. People know it from the manual, recruitment posters, ration-tin stickers and training reels.
- NPCs don't think of Wick as a person. The Veil is untouched: Wick is propaganda art, not a clue about the simulation.
- The name "Wick" is what the troops call it. Whether that's its official name is unrecorded.

## Suggested

- **The model.** Command never says which 258th unit Wick was drawn from. A Narrator can make it a medic who served alongside MPAT, possibly one of the robots the engineers destroyed at the hatch on M813.
- **Defaced manuals.** Robots in the 093rd scratch or paint over Wick's smile in their copies. Finding a defaced EPCCG is a quiet way to show a player what the machines think.
- **Scenes.** A recruitment poster of Wick on Outpost-1, above the repair bay MPAT carried its comrade into.
