# The Earth Sector

The Sector the EPCCG describes, and the default starting Sector. It holds Earth, humanity's homeworld, and Central City. **Canon** facts come from the EPCCG and the World Bible; anything marked **Suggested** is a proposal for Leon. The name "Earth Sector" itself is *Suggested*.

## Sheet

| Field | Earth Sector |
|---|---|
| **Span** | Several galaxies. **Earth** and **Central City** in one; the **Thousand-Planet City** and its Legion's outposts (Outpost-1, Outpost-813, Outpost-921) in another; **Bazaim**; the frontier worlds where the Hellraisers fight Orks; the dead world nuked in Operation Daggerfall, next to Earth. |
| **Places named in the EPCCG** | Central City and its inner ring; the desert and the ruined **lowlands** past it (on Earth); **Mandestra** (merchants); **Arganzaz** (a forbidden ruin Ragoul walked into); **Albert Town** (witch stories in the woods outside it); the **Sonz marsh**; **Chezknov**; **Arkvelton**; **Kemizuchi** and the rival school across town; the south (the sects); planet **M813** and black hole **B813**. |
| **Calendar** | Central City founding calendar. Daggerfall about 3800; M813 in 4084. Many powers here are far older than the count. |
| **Powers** | The full web is in [Factions.md](Factions.md): Anti-Heaven Operations Command; Central City; the Thousand-Planet City; the Lumeri Church; the Rigaz Trade Company and the Smetanita Conjunction; the schools and enclaves; Bazaim and the lands outside the walls. |
| **Tensions** | Centre vs frontier · saints vs demons · profit vs people · curse vs love · science vs the unreal ([Factions.md](Factions.md)). |
| **Texture** | Guns everywhere: revolvers, SMGs, shotguns, the .32 Decrkm, the .700 Blackpoint. Enhanced rounds are rare and expensive. Bank cards in Central City, gold coins outside it. Sigils are a science (the Science Team), Soul magic is witchcraft (Arkvelton), Blessings are religion (the Lumeri Church). Robots are built by the Factory and treated as disposable. |
| **What locals believe** | Humanity is at war with Heaven, "something from the upper realm". Daggerfall was a victory. Crawlers are monsters. The Progenitors were heroes who fought false gods. |
| **What it leaves out** | Everything in the World Bible: the simulation, that the Celestials are people, that Crawlers are human, what Daggerfall really was. |

## Origins

Pick one at creation. Format and the local-knowledge rule are in [Sectors.md](Sectors.md). Skills suggested are for presets only.

### 1. Central City
- **Home:** Earth, the capital of the old centre.
- **Upbringing:** precincts, bank cards, shock batons, somebody else doing the dirty work.
- **You know:** city services, officials, the precincts, the inner ring's gossip, how to get anything done with paperwork.
- **People see you as:** soft, rich, connected, out of place past the walls.
- **Skills:** Charisma, Ingenuity, Authority.
- **Traits that fit:** Central City Kid, Inner Ring Heir, Slicer, Wanted, Machine-Faced, Right of Way.
- **Hook:** a family account someone at the CCEC is watching.

### 2. The Thousand-Planet frontier
- **Home:** an extraction world, outpost or mining moon of the Thousand-Planet City.
- **Upbringing:** quotas, shift sirens, robots doing the jobs nobody survives.
- **You know:** mining, logistics, outpost life, the Thousand World Legion's ranks and rackets.
- **People see you as:** hard-working, expendable, owned by the quota.
- **Skills:** Proficiency, Durability, Camaraderie.
- **Traits that fit:** Glass Runner, Heavyworlder, Symbol of Order, Field Medic, Moon Hunter.
- **Hook:** the foreman who still owes you a shift's pay.

### 3. Hellraiser camps
- **Home:** the barracks worlds and mess halls behind the Hellraiser front.
- **Upbringing:** a soldier's child, or a soldier from sixteen. Orks were the bedtime story, and then the job.
- **You know:** Hellraiser doctrine, Orks, weapons, field rations, which officers get people killed.
- **People see you as:** brave, loud, disposable, a thug to the polite.
- **Skills:** Dexterity, Guts, Athletics.
- **Traits that fit:** Forlorn Hope, Spray and Pray, Grenadier's Arm, Live to Fight Another Day, Squad Anchor.
- **Hook:** a sergeant who'd take you back, and a deserter who wants you to say nothing.

### 4. Lumeri cloisters
- **Home:** a cloister or pilgrim world of the Lumeri Church.
- **Upbringing:** rites, scripture, the saints' feast days, nuns who could field-strip a shotgun.
- **You know:** the Church, its saints and Holy Father Nerukhan, its rites, its politics, its charity.
- **People see you as:** devout, trustworthy, judgemental, the Church's eyes.
- **Skills:** Faith, Conscience, Composure.
- **Traits that fit:** Mind Without Fog, True Believer, Heretic, Pacifist.
- **Hook:** the abbess who expects a report.

### 5. The Adamantile Brigade
- **Home:** a fortress-house of the knightly order.
- **Upbringing:** squire to a knight who slept in snake pits and arm-wrestled concrete for fun.
- **You know:** the Brigade's oaths and feuds, heavy arms, endurance, the Church's ceremonies.
- **People see you as:** honourable, terrifying, a little absurd.
- **Skills:** Raw Power, Pain Threshold, Authority.
- **Traits that fit:** Sworn Sibling, Pain Eater, Squad Anchor.
- **Hook:** the knight you squired for, still alive, still asking for help with breakfast.

### 6. Kemizuchi, or the school across town
- **Home:** the officer academy, or its rival.
- **Upbringing:** drills, rankings, rivalries, friendship speeches nobody was embarrassed by.
- **You know:** officers, tactics, the old-boy network, the rival school's secrets (or yours).
- **People see you as:** a future officer, arrogant, privileged.
- **Skills:** Camaraderie, Authority, Analysis.
- **Traits that fit:** Blade Recluse, Drill Echo, Rival, Born Officer.
- **Hook:** your classmate, now outranking you. *(Suggested: the rival school quietly takes mixed families' children.)*

### 7. Arkvelton
- **Home:** the witches' enclave.
- **Upbringing:** breathing exercises, meditation, the heart's link to the twin world, friends who shorted out.
- **You know:** Soul magic, Emanators, Mana Shorts, witch lore, the stories outside Albert Town.
- **People see you as:** a witch, gifted, dangerous, faintly heretical.
- **Skills:** Will Power, Empathy, Lore Master.
- **Traits that fit:** Upper-Touched, Hollow Heart, Heaven's Faithful.
- **Hook:** the teacher who told you never to come back without the thing you took.

### 8. The southern sects
- **Home:** the martial sects of the south.
- **Upbringing:** forms, katas and Sigils drawn with fists and blades. Professor Avdol sneers at people like you.
- **You know:** martial arts, the sects' lineages and feuds, Blade Scribe lore.
- **People see you as:** a mystic brawler, superstitious, unscientific.
- **Skills:** Athletics, Raw Power, Visualization.
- **Traits that fit:** Southern Fist, Blade Recluse.
- **Hook:** a sect elder who wants a scroll back.

### 9. The scholar halls
- **Home:** the Science Team's labs, a university, or the Institute of Forgotten Knowledge.
- **Upbringing:** libraries, lectures, Evokers, or banned books.
- **You know:** Sigil theory, history, science (or the occult), who's publishing what.
- **People see you as:** clever, useless in a fight, insufferable.
- **Skills:** Brain Power, Rumination, Lore Master.
- **Traits that fit:** Sigil Savant, Occultist, Bottle Butts for Eyes, Cold Case.
- **Hook:** a research partner who credited themselves for your work.

### 10. Mandestra and the trade lanes
- **Home:** a merchant house, a market stall, or a Rigaz Trade Company posting.
- **Upbringing:** haggling, ledgers, armed caravans, contracts with small print.
- **You know:** prices, routes, merchants, the RTC, who's smuggling what.
- **People see you as:** greedy, useful, untrustworthy.
- **Skills:** Charisma, Implication, Ingenuity.
- **Traits that fit:** Market Blood, Company Man, Cover Story, Lunarshine.
- **Hook:** a creditor, and a shipment that never arrived.

### 11. The gold-coin frontier
- **Home:** a frontier town past Central City's reach, where gold coins are money and the Smetanita Conjunction is the law.
- **Upbringing:** saloons, debts, favours, the Papa's people on every corner.
- **You know:** the black market, crime families, smuggling, frontier law.
- **People see you as:** a hustler, dangerous, possibly owned.
- **Skills:** Intimidation, Reaction Speed, Ingenuity.
- **Traits that fit:** Gambler, Wanted, Cover Story, Rogue Element.
- **Hook:** a favour you owe the Conjunction.

### 12. The lowlands
- **Home:** Earth's ruined lowlands past the desert: Black Scavenger country, a forest of dead buildings.
- **Upbringing:** salvage, ambushes, throwing first.
- **You know:** ruins, scavenging, traps, survival, the Black Scavengers.
- **People see you as:** feral, quick, a thief.
- **Skills:** Reaction Speed, Vigilant, Dexterity.
- **Traits that fit:** Lowland Born, Insomniac, Demolitionist.
- **Hook:** the scavenger crew that thinks you're dead.

### 13. Bazaim
- **Home:** the human nation that made peace with Daggerfall's survivors.
- **Upbringing:** neighbours with abilities, families mixed by love, Central City's spies everywhere.
- **You know:** espers, the truth about Crawlers (you've seen them as people), Bazaim's politics, how the CCEC operates.
- **People see you as (in Central City):** a spy, a traitor, a monster-lover.
- **Skills:** Empathy, Implication, Vigilant.
- **Traits that fit:** Crawler-Blooded *(needs a review against the one-way curse)*, Cover Story, Heaven's Faithful.
- **Hook:** someone in your family is a Crawler, and you can see them.

### 14. The Ork borderlands
- **Home:** the frontier where human towns sit next to Ork warbands.
- **Upbringing:** raids, truces, trading with the enemy, maybe raised by them.
- **You know:** Orks, their warbands and parley, border survival.
- **People see you as:** half-savage, a traitor to some, a guide to others.
- **Skills:** Raw Power, Intimidation, Guts.
- **Traits that fit:** Raised by Orks, Beast Handler, Moon Hunter.
- **Hook:** the warband that wants you home.

Daggerfall veterans can come from any origin (the Daggerfall Veteran and Old Timer traits cover them); people here live to about 400.

## Starting hooks (Suggested)

1. **M813.** The one-shot: MPAT's story ([MPAT-and-M813.md](MPAT-and-M813.md)).
2. **A god-plot on a mining world.** An abandoned Celestial plot on a Thousand-Planet extraction world still demands worship, and the quota is due.
3. **The town where the curse is lifted.** Inside one Crawler town's walls, every human sees the Crawlers as people. Outside, the Hellraisers are coming.
4. **A summoning in Central City.** Someone in the inner ring summoned a Celestial on purpose, and the headquarters chapter wants it buried.
