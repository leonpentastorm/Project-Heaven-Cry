## The fight music (Project Heaven Cry OST)

The engine picks the track (`fightTheme`, `noteFightSong` and `musicFor` in `packages/engine/src/music.ts`): an ordinary
fight gets one of the nine songs of the pool (`FIGHT_POOL`: Gunline and the eight of 11 Nov 2026), picked at random when
the fight begins, never the last ordinary fight's, and kept in the game's state for the whole fight; Heaven Falls and
Last Rounds take over by their own rules; the Proving Ground plays Gearing Up while the squad is gearing up. The browser
plays it from the file through Web Audio (`apps/web/src/game/ost.ts`). The files end on Suno's hard cut, so they are not looped as
files: each pass runs from a loop-in to a loop-out point and crossfades into the next pass (equal power), which makes
the loop seamless. The points live in `OST_FILES` in `ost.ts`:

| Track | Loop in | Loop out | Seam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gunline | 0.4 s (silence before it) | 67.7 s (the final hit's tail starts) | 1.2 s |
| Heaven Falls | 0.1 s | 135.5 s (the cut) | 1.5 s |
| Last Rounds | 0.1 s | 81.0 s (heartbeat and static at both ends) | 1.2 s |
| Brass on the Floor | 0.05 s | 54.24 s (124 beats at 140.3 bpm after the loop in; the last bar and the ring-out from 55.2 s are left out) | 1.2 s |
| Danger Close | 0.05 s | 59.5 s (the cut is at 59.56 s; the intro has no beat to line up with) | 1.2 s |
| Flank Left | 0.1 s | 60.5 s (148 beats at 150 bpm; the closing phrase's last bar, to the cut at 61.7 s, is left out) | 1.2 s |
| Held Breath | 0.1 s | 58.89 s (192 beats at 200 bpm; the last hits and the tail from 60.1 s are left out) | 1.2 s |
| Hold the Line | 0 s (no silence at the head) | 60.1 s (the tail starts) | 1.2 s |
| Line of Four | 0 s | 69.2 s (the last hit, 66.9 to 68.4 s, rings out into the quiet intro, which builds again) | 1.2 s |
| Mag Check | 0.05 s | 59.84 s (136 beats at 139.3 bpm; the cut is at 61.0 s) | 1.2 s |
| Suppressing Fire | 0 s | 55.4 s (on the beat at 145.8 bpm; the cut is at 56.8 s, silence after it) | 1.2 s |
| Gearing Up | 0.1 s | 173.68 s (the hand-over starts on the second of the three closing hits, 171.73 s, where the intro's own first hit lands; the file's fade-out from 175 s is never reached) | 2.0 s |

How the points of 11 Nov 2026 were set: the head and the tail of each file measured (RMS in 50 ms windows, mono, 48 kHz:
where the sound starts, where the ending drops or cuts); the tempo and the beat's phase at both ends from the onset
envelope; where both ends have a steady beat, the pass (loop in to the start of the seam) is a whole number of bars, so
the beat carries across. Each seam was then rendered (equal-power crossfade, as `ost.ts` plays it) and checked: its RMS
in 100 ms windows stays within 3 dB of the range of the 3 s on either side of it (no dip, no bump), and its largest
sample-to-sample step is about the music's own on either side or smaller (no click). Line of Four is the one that changes
level across its seam on purpose (a last hit, then the quiet intro).

Changing track crossfades over 1.2 s; the fight's end fades it out. A replacement file with a different length needs
new loop points.

Web copies: `ffmpeg -i <master> -map 0:a -c:a copy -map_metadata -1 -id3v2_version 0 <web copy>` (all twelve are under
4 MB, so they are not re-encoded; Gearing Up is the largest at 3.9 MB). `apps/web/src/game/ost.test.ts` checks that every
track has its file, that the loop points lie inside it, and that it carries no tags.
