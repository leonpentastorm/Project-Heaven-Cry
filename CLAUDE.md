# Project Heaven Cry (PHC) — working brief for Claude Code

PHC began in 2020 as a Vietnamese tabletop RPG by Leon ("Romantic B0ss") and friends. It adapts Disco Elysium's skill web (27 skills that are also inner voices) to tactical squad combat. In Nov 2026 it was revived, translated to English, rebalanced and turned into a game-ready rules spec and data pack. **The goal now is a web game**: play immersively in the browser with an AI Narrator (bring-your-own API key, any provider), or with a human Narrator, solo or co-op with up to 4 players in a room.

This file is the hand-off from the design sessions in claude.ai. Read it first, then `docs/rules/PHC-Core-Rules-v1.0.md`.

## The game in one screen

- **Character tree:** 3 Cores (Body, Mind, Soul) → 9 Attributes → 27 Skills (α combat, β utility/defence, γ dialogue/exploration per Attribute). Caps: Skill ≤ Attribute ≤ Core ≤ 10.
- **Rolls:** d20 + skill modifier vs TN 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 (Easy / Standard / Hard / Heroic). Modifier = −2 at 0 points, else **points − 1** (Mastered = +9). Nat 20 critical success, nat 1 complication.
- **Mastery:** at 3/5/7/9 points (M1–M4), Mastered at 10. Each of M1–M4 gives one **perk step** in that skill (one of its two perks at Tier I, or a Tier I upgraded to Tier II, in the order you choose); Mastered grants the skill's own **Mastered perk** (27, one per skill).
- **Voices:** every skill with 1+ point is a private inner voice with its own card (skills.json `voice`, toned after its EPCCG speaker). The Narrator attaches hidden voice hooks (skill, TN, truth flag) to each story beat; the engine passes them on passive 10 + mod; up to 2 lines per player per beat; voices can mislead. Ask a skill once per scene.
- **Overreach** (Mastery 2+): the skill pushes; **indulge** (consequence, +1 Echo) or **resist** (1 Echo, 2 if Mastered). No roll; once per scene. **Echo:** 1 at session start, max 3, reroll a d20 (1 per roll).
- **Creation:** 10 Skill points, 10 Attribute points and 6 Core points (the 2020 budgets; no creation caps beyond the tree's own, so 7/1/1 is legal; an unspent Core point trades for 2 Attribute points); placing a skill spends the Attribute/Core points it needs automatically; 0–2 traits. After creation, Attribute/Core points come from levelling. Class is **earned** at level 3 (250 XP, the end of session 1), not chosen. XP table paces level 20 at about session 51 (5e-like).
- **HP:** 100 + 15 × (Endurance − 1) + (level − 1) × (5 + 2 × Durability).
- **Classes (Class Roster Revision 3, 8 Nov 2026):** 9 base classes, one per attribute (Body: Defender, Fighter, Marksman; Mind: Infiltrator, Mage, Trickster; Soul: Bard, Channeler, Guardian). At level 3 you get the class of your highest attribute (a tie: you choose among the tied; all tied: any of the nine), and it is **fixed** (changing class is a later system). Rank II at that attribute 5, rank III at 8; two moves a rank. Reused moves keep their old keys (`class:medic.triage` on the Bard). The 21 advanced classes are **shelved** (not learnable; a held one gives nothing) until they return as attribute pairs (squared + cross-core hybrids, in batches); shelved moves are listed in `docs/design/move-pool.md`. Body classes run on ammo/weapons/tech; Mind classes burn mana cores to make mana (Sigils, rules 1.4: words drawn as glyphs on your own tile, walked into a straight Line of 2–4 and Evoked); Soul classes spend mana and crystallise cores (Outbursts filled in the heart over turns and released; rules 1.5). Any class with a patron and Faith can pray.
- **Combat:** tactical grid, 1.5 m tiles, 6 AP per turn (ceiling 9, floor 2; perks add at most +3), one reaction per round, initiative by Agility. **Movement is tiles per AP:** 1 + 0.5 × (Agility − 1), so at Agility 1 every tile costs 1 AP. **World Play 1.1 (rules 1.9):** what lies on a tile is one pile (drawn on every map, picked up for 1 AP with a checklist; it persists); reloads are speed (the magazine drops) or tactical (+1 AP, it goes in the pack); gear changes in a fight at 1 AP a hand from the backpack (drop free; light armour only); enemies drop their weapon, ammo and rolled loot (`data/loot_tables.json`, engine-rolled).
- **Mana:** ambient meter 0–40 (Thin / Normal / Rich / Flooded). Deaths raise it. Sigil magic (rules 1.4: Evoker + cores; 40 English words in five groups, Subjects (FIRE, MEND…), Reach (SEEK, ARC…), Delivery, Burst and Filter; each glyph is drawn on your own tile, one to a tile, and a spell is a straight Line of 2–4 glyphs (horizontal, vertical or diagonal; at least a Subject and one other word); Evoke from within 1 tile of it, and the facing rule aims it: away from you, along the Line from an end, across it from the side; excess becomes Flux and is vented). Soul magic (rules 1.5, Outburst 2.0: Emanator + MP; a design is a school + modules + a tone (Raw, or the six Sigil elements) in one shape (Bolt, Wave, Burst, Aura, Lance); **Begin** (2 AP) holds it at stage 0, **Pump** (1 AP) +1 stage to 4, **Release** (1 AP) at ×0.6 / 1 / 1.4 / 1.8 / 2.2; held past a turn it swells a stage; Strain over the limit burns 8 a point, 3 over is an automatic Mana Short; no vent: Strain falls each turn and by calming the heart (stillness, fighting, tending, Let go, Steady); Crystallise makes cores; **rules 1.8, Outburst 4.0:** stage 0 ×0.8, each Pump plain or with one of 25 **feelings** (its own step and behaviour; unlocked the first time it happens to you; residue on the tiles; Revert Pump), modules gated by Soul skills (never Visualization) and learned by practice, the first tone at Lore Master 1). Blessings (rules 1.6, Blessing 2.0; Faith 2.1, rules 1.6.1–1.6.2): every character is Faithful (one of 8 patrons, Devotion 0–10 as standing, earned as **Devotion XP** (curve 45/102/200/450 XP to 3/5/7/10): Hymn I/II/III at 3/5/7, the **Miracle at Faith 7 + Devotion 10**, once a session, and it lowers Devotion by 5 levels) or Atheist (prays by dice; knowledge pieces unlock other patrons' Hymns); Whisper 1 AP / Hymn 3 AP / Miracle 3 AP; overreach above your standing; the patron sees tagged actions (a liked tag +1 XP, each tag once a fight and once a scene, capped 2 a fight / 2 a scene / 6 a session; a taboo −3), vows (+3), rites (+4), Narrator deeds (+2 to +5); leaving a patron is Devotion 0 and 90 days (on the day counter: a long rest is a day) before any pact, switching to another has no cooldown but the old patron may hold a grudge; Witnessed (Faith 5; rules 1.6.3: from a Hymn or Miracle until the fight ends or the patron intervenes) and interventions (each ends Witnessed; once a fight, twice from Devotion 7; 20 + 4 × Devotion; a Miracle plays as a full-screen cut-in, `apps/web/src/game/cutIn.ts`); voice lines from data (faith.ts, patrons.json v2).

## Lore essentials (canon decided with Leon)

- The world is a **simulation**. NPCs are its people. **Nanobots are mana.** Players are a layer above even real Earth. (In docs, *real Earth* is the layer above; *Earth* alone is the in-world homeworld where Central City stands.)
- **Celestials** are trapped Earth humans acting as gods, bound by treaties (the Celestial Accords). Creation ladder: players → real Earth → Celestials → NPCs → robots (a robot is an NPC made by NPCs). **The ladder** (canon, 9 Nov 2026): every layer was made by the one above it and then abandoned by it (real Earth cut off the Celestials, the Celestials treat NPCs as scenery, the NPCs told their robots to shut themselves down on M813). Everyone is crying up at a heaven that doesn't answer: the title's double meaning.
- **The Knowing:** an NPC who learns the truth is killed or made a demigod.
- **Evoker = Sigil magic. Emanator = Soul magic.** Locked; never swap them.
- Kills spawn bots; Sigil users vent Flux, Soul users crystallise cores.
- **Scale:** billions of years; several sextillion humans on trillions of worlds and factions. Each pocket of humanity is a **Sector** with its own powers, peoples and Celestial messes; everything in the EPCCG (Crawlers included) is the **Earth Sector**'s story (name Suggested). Anti-Heaven Operations Command is the only constant. Characters pick a Sector, then an **origin** (an upbringing with no numbers; 14 for the Earth Sector), then race once races exist. Then an **archetype**: one of 46 presets in 9 families, including the All-in set of 7/1/1 builds (data/archetypes.json) that spends every creation point (Skill, Attribute and Core). Guidance only: it's not saved and does nothing after that screen.
- **The Unspoken Name.** Celestials can LARP anything from real Earth, and players can import any character as a Celestial's LARP, but no real-Earth name is ever spoken in the world: references are plain to the players, never to the characters. Official shipped content uses archetypes, not recognisable recreations.
- **Crawlers** are NPC version 2: humans with esper abilities, cursed by a Celestial to look to ordinary humans like whatever they fear most; true love lifts the curse. **Operation Daggerfall** (~3800) ended with their main world nuked from orbit. People live ~400 years. Years (4084 etc.) are on the Central City founding calendar; the world's history runs back billions of years. Crawlers see ordinary humans as humans: the curse runs one way. The Thousand-Planet City's army is the Thousand World Legion. Bazaim is a human nation of lifted curses. **Anti-Heaven Operations Command** was founded ~5,000 years before Central City by the Progenitors (a thousand isekai-powered humans who learned the truth; hunted to extinction); their semi-powered descendants (who suspect the truth but can't act) now run it, in decay: from overthrowing Heaven to damage control. It's everywhere, like a containment foundation, and turns up in every campaign through its local chapters. Players aren't members by default, but creation always uses its document, the EPCCG. **Celestials** are friends who don't fight each other; the Accords say hands off, create but never erase, and appear only by cause (summoning, miracle, pact); an uncaused appearance is a catastrophe. **The Veil exception:** an NPC who proves the truth may be erased (why the Progenitors died; suspicion isn't proof). **Heroes are patches:** a Celestial can't erase its mistake, only create a counter to it. Celestials have politics: no fighting, plenty of lobbying, cliques and grudges.
- **Tone:** NPCs are funny the way people are; Celestials are funny the way people are when nothing can hurt them. The tragedy belongs to the NPCs.
- In-universe language only. Some traits are deliberately ugly (the racism trait is kept in clean in-world language, plus a xenophobe trait, Humanity First). That's intentional design, not something to sand off.

## Two referees: engine vs Narrator

The **engine owns numbers**: rolls, AP, damage, caps, resources, conditions, the grid. The **Narrator** (AI or human) owns judgement: TNs, scene, NPCs, consequences, whether a trigger happened, Achieved perks, legendary deeds. Every rule-bearing data item has `adjudication: engine | hybrid | narrator`. Effects use ops from `data/effect_ops.json`: 50 **core** ops the engine implements generically, 82 **bespoke** ops (~10% of uses) that are per-item hooks. **An op the engine doesn't recognise falls back to the Narrator with the item's `text`; never silently ignore it.** The AI Narrator never sets numbers the engine owns; it requests checks and the engine resolves them.

## Product decisions so far

- Web game; tactical grid combat; story mode with narrative stream.
- AI Narrator: **bring your own key, any provider** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, local OpenAI-compatible). Key stays in the browser and goes only to the provider. Built: `narrator/anthropic.ts` (Anthropic, and Anthropic-format sites by address) and `narrator/providers.ts` (the provider list, and one OpenAI-compatible adapter for OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Gemini, local and third-party sites); decision 104.
- Human Narrator mode ("I'll narrate") with a private Narrator screen; the engine still handles dice, grid and numbers.
- Co-op up to **4 players per room**; solo works too.
- **Speaking order:** in story mode each player speaks once per round, then the Narrator answers everyone together. Players can request a turn; the host can skip an away player.
- **Characters are locked in before a session starts** (or saved as drafts and brought into any room).
- Stack (built, decision 62): TypeScript monorepo; React + Vite client rendering the prototype templates; Cloudflare Worker + Durable Objects; host-authoritative rooms. **Live: https://project-heaven-cry.leonpentastorm.workers.dev**
- Run locally: `npm install`, `npm run assets` (web images from art/), `python3 design/canvas-build/build.py --web` (screen data), `npm run build`, then `npm run dev -w @phc/server` (http://127.0.0.1:8787). Tests: `npm test -w @phc/engine`, `npm test -w @phc/web`, `node apps/web/e2e/two-players.mjs`.
- Deploy: `npm run build`, then `python3 tools/deploy_site.py` (site files to KV) and `npx wrangler deploy` in apps/server (needs a Cloudflare token with Workers and KV edit rights).

## UI direction (see `docs/design/ui-screens.md` and the canvas)

- Inspired by AI-DM web RPGs (character panel | narrative stream | dice) and by **Disco Elysium's** skill screens: inner-voice cards in the story, a portrait card wall for skills, big `‹ 3 ›` steppers. Original art and text only; no DE assets.
- Colour = meaning everywhere. Full rules in `docs/design/colour-system.md`; values in `art/style-guide/palette.json`.
  - **Skills take their attribute's colour (9 hues).** Art uses the pigment; UI uses the contrast-safe ink: Endurance `#F65157`, Strength `#F68A38`, Agility `#E3BF51`, Perception `#40BAA0`, Intelligence `#8BDEEE`, Expression `#5394AC`, Insight `#EB95B7`, Determination `#9D7ADC`, Virtue `#D36A90`.
  - **Cores** keep family colours for core-level UI: Body amber `#F2A54A`, Mind cyan `#4FC8DF`, Soul violet `#AE9BFF`.
  - **States:** success `#6FEA77`, failure muted `#A3AEC2`, HP/damage/complication `#F65157` (HP comes from Endurance), MP `#5C9EFF`, Echo and still-to-do white `#FFFFFF`. Always pair with a word or glyph.
  - Neutrals: ground `#0A0D13`, panels `#10151F`, raised `#1C2536`, lines `#232D3F`, text `#E7EBF3`, muted `#A3AEC2`.
- Type: **Spectral** for narrative prose and titles, **Chakra Petch** for UI and numbers.
- Art has three modes (one style guide: `art/style-guide/PHC-Style-Guide.md`; layout: `art/README.md`): **skill portraits** are 27 faceless gesture figures at 4:5 (800 × 1000), black + cream + the attribute pigment, in `art/icons/skills/`; **character portraits** are Heavenprint paintings in `art/portraits/` (`epccg/` = the First-Patch Revival EPCCG cast; `world/` = Original Heavenprint and New Faces, a pool of world characters for NPCs; `archetypes/` = the 46 player cards; `m813/` = MPAT, the comrade and the M813 cast; `patrons/` = the eight gods; none of them are skill icons); **world illustrations** are in `art/illustrations/m813/` (ending-only images under `spoilers/`), maps in `art/maps/`, object sprites in `art/sprites/`; **trait, perk and prayer icons** are Wick, pure white flat ink, in `art/icons/` (master: `art/icons/traits/mind-without-fog.png`); flat ink is for those icons only. **Weapon icons** (rule, Leon, 10 Nov 2026) are their own mode: monoline like a professional line-icon set (64 grid, 2-unit round stroke in currentColor, one silhouette outline plus a few interior lines, readable at 32 px), and every weapon looks cool and edgy through its silhouette and a faction signature; invented, never real. Rule and reference icons: `docs/design/weapon-art.md`. The **Narrator's avatar** is Wick fully 3D and rendered (brief: `art/briefs/wick-narrator-3d.md`). The **AHOC seal** (Leon's 2020 emblem) is in `art/emblems/ahoc/`, white ink in the UI. Wick's own mark (the welcome pose, the app icon) and menu clips are in `art/mascot/`. Asset IDs resolve through `art/catalog.json`; superseded art is in `art/_archive/`.
- Dice are transparent: the screen always shows TN and the sum, so players can see the AI isn't fudging.

## Working agreements with Leon

- **Branches:** work happens on `develop`; `main` only receives merges from it.
- He wants **direct pushback**, **numbered feedback** and **full drafts**, not hedged options.
- Change the spec first, then the data, then run the validator.
- Never invent a number silently. Provisional numbers are flagged (`provisional: true`) and listed in spec section 14.
- Keep text in-universe: no real-Earth names in game text (the Unspoken Name). Recognisable homages through Celestials are fine.
- Rules revision 1.1 (accepted 5 Nov 2026) is in both the repo spec and the living Doc. Its provisional numbers are spec items 35–42.
- **Versioning** (Leon, 9 Nov 2026; current version in `package.json`, history in `CHANGELOG.md`): **0.MINOR.PATCH** until the 1.0 release. Every branch merged into `develop` bumps the middle number; the first number becomes 1 at the 1.0 release. *Reading, for Leon to confirm:* a hotfix committed straight to `develop` without a branch bumps the last number. **Whoever merges a branch** bumps `"version"` in the root and every workspace `package.json` (all four stay equal) and adds a `CHANGELOG.md` entry in the same merge. The title screen and the Report a problem bundle show it.
- **Sound and VFX assets** (Leon, 9 Nov 2026): go and find openly licensed assets: **CC BY 4.0** (or CC0) sound effects, music and VFX sprites/particle sheets. Credit every one in the game's credits (`apps/web/src/game/sfxCredits.ts` / `credits.ts`) exactly as the author asks, with source URL and licence. Never NC, ND or "personal use" licences. Only make an effect yourself when it looks good as pure SVG or CSS (rings, lines, glyph strokes, tints); anything that needs texture (fire, smoke, sparks, magic bursts, impacts) comes from a sourced asset.
- **New collaborators** (human or AI) read this file, then `docs/HANDOFF.md` (how to pick the project up) and `docs/ROADMAP.md` (what's next).
