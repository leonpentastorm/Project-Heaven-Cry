CHAPTER ONE

The Outpost at the Edge

Outpost-813 was built to watch the dark. It sits on the rim of a black hole the old charts call the Throat, close enough that time runs thick at the windows and every clock on the station disagrees.

Six days ago the infirmary stopped reporting. Three days ago the relief shuttle docked, and nobody got off. You were sent to find out why.
